# 6 nimmt! 30 Years Anniversary Edition Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

Classic simultaneous-play card game: each round you hold **10** number cards and try to avoid collecting **bullheads** (negative points). Cards go into **four rows** in ascending order; play the **sixth** card in a row and you must take the first five. Fewest points when someone reaches **66+** wins.

This **30th Anniversary Edition** adds:

- **4 fan special-card variants** (use one at a time)
- Cooperative / solo mode **Beat the Buffalo**
- Optional **Pro** draft variant (2–6 players)
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In 6 nimmt! 30th Anniversary, you choose number cards at the same time as everyone else and try not to collect bull heads. Your goal is to finish with the lowest penalty total.

| Item | Count / notes |

<!-- rule-section: base-game-2-10-players -->
## Base Game (2–10 players)

![24 joins the 12 — smallest difference](/images/rules/6-nimmt-30th-anniversary/play-to-rows.svg)

![The 6th card takes five; yours starts a new row](/images/rules/6-nimmt-30th-anniversary/take-sixth.svg)
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
### Setup

1. Shuffle all number cards; deal **10** to each player.
2. Place **4** of the remaining cards face up in a column — each starts a row (max **5** cards per row, including the starter).
3. Set aside unused cards until the next round.
<!-- rule-section: playing-a-round -->
### Playing a round

1. **Simultaneously** choose 1 hand card face down, then reveal together.
2. Place cards from **lowest to highest**. Each card must follow:

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **1. Ascending** | Only after a lower last card in that row |
| **2. Lowest difference** | Must join the row where (your card − last card) is smallest |
| **3. Full row** | 6th card → take the 5 cards, your card starts the new row |
| **4. Too low** | Fits nowhere → take **any** row of your choice; your card starts that row |

Taken cards go to your **bull pile** (never back to hand).
<!-- rule-section: bullheads-negative-points -->
### Bullheads (negative points)

| Card type | Bullheads |
|-----------|-----------|
| Normal | 1 |
| Ends in 5 (5, 15, 25…) | 2 |
| Multiple of 10 | 3 |
| Double (11, 22, 33…) | 5 |
| **55** | **7** |
<!-- rule-section: end-of-round-game -->
### End of round / game

After all 10 cards are played, count bullheads in your bull pile and record them. Start a new round. Continue until a player has **≥ 66** points. **Lowest total wins** (ties share). You may agree on another target or a fixed number of rounds.
<!-- rule-section: pro-variant-2-6 -->
### Pro variant (2–6)

Use only `players × 10 + 4` cards (values 1 through that count). Draft hands face up until each has 10; the remaining 4 start the rows. Then play as usual.

## Fan Special Cards (add one variant at a time)

![Add only one fan variant at a time](/images/rules/6-nimmt-30th-anniversary/fan-specials.svg)
<!-- rule-section: even-odd -->
### Even/Odd

Place the card left of the **lowest** starter; show even/odd to match that number. That row only accepts even **or** odd cards (as shown). Whenever anyone takes a row, move the marker to the row whose **last** card is currently lowest and flip even/odd to match.
<!-- rule-section: mountain-climbing -->
### Mountain Climbing

Starts on the 4th row (ascending arrows). That marked row is played in **descending** order; on equal difference, prefer the marked row. After any take, move the marker one row in the arrow direction; reverse at the end of the column.
<!-- rule-section: the-jumping-cow -->
### The Jumping Cow

Sits in a row and has **no value**; use the card to its left as reference. Placing into its row makes it jump to the row with the current lowest last card. The Cow **counts** toward the 5-card limit (4 numbers + Cow = full). Taking its row: take numbers only; Cow jumps again (may force another take).
<!-- rule-section: flippin-digits -->
### Flippin’ Digits

Each player gets one with their 10 cards. Once per round, play it with a two-digit card to **swap digits** for placement order only (e.g. 24 → 42). Not for 100–104. After placement the card keeps its **original** value. Used Flippin’ Digits leaves the game.

## Beat the Buffalo (cooperative, 1–6)

Team vs the buffalo. Base placement rules apply with these changes:
<!-- rule-section: section-16 -->
### Setup

- Deal 10 facedown cards to each player **and** 10 to the buffalo (blue side of the sheet — do not look).
- 4 row starters as usual.
- Special-card deck size by player count: **1→0, 2→2, 3→4, 4→6, 5→11, 6→16**. Reveal **2** face up.
<!-- rule-section: play -->
### Play

Reveal player cards and the buffalo’s top card together; place all by the usual lowest-to-highest order. Team takes go to a **shared** bull pile; buffalo takes go to its yellow pile. If the buffalo’s card is “too low,” it takes the row with fewest bullheads (tie → highest last card).

Use face-up specials anytime before/during placement; replace from the deck immediately. Discuss after reveal; **hands stay secret** (no talking about unplayed numbers).

| Special | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Take 7!** | Chosen row needs a **7th** card to be taken (take all 6) |
| **Stop!** | Lock a row until you remove the card |
| **Replace!** | A teammate swaps their played card for another from hand (not buffalo) |
| **Insert!** | Place a teammate’s card inside a row (still ascending) |
| **Push!** | Move an already-placed card to another legal spot |
| **First / Last Card** | Choose who places first / last (not buffalo) |
| **Sort** | Decide full placement order for this reveal |
<!-- rule-section: winning -->
### Winning

One round: if the team’s bullheads **<** buffalo’s, you win. With **1–2 players**, **double** the team’s points before comparing. Solo uses **0** special cards.
