# 7 Wonders (Second Edition) Rules

## Overview

**7 Wonders** (Antoine Bauza, Repos Production, **Second Edition 2020**) is a simultaneous card-drafting civilization game for **3–7** players. Over **three Ages**, you build a city and a Wonder. After Age III, the highest victory-point total wins.

This edition updates art, symbols (including colorblind icons), some cards and Wonder costs/effects, and **drops the 2-player variant** — use *7 Wonders Duel* for two. First-edition expansions are **not** compatible (different card backs).

## Components

- **7 Wonder boards** (Day / Night sides)
- **148 Age cards:** 49 Age I, 49 Age II, 50 Age III
- **78 coins:** 54×1 and 24×3
- **48 military tokens:** defeats (−1) and victories (+1 / +3 / +5 by Age)
- Score pad, 3 Description of Effects sheets, 1 card-and-chains sheet, rulebook

## Setup

1. Each player takes a **Wonder board** (random or chosen) and picks **Day** or **Night**. First games: use **Day**.
2. Each player takes **3 coins** (value 1) as starting Treasure. Remaining coins form the reserve.
3. Military tokens go to a central reserve.
4. Prepare Age decks:
   - Split cards into Ages I, II, III.
   - In each Age, keep only cards whose **minimum player number ≤ actual players**. Return the rest.
   - From Age III **purple guilds**, draw **players + 2** at random and add them; return unused guilds.
   - Shuffle each Age deck.

**Example (5 players):** keep 3+, 4+, and 5+ cards; draw **7** guilds for Age III.

## How to Play

Three Ages in order. Points are counted only after Age III.

**Neighbors** = left and right players. **City** = your Wonder plus all constructed cards.

### An Age

Deal **7** cards to each player. Each Age has **6** simultaneous turns:

1. **Choose** 1 card secretly; set the rest aside facedown.
2. **Play** that card (everyone reveals together).
3. **Pass** the unused cards: **left** in Ages I and III, **right** in Age II (arrow on the card back).

Hands shrink each turn. On the **sixth** turn you receive **2** cards: play 1 and **discard** the other with **no coins**. Then resolve military.

### Play actions (pick one)

**A. Construct the building** — pay its cost and place it face up above your board. **No two cards in your city may share a name.** Stack same-color cards so effects stay visible; stack resources above your starting resource.

**B. Construct a Wonder stage** — pay the **stage** cost on your board (not the card’s cost). Slide the card facedown under that stage. Stages must be built **left to right**. Optional; any Age; any card may be used as the marker.

**C. Sell** — discard facedown to the central pile and take **3 coins** from the reserve.

## Construction costs

Resources are **not spent**; each symbol may be used **once per turn**.

| Cost | Rule |
|------|------|
| Empty | Free |
| Coins | Pay that many from Treasure to the reserve |
| Resources | Use your city and/or **buy** from neighbors |
| Chain | If you already have the matching chain symbol (upper right of an earlier card), build **free** (Age II–III). You may still pay the resource cost instead |

**Your production:** Wonder starting resource, brown, grey, yellow, and some Wonder stages — **one use per symbol per turn**.

**Buying from neighbors (2 coins each):**

- Allowed: their **starting resource**, **brown**, and **grey**
- **Not** allowed: their **yellow** production or **Wonder-stage** production
- Pay at the **start** of the turn from coins you already have; coins gained this turn cannot pay this turn’s purchases
- Cannot buy a resource from a building they built **this same turn**
- You cannot refuse a sale; both neighbors may buy the **same** symbol
- On a “1 of 2 resources” card, the buyer chooses which one, independent of the owner

## Military conflicts

At the end of each Age, compare **shields** (red cards + Wonder) with each neighbor:

- Equal → nothing
- Lower → take a **Defeat (−1)**
- Higher → take the **Victory** for this Age: Age I **+1**, Age II **+3**, Age III **+5**

Then start the next Age (or end the game after Age III).

## Science

Green symbols (and some Wonder stages) score **both**:

- **Identical:** 1 / 4 / 9 / 16 / 25 / 36 / 49 for 1–7 copies of one type
- **Sets:** **+7** for each complete set of **three different** types

Add the two totals.

## End of the game

After Age III military, score with the pad. Highest wins. Tie → most **Treasure**. Still tied → shared win.

| Category | How to score |
|----------|----------------|
| Wonder | VP printed on built stages |
| Treasure | **1 VP per complete set of 3 coins** (remainder 0) |
| Military | Sum of all conflict tokens (may be negative) |
| Blue | Printed VP |
| Yellow | Card effects (see below) |
| Green | Science (above) |
| Purple | Guild effects (usually neighbors’ cards) |

## Common effects

See the in-box Description of Effects sheets. Frequent symbols:

- **Trade posts:** brown resources from the pointed neighbor cost **1** instead of 2
- **Marketplace:** grey resources from **both** neighbors cost **1**
- **Coins now / VP later:** take coins for cards of a color (yours, or yours + neighbors, including same-turn builds); many also score VP for that color at game end
- **Haven / Chamber of Commerce / Ludus / Arena-type:** coins now + VP at end for matching cards in **your** city
- **Lighthouse-type:** 3 coins per Wonder stage now + 1 VP per stage at end
- **Halicarnassus:** at end of the turn this stage is built, take the discard, build 1 card **free**
- **Babylon (play 7th):** you may play the card normally discarded at the end of each Age (extra turn after everyone else)
- **Olympia Day:** first Age card of **each color** in your city is free (tracked for the whole game)
- **Olympia Night:** first card of each Age free; last card of each Age free (plus printed VP)
- **Wild science:** choose a symbol at **game end**
- **Decorators Guild:** **7 VP** only if **all** Wonder stages are built
- **Shipowners Guild:** 1 VP per brown + grey + purple in **your** city (including itself)
- **Builders Guild:** 1 VP per Wonder stage in **your and both neighbors’** cities

## Wonders (Second Edition)

Each board has a starting resource and Day/Night stages. **Costs are on the board** (several were rebalanced vs 2010). Signature ideas:

| Wonder | Starting resource | Typical focus |
|--------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Alexandria** (Lighthouse) | Glass | Extra resource production |
| **Babylon** (Hanging Gardens) | **Wood** | Wild science (Day) or play the 7th card (Night, **2 stages**) |
| **Ephesus** (Artemis) | Papyrus | Coins and VP |
| **Giza** (Pyramids) | Stone | High VP (Night has **4 stages**) |
| **Halicarnassus** (Mausoleum) | Cloth | Build from the discard |
| **Olympia** (Zeus) | **Clay** | Free construction (color / first-or-last of Age) |
| **Rhodes** (Colossus) | Ore | Shields and VP (often **2 stages** on one side) |

Olympia and Babylon differ the most from the first edition; copy-a-neighbor’s-guild was removed from Olympia Night.

## Second Edition notes

- Players **3–7** only
- Chains use **icons**, not building names
- Guild mix: **Decorators** replaces **Strategists**; Age III military includes **Castrum**; some civilian chains skip a middle building (e.g. Altar → Pantheon)
- Wonder “cards” for random assignment were removed — deal boards directly
