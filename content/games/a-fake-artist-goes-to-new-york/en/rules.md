# A Fake Artist Goes to New York Rules

## Learn It in 30 Seconds

One player is the **Question Master** (QM), so everyone else is an artist. One artist secretly receives an **X** and is the **Fake Artist**; every other artist receives the same secret title. Everyone adds two marks to one shared drawing, then votes together to expose the fake. Real artists must show that they know the title without making it easy for the fake to work it out.

## Game Objective

Be the first player to reach **5 points**.

- Real Artists score only when they expose the Fake Artist and the fake cannot name the title.
- The Fake Artist and QM score when the fake escapes the vote, or is caught but names the title correctly.

## Roles and Components

| Role | What they know | What they do this round |
|---|---|---|
| Question Master (QM) | Category, secret title, and X card | Prepares cards, chooses the first artist, and runs the vote; **does not draw** |
| Real Artist | Category and secret title | Draws a subtle clue and identifies the player without the title |
| Fake Artist | Category only | Pretends to know the title, reads the drawing, and avoids suspicion |

Use title cards, coloured pens, drawing paper, and point chips. Ideally each artist takes a different pen colour, so everyone can retrace who drew each mark during the vote.

## Set Up a Round

1. Choose one player as QM; everyone else is an artist.
2. QM chooses and announces a **category**, then chooses an easy-to-recognise **title** from it.
3. QM makes one title card for each artist: write **X** on one card and the same title on every other card. Shuffle and deal them face down.
4. Each artist secretly reads their card; do not show it or read it aloud. The X holder is the Fake Artist. QM remembers the X card's colour or holder.
5. QM chooses the first artist and puts out a fresh sheet of paper.

> The category is public; the title is known only to Real Artists. A title that is too obscure gives the fake no fair way to deduce it, while a title that is too obvious gives the game away too quickly.

## Play a Round

### 1. Draw Exactly One Mark

Starting with the artist chosen by QM, take turns clockwise. On your turn, add **one continuous line** to the same sheet, then immediately pass it on.

- One mark is one unbroken line from pen-down to pen-up. You may not lift the pen and add a second piece.
- Every artist draws **two marks** total: once around the table, then once around again.
- Do not show a title card or say the title aloud. Leave a useful clue rather than writing the answer.

![Each artist draws two single marks to build the shared artwork while hiding the title](/images/rules/a-fake-artist-goes-to-new-york/drawing-round.svg)

### 2. Point Together

After the second circuit, QM gives everyone a brief look at the drawing, then counts: “3, 2, 1, point!” Every artist—including the fake—simultaneously points at one suspect.

- If the Fake Artist is **not the sole highest-voted** player (including a tie, or a Real Artist receiving the most votes), the fake escapes.
- If the Fake Artist is the **sole highest-voted** player, they reveal the X card and get one chance to name the exact title.

![The fake must be the sole highest-voted player to be caught, then gets one title guess](/images/rules/a-fake-artist-goes-to-new-york/vote-and-score.svg)

### 3. Guess and Score

A caught Fake Artist gets **one guess only**. Score immediately from this table:

| Vote and guess result | Points |
|---|---|
| Fake is not the sole highest-voted player, including a tie | Fake Artist and QM: **2** each |
| Fake is sole highest-voted and names the title correctly | Fake Artist and QM: **2** each |
| Fake is sole highest-voted but names the title incorrectly | Every Real Artist: **1** each |

An escaped fake may still share a guess while revealing the answer, but receives no extra points for it.

## Start the Next Round

Write the title beside the finished masterpiece and use a new sheet. The player to the left of the previous QM becomes the next QM. Prepare new cards and begin again. As soon as any player reaches **5 points**, they win.

## Table-Side Answers

- **Does the Fake Artist vote?** Yes. The fake points at one artist at the same time as everyone else.
- **Does a tie catch the fake?** No. The Fake Artist must be alone in first place to reveal their identity.
- **What if the fake draws something plausible?** That is the bluff at the heart of the game. The fake can infer from the category and earlier marks; Real Artists must balance signalling each other against revealing the title.
- **Does the quality of the finished picture matter?** No. The drawing is evidence for the vote; only the vote, guess, and score decide the round.
