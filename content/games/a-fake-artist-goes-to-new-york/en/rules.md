# A Fake Artist Goes to New York Rules

## Overview

One **Question Master** secretly gives the same title to every artist except one: the **Fake Artist** receives an X. Everyone adds one line at a time to the shared drawing, trying to signal knowledge without making the title too obvious.

## Game Objective

Be first to **5 points**. Artists win a round by catching a fake who cannot name the title. The Fake Artist and Question Master win if the fake escapes the vote—or is caught but correctly names the title.

## Components

- Title cards, 12 colour pens, white-board marker, point chips, and drawing paper

## Setup

1. Choose the first Question Master (QM); they do not draw. The remaining players are artists.
2. QM announces a category, takes one title card per artist, writes the same title on all but one, and writes **X** on the remaining card.
3. Shuffle and deal one card face down to each artist. Artists secretly read their card; the X holder is the Fake Artist. QM remembers which colour/card is X.

## How to Play

### Draw 1 line → pass clockwise → repeat for 2 circuits

QM chooses the first artist. Each artist takes a colour pen and adds **one continuous line** to the same paper, then play passes clockwise. Go around the table twice. Real artists should make a clue that proves knowledge to other real artists but is not so clear that it reveals the title to the fake.

![One line per artist builds a shared drawing while hiding the title](/images/rules/a-fake-artist-goes-to-new-york/drawing-round.svg)

### Vote together

After the second circuit, QM counts “3, 2, 1, point!” Every artist points simultaneously at the suspected Fake Artist. The fake also points at someone. If the Fake Artist has the most votes, they reveal themselves and get **one** guess at the exact title.

![The fake needs a plurality of votes to be caught; then gets one title guess](/images/rules/a-fake-artist-goes-to-new-york/vote-and-score.svg)

## Scoring

| Result | Points |
|---|---|
| Fake is not uniquely most-voted (including a tie) | Fake Artist + QM: **2** each |
| Fake is most-voted and guesses the title correctly | Fake Artist + QM: **2** each |
| Fake is most-voted and guesses incorrectly | Every real Artist: **1** each |

The fake may guess after escaping, but earns no extra points. The player to the QM's left becomes next round's QM. First player to 5 wins.

## Special Rules

- Everyone hears the category, including the fake; only real artists know the title.
- Do not reveal your title card or say the title aloud during the round.
- Name the finished masterpiece after scoring, then start a new sheet.
