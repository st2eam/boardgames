# Ark Nova: Marine Worlds Rules

## Overview

**Marine Worlds** is an expansion for **Ark Nova** and cannot be played alone. Mix its cards and components into the base game to add marine animals, aquariums, reef effects, a new university, optional asymmetric action cards, and more zoo and end-game cards.

## Game Objective

Your objective remains the same: develop the strongest conservation-focused zoo. Marine Worlds adds new ways to combine cards and meet projects; it does not change the base game's end trigger or final scoring method.

## Components

- New zoo cards, including marine animals and cards with **reef** effects
- Aquarium special enclosures and seven animal figures
- New base conservation projects, end-game cards, bonus tiles, and a university
- Four alternative action-card versions for each of the five actions
- Individual player markers and related reference material

## Setup

1. Set up the Ark Nova base game normally. This expansion uses the same 1–4 player count and play structure.
2. Shuffle the expansion's zoo cards, end-game cards, and base conservation-project cards into their matching base-game decks. Add its eligible bonus tiles to the supply.
3. Put aquarium special enclosures with the other special enclosures. They can house only animals whose card allows an aquarium.
4. Add the expansion university to the university supply; use the expansion's setup material wherever its printed rules direct.
5. Before handing out action cards, decide whether to use the **alternative action cards**. If used, each player takes one complete matching set; do not mix alternative and base versions of the same action in one personal set.

## New Rules

### Aquariums and marine animals

Marine animals are played through the normal **Animals** action: pay their cost and meet every printed requirement. They need an eligible empty enclosure. If a card requires an **aquarium**, use an aquarium special enclosure; it is not a generic replacement for every enclosure. Follow the animal card for size, tags, water/rock adjacency, partner-zoo, reputation, and all other conditions.

![Play a marine animal into a matching aquarium, then resolve its effect](/images/rules/ark-nova-marine-worlds/aquarium-reef.svg)

### Reef effects

Some new cards have a **reef effect**. Resolve it when its condition is met, as directed on the card. Reef effects are designed to reward a connected chain of relevant cards or animals, so resolve a newly triggered effect completely before continuing. If several effects could apply, follow their timing text; card text always has priority over this overview.

### The new university

The new university is available through the normal **Association** action. It gives reliable access to an animal class of your choice: choose the permitted class when taking it, gain that class icon, and take the specified animal card from the deck. Use its printed restrictions and rewards exactly as shown.

### Alternative action cards

Marine Worlds includes four alternatives for each base action card. They make players asymmetric from the start by making small adjustments to their action effects. The action-card strength cycle is unchanged: choose one card, resolve it at its current strength, shift cards, and return the used card to strength 1. The alternative side's wording replaces the corresponding base action-card wording for that player.

![A player uses one consistent alternative action-card set; the strength cycle is unchanged](/images/rules/ark-nova-marine-worlds/alternative-actions.svg)

## How to Play

### Normal turn loop with new choices

**Choose 1 action card → resolve it → shift it to strength 1.** Use the base game rules for Cards, Build, Animals, Association, Sponsors, X-tokens, upgrades, breaks, and game end. The expansion adds cards and components to those existing systems rather than a sixth action or a separate phase.

When an expansion card offers a choice, make it at the timing stated on that card. An aquarium, reef effect, or alternative action card does not waive normal costs and prerequisites unless it explicitly says so.

## Game End and Scoring

Use the base game's end trigger: the game ends once a player's Appeal and Conservation markers meet or cross. Finish the current turn, give all other players one final turn, then score the distance between the markers and all end-game cards. Include expansion end-game cards if you dealt them during setup.

## Special Rules

- **Requires Ark Nova.** Marine Worlds supplies additions, not a standalone game.
- Expansion card text, glossary entries, and current official errata override this summary and the base rules if they conflict.
- All players should use a clear, consistent action-card setup; agree on whether the alternative set is in use before the game begins.
- You may mix the expansion with the base game's normal map choices unless a component says otherwise.
