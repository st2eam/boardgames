# Ark Nova Rules

## Overview

In **Ark Nova**, each player develops a modern, conservation-minded zoo. You build enclosures, add animals, hire sponsors, and use association work to support conservation projects. There are no fixed rounds: take turns until a **break** is triggered, resolve it, and continue.

![Action-card strength and the return-to-1 cycle](/images/rules/ark-nova/action-cycle.svg)

## Game Objective

Build a zoo whose **Appeal** and **Conservation** achievements meet on the shared scoring area. Animals, pavilions, and effects raise Appeal; conservation projects raise Conservation and unlock bonuses. When the markers meet or cross, finish final turns, resolve end-game effects, then score the value beside your Conservation marker plus your Appeal. Highest score wins.

## Setup

1. Put out the main and association boards. Put the break marker on the player-count start space. Shuffle the 9 bonus tiles; put one face-down on each of the four board bonus spaces, add the printed tiles on the yellow spaces, and reveal them. Put the six-card display face-down for now.
2. For a first game, each player uses **map A** and places its printed size-3 standard enclosure empty-side up. Give each player five level-I action cards: **Animals** in strength 1, and the other four randomly in strengths 2-5.
3. Reveal one partner zoo per continent and one university of each type. Reveal four base conservation projects with four players, otherwise three. In a two-player game, use an unused colour to block the indicated project levels and donation spaces.
4. Each player takes a colour, sets Appeal, Conservation, and Reputation to their starts, puts seven markers on the left edge of the map, takes one active worker, 25 money, and three inactive workers. Players after the start player begin with 1 / 2 / 3 Appeal in turn order.
5. Each player secretly takes two end-game cards, draws eight zoo cards, keeps four, and discards four. Reveal the display only after everyone has chosen; choose the first player randomly.

## How to Play

### Turn loop: choose 1 card -> resolve it -> return it to strength 1

Choose one of your five action cards. Its position gives it **strength 1-5**. Before beginning, spend any number of **X-tokens** to increase this action's strength by 1 each; decide before resolving, and never hold more than five X-tokens.

Resolve the action completely. Shift every action card to the used card's left one space right, then put the used card in space 1. Resolve an effect that says **“after your action”** only after this shift. An upgrade flips a card to level II and takes effect from its next use. If you cannot or do not want to take another action, use any action card to gain one X-token instead (only below five), then shift that card normally.

### Cards: move break 2 -> draw, take in range, or snap

First move the break marker **2 spaces**; this never pays money. With level I, draw from the deck according to the card table (up to three cards, then discard when its table says so), or at the required strength **snap** exactly one card from anywhere in the display. Snapping replaces drawing.

With level II, the table also permits cards from your **Reputation range**: your register and every lower-numbered register, mixed with deck draws. Refill only after the whole turn: slide cards down without changing order, then refill. Only upgraded Cards can raise Reputation to 10 or more.

![Teaching illustration: a Reputation-3 player takes an in-range display card, slides cards, then refills](/images/rules/ark-nova/generated-cards-display.png)

### Build: pay -> place connected buildings -> claim bonuses

Pay **2 money per covered space** before placing each building. Level I builds one kiosk, pavilion, standard enclosure, or petting zoo no larger than the action strength. A pavilion immediately gives 1 Appeal; kiosks earn money during breaks.

Buildings cover empty build spaces, stay within the map, and share an edge with an existing building; only your first building may instead touch any map edge. Never build on rock or water; keep at least two spaces between kiosks. Take covered placement bonuses immediately.

Level II may build different buildings whose **total size** is at most the action strength, unlocks restricted spaces, and adds the reptile house and large bird aviary. A zoo may have one of each special enclosure. Building either new size-5 enclosure may immediately move eligible animals into it once. Filling every build space other than rock and water gives 7 Appeal.

![Teaching illustration: connect a new enclosure, claim its bonus, and keep kiosks separated](/images/rules/ark-nova/generated-build-example.png)

![Build an empty enclosure before housing an animal](/images/rules/ark-nova/animal-placement.svg)

### Animals: meet conditions -> pay -> occupy an enclosure -> resolve effects

Play the number of animals permitted by the action table, one at a time. For each, meet every printed prerequisite, pay its cost, and occupy a legal empty enclosure. A partner zoo reduces the cost by **3 money for each matching-continent icon** on that animal. Standard enclosures hold one animal and must be large enough; adjacent rock/water requirements apply. Petting animals need the petting zoo. Reptiles and some birds may use their matching special enclosure and occupy the stated spaces.

Place the card by your zoo, gain Appeal and other immediate rewards, then resolve its ability. “After” effects wait until all animals and the action-card shift finish. Level II at sufficient strength can raise Reputation by 1 before the action, play an in-range display animal for its register number in extra money, and meet the level-II prerequisite; refill only after the turn.

### Association: assign workers -> complete work -> optionally donate

Assign active workers to association work whose printed strength requirements total no more than this action's strength. Before the upgrade, do exactly one work; after it, do several **different** works. Your own worker already on a work makes a repeat cost two workers; other players' workers do not block it.

The works raise Reputation, take a partner zoo, take a university, or support a conservation project. Place a partner zoo/university in the lowest open map slot and take its revealed bonus. A university can raise hand limit from 3 to 5; upgraded Association is needed for a third or fourth partner zoo.

To support a project, choose an unoccupied requirement you meet, place a marker from your map's left edge on it, and take its Conservation / Reputation reward. You may instead play a project from hand and support it immediately; level II also allows an in-range display project for its register number in extra money. Each support removes one left-edge marker, activating that map bonus immediately and, where shown, at breaks.

After upgrading Association, if you completed at least one work, you may make **one** donation: pay the lowest visible cost for 1 Conservation. It needs no worker.

![A worker commits icons to a conservation project](/images/rules/ark-nova/conservation-project.svg)

### Sponsors: play sponsors or advance the break for money

With level I, play exactly one sponsor from hand if strength meets its level and you meet its prerequisites, or move the break marker by action strength and take that much money. With level II, play sponsors one at a time whose total levels are at most **action strength + 1**; you may play in-range display sponsors for their register number in extra money. The break alternative pays **twice** the action strength. Level II also enables level-II prerequisite cards.

## Important Systems

### Reputation, upgrades, and icons

Reputation determines display range. Take a reward beside a Reputation space immediately; at 15 Reputation, every further Reputation becomes 1 Appeal. The four normal upgrade opportunities are on the Conservation and Reputation tracks and when taking your second partner zoo or second university.

For conservation-project requirements, use icons at the tops of played animal/sponsor cards, partner zoos, and universities; card prerequisites are not icons you own. A project that releases an animal discards an eligible animal, loses its Appeal, and frees its enclosure / special-enclosure spaces as directed.

### Break: finish the turn -> resolve these six steps

When the break marker reaches its final space, finish the turn, take one X-token, and lose excess movement. Then:

1. Everyone discards to hand limit: 3 normally, or 5 with the matching university.
2. Return poison and constrict markers from action cards to supply.
3. Return association workers; refill partner zoos and universities.
4. Discard display registers 1 and 2, slide cards down, and refill.
5. Take Appeal income, kiosk income, and activated purple break income. If display-card income conflicts, the break triggerer resolves first, then clockwise, refilling between players.
6. Reset the break marker to the player-count start space; the next player clockwise takes a turn.

## Game End and Scoring

At the end of a turn or during a break, game end triggers when a player's Appeal and Conservation markers occupy the same score band or have crossed. If triggered at turn end, every **other** player gets one final turn. If triggered during a break, **everyone** gets one final turn.

Resolve end-game cards and all played end-game effects. Each end-game card can award at most 4 Conservation. When any player first reaches 10 Conservation, everyone keeps one of their two end-game cards and returns the other; if nobody reaches 10, do this before scoring.

Final score is **Appeal + the small white number beside your Conservation space**. Ties go to the player who supported more conservation projects, then are shared.

## Solo Game

Solo uses the solo tiles instead of the break marker. Start at 20 Appeal (or 10 / 0 for harder games), move one solo marker after every turn, and take a break when the last marker in the left column moves. After six rounds, score normally: 100 or more wins. Blue solo effects replace interactive animal effects where printed.

## Rules Priority

Card text, the glossary, and the symbol reference handle exceptions and override this play aid.
