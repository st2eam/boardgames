# Ark Nova Rules

## Overview

In **Ark Nova**, each player plans and runs a modern, science-led zoo. Build enclosures, welcome animals, recruit experts and sponsors, and contribute to conservation projects. The game has no fixed rounds: players take turns until a break is triggered, then the zoo engine pays out before play continues.

![Action-card strength and the return-to-1 cycle](/images/rules/ark-nova/action-cycle.svg)

## Game Objective

Build the most successful zoo. Animals and buildings raise **Appeal**; conservation work raises **Conservation**. When the two markers meet or pass, finish the current round of turns and score the gap between them, plus end-game cards. Highest total wins.

## Components

- Main board, association board, break marker, appeal / conservation / reputation tracks
- Zoo cards: animals, sponsors, and conservation projects; base conservation-project cards and end-game cards
- Player boards, five action cards per player, cubes, association workers, money, and X-tokens
- Standard and special enclosures, kiosks, pavilions, unique buildings, partner zoos, universities, and bonus tiles

## Setup

1. Put the main board and association board in reach. Set up the break track for the player count, draw the visible association projects, partner zoos, and universities, and prepare the card row.
2. Give every player a zoo board, colour, five **level-I** action cards, one active association worker, money, and starting markers. The **Animals** card begins in strength 1; distribute the other four action cards randomly among strengths 2–5.
3. Put every player's appeal, conservation, and reputation markers at their starting spaces. Later players receive the listed appeal compensation.
4. Each player takes two secret end-game cards, draws eight zoo cards, keeps four, and discards the rest. Reveal the card row only after everyone has chosen.
5. For a first game, use map A and place its printed starting size-3 enclosure. Experienced players may use map O or choose advanced zoo maps instead.

## How to Play

### Turn loop: choose 1 card → resolve it → move it to strength 1

On your turn, choose one of your five action cards. Its current space gives it a **strength from 1 to 5**. Resolve the card at that strength, then slide every card to its left one space right and put the used card in space 1. You may spend any number of **X-tokens** before resolving to increase its strength by 1 each (normally no higher benefit than 5).

You must make a legal, meaningful action. Card effects that say “after your action” wait until the whole action, including the card shift, is complete. Upgrades flip an action card to its stronger level-II side and apply from a later use onward.

### Cards action

Use **Cards** to take zoo cards from the deck and/or the face-up row. At higher strength, take more cards and may take from farther right in the row; taking a displayed card costs reputation according to its slot. Refill emptied row spaces immediately. The level-II card lets you take one more card from the display under its printed condition.

### Build action

Use **Build** to place standard enclosures, special enclosures, kiosks, and pavilions in your zoo. Pay the printed building cost, cover only buildable empty spaces, and claim any placement bonus immediately. Rocks and water are not build sites, while some marked spaces require the upgraded Build card. At strength 5, build two eligible buildings with the action.

![Build an empty enclosure before housing an animal](/images/rules/ark-nova/animal-placement.svg)

### Animals action

Use **Animals** to play animal cards from your hand into an empty, matching enclosure. Pay the animal's money cost, meet its enclosure-size/type, continent, partner-zoo, reputation, and other printed requirements, then resolve its immediate effect. The animal's icons and ongoing ability remain in your zoo. At level II and high strength, the action can play a second animal when the card permits it.

### Association action

Use **Association** to place an active worker on an unoccupied association-board space. Depending on the strength and chosen space, raise reputation, take a university, partner with a zoo, support a conservation project, or donate. Taking a university or partner zoo can provide icons, a reward, and later discounts. Supporting a project spends the required animal/continent icons and advances Conservation; it also removes a bonus marker from your zoo board.

![A worker commits icons to a conservation project](/images/rules/ark-nova/conservation-project.svg)

### Sponsors action

Use **Sponsors** to play sponsor cards, usually by paying their cost and resolving their effect. At high strength, take money from the bank instead; after upgrading the action, playing sponsors becomes more efficient. Sponsor cards can provide specialists, ongoing effects, special buildings, or bonuses tied to your zoo.

## Important Systems

### Zoo cards and requirements

Animal, sponsor, and conservation cards are the core of the game. Resolve a card's costs and prerequisites exactly as printed. A **partner zoo** reduces an animal's cost for matching-continent icons. A university supplies its printed icon(s) and benefit. Unless an effect says otherwise, card icons in your zoo count toward future requirements.

### Conservation bonuses and action upgrades

Whenever you support a conservation project, remove the leftmost available bonus marker from your zoo board and place it on the project. Take the revealed bonus immediately; some bonuses recur at breaks. Conservation and reputation tracks, plus particular association rewards, offer opportunities to upgrade action cards. You cannot normally upgrade every card, so choose the action pattern your zoo needs.

### Breaks

Many effects move the break marker. When it reaches the final space, complete the triggering turn, then take a **break**:

1. Discard down to your hand limit.
2. Resolve income and break effects in player order, including recurring zoo-board bonuses.
3. Return association workers from the board, ready for later use.
4. Reset the break marker for the player count and continue with the next player in turn order.

The player who reaches the last break space also receives an X-token. Do not carry excess break movement past the end.

## Game End and Scoring

The end is triggered as soon as any player's Appeal and Conservation markers meet or cross. Finish the current player's turn, then every other player takes one final turn. Each player calculates the space between their two markers on the shared scoring track, then adds points from both secret end-game cards. The highest result wins; use the official tie-break procedure if tied.

## Special Rules

- You may never hold more than five X-tokens; extras are lost.
- A player cannot choose an association-board space already occupied by another worker.
- Build first: an animal needs an eligible empty enclosure unless its card explicitly supplies an exception.
- Keep end-game cards secret until final scoring. Card text and the glossary override general summaries when they conflict.
