# Bloody Poker Rules

**Bloody Poker** is a **deck-building × poker-hand duel** game for 2–4 players, about 20 minutes per game. Winners take the tickets; losers become the Foundation's credit fodder.

<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

In 2099, on the homebound express. Debtors sit down at the table — winners take the tickets, losers become the Foundation's credit fodder. You **deck-build** with black-market cards while **dueling with poker hands**; the first to reach the target ticket count takes the window seat.

Unlike ordinary poker, a card can be bought again, strengthened, and even played as a seven-of-a-kind. **Your build decides what you draw; your hand decides whether you win this round.**

<!-- rule-section: objective -->
## Game Objective

You are a debtor on the train, winning tickets home by winning hands. The goal is to be the first to **collect the target number of tickets**:

| Players | Target tickets |
|---------|:--------------:|
| 2 | **24** |
| 3 | **20** |
| 4 | **16** |

**Tie-break:** when several players reach the target together, compare **remaining blood chips**; the one with more wins. Still tied, the player **closest to the Temporary Privilege Token** wins.

<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- **54 playing cards** per player (sleeved, shuffled into each player's draw pile)
- **Character cards** (each player draws 2 at setup and keeps 1) with the character arrow
- **Temporary Privilege Token** ×1
- A **black-market deck** and the black-market mat
- **Blood chips** (money) and **ticket counters**
- 1 die

<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

Follow these five steps to start a game.

<!-- rule-ui: sidebar default=setup-black-market -->
1. <!-- rule-item: setup-black-market -->
   **Set up the black market**

   Put the black-market mat in the center, shuffle all black-market cards into a deck (to the leftmost empty slot), and reveal **5 cards** to form the display row.

2. <!-- rule-item: setup-personal -->
   **Personal setup**

   Each player takes 54 playing cards (sleeved, shuffled, face-down in the draw area), sets the ticket counter to 0, and lays out their play area.

3. <!-- rule-item: setup-role -->
   **Choose a character**

   Draw 2 character cards, keep 1, and flip it face-up to trigger its opening privilege. Place the character card in the center of your play area with the **arrow pointing to 3** (the base swap count).

4. <!-- rule-item: setup-start-player -->
   **Determine the start player**

   Roll the die; the highest roll gains the **Temporary Privilege Token** and **+2 blood chips**; the others each take 3 blood chips (blood chips are money). The start player points their character arrow to the **"4/0"** position.

5. <!-- rule-item: setup-initial-build -->
   **Initial build**

   Draw 8 cards, trash up to 4 (remove from the game), and put the rest into the discard pile. Draw 8 more and repeat; your discard pile must hold **at least 8 cards** to finish.

<!-- rule-section: turn -->
## The Eight Phases

Each round proceeds through the eight phases below.

<!-- rule-ui: sidebar default=turn-draw -->
1. <!-- rule-item: turn-draw -->
   **Draw**

   Refill your hand; if the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discard pile.

   - Draw from your **draw pile** until you reach your **hand limit of 6**.
   - If the pile runs out, draw the remainder, then **shuffle the discard pile** into a new draw pile and continue.

   Your hand limit can be changed by abilities.

2. <!-- rule-item: turn-swap -->
   **Swap**

   Swap cards as many times as your character arrow shows; discard old, draw new.

   - Execute the number shown by your **character arrow** (normally 3 times).
   - Each time, discard **up to 3 cards** to your discard pile, then draw back to your hand limit.
   - Each swap, rotate the character card **one step counterclockwise** to track remaining swaps.

   Unused swap count converts to money: each leftover swap = **+1 coin**.

3. <!-- rule-item: turn-play -->
   **Play**

   Set aside 5 cards face-down, then reveal and announce them in order.

   - Choose **5 cards** from your hand, place them **face-down** in the play area, and discard the rest.
   - Once all are revealed, announce starting from the **Temporary Privilege Token holder** clockwise.

   The announcement carries 5 pieces of info: hand type, each card's value, card effects, character privilege, and whether any backup gadget is used.

4. <!-- rule-item: turn-duel -->
   **Duel**

   Compare hand types, then compare total values.

   - Compare **hand types** (see the ranking table below).
   - Same hand type: compare the **sum of the 5 card values**; the higher wins.
   - Still tied: the player **closest to the Temporary Privilege Token** wins.

   Character abilities can alter duels, e.g. the "Miner": if all played cards are black, gain **+3 coins**.

5. <!-- rule-item: turn-settle -->
   **Settle**

   The winner takes the token and the prize; tickets and blood chips are awarded by rank.

   - The winning player gains the **Temporary Privilege Token**.
   - Awards are distributed by rank (★ tickets, blood chips) — see [Prizes](#rule-rewards).
   - Target tickets: **2p 24 / 3p 20 / 4p 16**; reaching it ends the game immediately.

   If nobody reaches the target, all cards in the play area go to the discard pile and the game moves to the next phase.

6. <!-- rule-item: turn-buy -->
   **Buy**

   Take turns buying a card or passing.

   - Proceed clockwise from the **Temporary Privilege Token holder**.
   - Buy **1 black-market card** (price printed on the card), or **flip your character card** to pass.
   - The **rightmost two** black-market cards each carry +1 coin; the buyer takes it.

   Passing means flipping your character card; flip it back when everyone has passed.

7. <!-- rule-item: turn-trash -->
   **Trash**

   Slim your deck — the first trash is free.

   - Trash **1 card** from your draw pile for free.
   - Pay **2 coins** per additional card trashed, **unlimited times**.

   Trashing happens from the draw pile, with no limit on how many; more trash costs more money.

8. <!-- rule-item: turn-rebuild -->
   **Rebuild**

   Shuffle to restart, or skip shuffling for money.

   - Option one: **shuffle all cards** into a new draw pile.
   - Option two: **do not shuffle**, gain **2 coins**.

   Choose one: shuffling is more controlled; not shuffling earns you 2 coins.

<!-- rule-section: black-market -->
## Black-Market Cards

Black-market cards come in three types; the price is printed on the card. The effect triggers per type after purchase.

<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->
- <!-- rule-item: market-chip -->
  **Enhancement Chip**

   Immediately after purchase, insert it into one card in your draw pile to grant an ability, e.g. **permanent +1 value**.

   Each card holds only 1 chip and it cannot be replaced; a strengthened card's maximum value is 14 (A).

- <!-- rule-item: market-deal -->
  **Secret Deal**

   Immediately resolve its effect after purchase, e.g. take the Temporary Privilege Token, or gain +1 swap next round.

   After resolving, place it face-down into the recycling area, out of the game.

- <!-- rule-item: market-gadget -->
  **Backup Gadget**

   Put it in your gadget area after purchase; use it in the matching phase as described on the card.

   After use, discard it face-down into the recycling area.

  <!-- rule-details -->

  The rightmost two black-market cards each carry +1 coin; the buyer takes it.

## Hand Rankings

Same hand type compares the **sum of the 5 card values**; the higher wins. Three tiers from high to low.

<!-- rule-section: six-or-more -->
### Six or More

| Hand | Composition | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Seven of a Kind** | 7 cards of the same value | 9999999 |
| **Straight Six Flush** | 6 cards of the same value and suit | JJJJJJ |
| **Six of a Kind** | 6 cards of the same value | 999999 |

<!-- rule-section: five-special -->
### Five-Card Specials

| Hand | Composition | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Five of a Kind Flush** | 5 cards of the same value and suit | JJJJJ |
| **Full House Flush** | 3 of a kind + 2 of a kind, all same suit | JJJ1010 |
| **Straight Flush** | 5 consecutive values, same suit | 10JQKA |
| **Five of a Kind** | 5 cards of the same value | 99999 |

<!-- rule-section: standard -->
### Standard Hands

| Hand | Composition | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Four of a Kind** | 4 cards of the same value | 9999 |
| **Full House** | 3 of a kind + 2 of a kind | 9991010 |
| **Flush** | 5 cards of one suit, not consecutive | 25JKA |
| **Straight** | 5 consecutive values, mixed suits | 23456 |
| **Three of a Kind** | 3 cards of the same value | 444 |
| **Two Pair** | two pairs of different values | JJQQA |
| **One Pair** | 2 cards of the same value | KKQ9Q |
| **High Card** | none of the above | 10579 |

<!-- rule-section: rewards -->
## Prizes

After a duel, prizes are awarded by rank — ★ is a ticket, blood is a blood chip.

| Rank | 2 players | 3 players | 4 players |
|------|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|
| 1st | 4★ | 4★ | 4★ |
| 2nd | 4 blood | 2★ 2 blood | 2★ 2 blood |
| 3rd | — | 4 blood | 1★ 3 blood |
| 4th | — | — | 4 blood |

<!-- rule-details -->

**6–8 players:** target 16 tickets. 1st gets 2★, 2nd gets 2★ 1 blood, 3rd–4th get 1★ 1 blood, lower ranks receive blood chips to top up, and last place gets 3 blood.
