# Bomb Busters Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

Bomb Busters is a cooperative deduction game for 2–5 players designed by Hisashi Hayashi. Players form a bomb disposal squad, working together to cut matching wires and defuse a bomb before it explodes. The game features 66 progressive missions that introduce new rules and equipment, creating a campaign-style experience.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Bomb Busters, you are on a cooperative bomb-disposal team. Your goal is to identify matching wires and cut them safely before the bomb explodes.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- 1 game board with detonator dial
- 48 blue wire tiles (numbers 1–12, 4 of each)
- 11 yellow wire tiles ("1.1" to "11.1")
- 11 red wire tiles ("1.5" to "11.5")
- 5 tile stands
- 5 character cards with unique abilities
- 12 equipment cards
- 26 info tokens (including 2 yellow)
- 12 validation tokens
- 8 mission cards (plus surprise boxes for later missions)
- 7 markers (4 yellow, 3 red)
- 1 Bomb Busters standee
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup
<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->
1. <!-- rule-item: setup-item-1 -->
   **Choose a mission.** Missions get progressively harder; it's recommended to play in order.
2. <!-- rule-item: setup-item-2 -->
   **Designate a Captain** (random for the first mission, then rotate left). The Captain reads the mission card aloud.
3. <!-- rule-item: setup-item-3 -->
   **Distribute tile stands** based on player count:

| Players | Captain Stands | Others' Stands |
|:-------:|:--------------:|:--------------:|
| 2       | 2              | 2              |
| 3       | 2              | 1              |
| 4       | 1              | 1              |
| 5       | 1              | 1              |

4. <!-- rule-item: setup-item-4 -->
   **Prepare wire tiles:** Collect 48 blue wires, add random red and yellow wires as indicated by the mission card. Place colored markers on the board to indicate which red/yellow values are in play.
5. <!-- rule-item: setup-item-5 -->
   **Deal wires** face-down equally among all tile stands.
6. <!-- rule-item: setup-item-6 -->
   **Sort wires** on stands from left to right in ascending order (face toward owner). The decimal values on red/yellow wires are only for sorting.
7. <!-- rule-item: setup-item-7 -->
   **Place equipment cards** face-up on the board (unusable until unlocked).
8. <!-- rule-item: setup-item-8 -->
   **Place info tokens:** Starting with the Captain, each player places one info token pointing at one of their blue wires with a matching value to give the team a starting clue.

<!-- rule-section: how-to-play -->
## How to Play

![Point at a wire and name its number](/images/rules/bomb-busters/guess-wire.svg)

Starting with the Captain, players take turns clockwise. On your turn, you **must** perform one of three actions:
<!-- rule-section: dual-cut-main-action -->
### Dual Cut (Main Action)

The core action of the game. You attempt to cut two wires of the same value — one yours, one a teammate's.

1. Point at a specific wire on a teammate's stand.
2. State a value (e.g., "This wire is a 9").
3. Resolve:
   - **Correct:** Both wires are revealed and placed face-up in front of their stands — successfully cut!
   - **Wrong (blue/yellow wire):** The detonator advances 1 space. The teammate places an info token showing the wire's true value.
   - **Wrong (red wire):** The bomb **explodes immediately** — mission failed!

**Note:** You do not reveal where in your hand the matching wire is.
<!-- rule-section: solo-cut -->
### Solo Cut

If the remaining wires of a certain value are **all** in your hand, you can cut them without involving a teammate.

- If you have all 4 wires of the same value → cut all 4 at once.
- If 2 wires of that value were already cut and you hold the remaining 2 → cut both.

Solo cuts are based on public information and cannot fail.
<!-- rule-section: reveal-your-red-wires -->
### Reveal Your Red Wires

If **all** your remaining uncut wires are red, reveal them. This removes you from play — your turns are skipped going forward.
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: special-wires -->
## Special Wires

![Yellow wires share one value; a wrong red guess explodes the bomb](/images/rules/bomb-busters/special-wires.svg)
<!-- rule-section: yellow-wires -->
### Yellow Wires

Yellow wires are cut the same way as blue wires, but during gameplay they all share the value **"YELLOW"** (the printed number is only for sorting during setup).

- To cut a yellow wire via Dual Cut: say "This wire is yellow."
- Solo Cut with yellow wires requires having all remaining yellows in your hand.
<!-- rule-section: red-wires -->
### Red Wires

Red wires are deadly. If a Dual Cut targets a red wire incorrectly, the bomb explodes. Red wires share the value **"RED"** during gameplay.
<!-- rule-section: equipment-characters -->
## Equipment & Characters
<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->
- <!-- rule-item: equipment-characters-item-1 -->
  **Equipment cards** unlock when 2 wires of the value shown on the card have been cut. Most equipment can be used by anyone at any time (once per mission).
- <!-- rule-item: equipment-characters-item-2 -->
  **Character cards** give each player a unique one-time ability (e.g., the Double Detector lets you point at 2 wires during a Dual Cut).
<!-- rule-section: validation-tokens -->
## Validation Tokens

When all 4 wires of a value have been cut, place a validation token on that number on the board as a reminder.
<!-- rule-section: communication-rules -->
## Communication Rules
<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->
- <!-- rule-item: communication-rules-item-1 -->
  **Forbidden:** Discussing wire values, implying values, recalling info from previous turns, or sharing guesses aloud.
- <!-- rule-item: communication-rules-item-2 -->
  **Allowed:** Discussing general tactics, equipment usage, and reminding teammates about available abilities.
<!-- rule-section: game-end -->
## Game End
<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->
- <!-- rule-item: game-end-item-1 -->
  **Victory:** All tile stands are empty — every wire has been cut!
- <!-- rule-item: game-end-item-2 -->
  **Defeat:** The bomb explodes (a red wire is cut, or the detonator reaches the skull space).

If a mission fails, change the Captain and try again.
<!-- rule-section: campaign-progression -->
## Campaign Progression

Complete mission 8 to unlock the first surprise box (missions 9–19). Each box introduces new rules, equipment, and challenges. Track your progress on the box's score sheet.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-equipment -->
  **Equipment & Characters**

  ### Equipment Cards
  - Each mission provides equipment cards equal to player count
  - Equipment **unlocks** when 2 wires of the value shown on the card have been cut
  - Each piece of equipment can be used **once per mission**
  - Most can be used by **anyone at any time** (even not on your turn)
  - Multiple equipment can be used in a row
  
  ### Character Cards
  Each player gets a character card with a **unique one-time ability**:
  - **Double Detector:** During a Dual Cut, point at 2 of a teammate's wires instead of 1. If either matches, success! If both match, the teammate chooses which to cut.
  
  ### Tip
  Equipment and character abilities are powerful — coordinate with your team on when to use them for maximum impact.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-communication -->
  **Communication Rules**

  Communication is intentionally limited to increase the challenge.
  
  ### 🚫 Forbidden
  - Talking about the wires in your hand
  - Implying wire values (hints, gestures, coded language)
  - Recalling information from previous turns aloud
  - Sharing your guesses or assumptions
  
  ### ✅ Allowed
  - Discussing **general tactics** ("maybe we should focus on lower numbers")
  - Discussing **equipment usage** ("should we use the detector now?")
  - Reminding teammates about **special rules** or available abilities
  - Recalling **public information** (validation tokens, info tokens already placed)
  
  The tension between what you know and what you can say is the heart of the game!

## Quick reference

- <!-- rule-item: flow-overview -->
  **Game Overview**

  **Bomb Busters** is a cooperative deduction game for 2–5 players. You form a bomb disposal squad, working together to cut matching wires before the bomb explodes.
  
  **Goal:** Cut all safe wires (blue + yellow) without detonating the bomb.
  
  **You lose if:**
  - A red wire is incorrectly targeted during a Dual Cut
  - The detonator dial reaches the skull space
  
  The game features **66 progressive missions** that introduce new rules and equipment.

  Related topics: 
  - [Setup](#flow-setup)
  - [Your Turn Actions](#flow-turn)
  - [Special Wires](#flow-special-wires)
  - [Equipment & Characters](#flow-equipment)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-setup -->
  **Game Setup**

  1. **Choose a mission** — missions are progressively harder; play in order is recommended
  2. **Designate a Captain** (random for first mission, then rotate left)
  3. **Distribute tile stands:**
  
  | Players | Captain | Others |
  |:-------:|:-------:|:------:|
  | 2 | 2 stands | 2 stands |
  | 3 | 2 stands | 1 stand |
  | 4–5 | 1 stand | 1 stand |
  
  4. **Prepare wires:** 48 blue wires + red/yellow wires per mission card. Place colored markers on the board.
  5. **Deal wires** face-down equally among all stands
  6. **Sort wires** left-to-right in ascending order (face toward owner)
  7. **Place equipment cards** on the board (locked until unlocked)
  8. **Place info tokens:** Each player places 1 info token pointing at one of their blue wires, revealing its value as a starting clue

  Related topics: 
  - [Your Turn Actions](#flow-turn)
  - [Back to Overview](#flow-overview)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-turn -->
  **Your Turn — 3 Actions**

  Starting with the Captain, take turns clockwise. On your turn, you **must** do exactly **one** of these three actions:
  
  | Action | When to Use |
  |--------|-------------|
  | **Dual Cut** | Main action — guess a teammate's wire to cut a matching pair |
  | **Solo Cut** | All remaining wires of a value are in your hand |
  | **Reveal Red** | All your remaining wires are red |
  
  Choose an action to learn more:

- <!-- rule-item: flow-dual-cut -->
  **Dual Cut Action**

  The core action — you cut 2 wires of the same value: 1 yours + 1 teammate's.
  
  ### How it works
  1. Point at a specific wire on a teammate's stand
  2. State a value: *"This wire is a 9"*
  3. Resolve:
  
  | Result | What Happens |
  |--------|-------------|
  | ✅ **Correct** | Both wires revealed and placed face-up — cut! |
  | ❌ **Wrong (blue/yellow)** | Detonator advances 1 space. Teammate places an info token showing the true value. |
  | 💥 **Wrong (red wire)** | Bomb **explodes immediately** — mission failed! |
  
  **Important:** You do NOT reveal where your matching wire is in your hand.
  
  **For yellow wires:** Say *"This wire is yellow"* — all yellows share the value "YELLOW" during gameplay.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-solo-cut -->
  **Solo Cut Action**

  If the remaining wires of a certain value are **all in your hand**, you can cut them without a teammate.
  
  ### When you can Solo Cut

  Solo cuts are based on **public information** (validation tokens on the board show which values have pairs cut) and **cannot fail**.
