# Brass: Birmingham Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

**Brass: Birmingham** (Matt Tolman, Martin Wallace, Gavan Brown; Roxley, **2018**) is an economic strategy sequel to Wallace’s 2007 *Brass*. You compete as entrepreneurs in Birmingham during the Industrial Revolution, **1770–1870**.

Develop, build, and network your industries to exploit low or high demand. The game has two halves: the **Canal Era** (1770–1830) and the **Rail Era** (1830–1870). Score victory points (VPs) at the end of each half for your canals/rails and **flipped** industry tiles. Highest VP after the Rail Era wins.

Each round, take turns in turn-order-track order. You get **two actions** (the first Canal round: **one action**). You may take the same action twice.

![Chinese detailed one-page guide: eras, turn loop, six actions, resources, flipping, income, and era scoring](/images/rules/brass-birmingham/quickstart-guide.png)

| # | Action | Needs a matching card? |
|---|--------|------------------------|
| 1 | **Build** — pay and place an industry | Yes (location or industry) |
| 2 | **Network** — add a canal or rail | Any card |
| 3 | **Develop** — remove lower tiles from your mat | Any card |
| 4 | **Sell** — flip mills / manufacturers / potteries | Any card |
| 5 | **Loan** — £30, income 3 **levels** back | Any card |
| 6 | **Scout** — discard 3 cards, take 2 wilds | Any card (+ 2 more) |

Scout replaces original Brass’s double-build.

![Two actions, each discards a card; cash sits on your pawn](/images/rules/brass-birmingham/turn-flow.svg)
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Brass: Birmingham, you are an industrial entrepreneur working through canal and rail eras. Your goal is to build profitable networks and industries, then earn the most victory points by 1870.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- 1 double-sided board (day / night)
- 4 player mats, 4 character tiles, 4 player aids
- 56 link tiles (14 per colour)
- **180** industry tiles (45 per colour). Per colour: 11 cotton mills, 11 manufacturers, 7 coal mines, 7 breweries, 5 potteries, 4 iron works
- 64 location / industry cards + **8 wilds** (4 location, 4 industry)
- 9 merchant tiles
- Coal cubes, iron cubes, beer barrels, money
- Income markers and VP markers
- Rulebook (v2018.11)

Blank punchboard leftovers are unused.

<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup
<!-- rule-section: board -->
### Board

1. Place the board (day or night).
2. If fewer than 4 players, return cards and merchant tiles whose **player-count icon is higher than your count**.
   - **2 players:** no blue or teal location cards; no merchants in **Warrington** or **Nottingham**.
   - **3 players:** no teal location cards; no merchant in **Nottingham**.
   - Banner colours on the map tell you which location cards are in the deck.
3. Place wild location and wild industry cards **face up** on two card-draw areas (not shuffled in).
4. Shuffle remaining cards as the **draw deck**.
5. Shuffle remaining merchant tiles onto merchant spaces marked for your player count.
6. Put **1 beer** on each beer space beside a **non-blank** merchant.
7. **Coal market:** fill every space except **one** of the £1 spaces.
8. **Iron market:** fill every space except **both** £1 spaces.
9. Remaining cubes, barrels, and money form the general supply and bank. The supply is **unlimited** (improvise if you run out).
<!-- rule-section: player-area -->
### Player area

1. Player mat, **£17**, matching character, links, and industry stacks. Stacks sit **unflipped** (black top **down**).
2. VP marker on **0**; income marker on **10**.
3. Draw **8** cards (secret). Draw **1 extra** and place it **face down** as the bottom of your discard pile.
4. After everyone is ready, shuffle character tiles onto the turn-order track at random.

<!-- rule-section: how-to-play -->
## How to Play

Two eras. Each era has **8 / 9 / 10** rounds at **4 / 3 / 2** players — the draw deck and all hands empty together.
<!-- rule-section: your-turn -->
### Your turn

Perform **2 actions**. **Canal Era, round 1:** only **1 action**. Passing is allowed but still discards a card.

- Every action discards **1 card** face up. **Wilds return to their draw areas**, not your discard.
- Only **Build** needs a matching card. Other actions discard any card.
- After both actions, draw back to **8**. Once the deck is empty, hands shrink each round until empty.
- Money spent this turn sits **on your character tile** (not the bank). That pile sets next-round turn order.
<!-- rule-section: end-of-round -->
### End of round

1. **Turn order:** least money spent goes first next round. Equal spenders keep their **relative** order. Then dump the piles into the bank.
2. **Income:** take (or pay) the amount shown on the **coin beside** your income marker — that number is your **income level**. **Skip income after the last round of the game.**
   - Negative income: pay the bank. If you cannot, sell your own **industry** tiles (not links) for **half printed cost, rounded down**, removing them from the game. Stop as soon as you cover the shortfall; keep leftover cash. You may not sell industries for any other reason. If you still cannot pay, **−1 VP per £1** short (if you have VP to lose).

**Example:** You spent £4, an opponent spent £12, another spent £4. The two £4 players keep the same order they had this round, then the £12 player goes last.
<!-- rule-section: cards -->
## Cards

| Card | Build with it | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| **Location** | Any industry at that **named city**, even off-network | 2/3p: cities whose cards were removed can still be built |
| **Wild location** | Any city **except** the two farm breweries | Returns to the wild pile when used |
| **Industry** | That industry, and only **in your network** | Icon in the top corner |
| **Wild industry** | Any industry (farm breweries allowed) | Returns to the wild pile |

<!-- rule-section: actions -->
## Actions
<!-- rule-section: 1-build -->
### 1. Build

1. Discard an appropriate card (see table above).
2. Take the **lowest-level** tile of that industry from your mat (black-top down).
3. Place it on an undeveloped space in the chosen city:
   - Prefer a space showing **only** that industry’s icon.
   - Else a space showing that icon **plus** another.
   - If no such space remains, you cannot build there (except **overbuild**).
4. Pay the cost printed **left of the slot** onto your character. Consume any required coal / iron. Coal needs a connection **after** the tile is placed.

| Era | Placement |
|-----|-----------|
| Canal | At most **1 of your** tiles per location (opponents may share the city). Only tiles with a **canal icon** left of the slot. |
| Rail | Several of yours per city allowed. Canal-icon tiles **cannot** be built — **Develop** them off the mat. **Exception:** level 1 **pottery** has no canal icon and **may** be built in the Rail Era. |

**When the tile hits the board**

- **Coal mine / iron works:** put cubes from supply equal to the bottom-right icon.
- **Brewery:** **1 beer** in the Canal Era, **2** in the Rail Era.

**Selling to market — only in this same Build action**

- **Coal mine** connected to **any merchant space** (even empty of a merchant tile), **or**
- **Iron works** (connection **not** required):

Move as many cubes as will fit onto that market (**highest price first**) and take the printed cash. If the last cube leaves the tile, **flip** it and gain the printed **income spaces**. Cubes are **never** sold to market on later turns.

**Empty board:** you have no industries and no links → an industry card may build that industry on any legal space; any card may place a link on any legal line (still pay coal for rails).
<!-- rule-section: 2-network -->
### 2. Network

Discard any card. The new link must touch **your network**, unless the board is empty of your tiles.

![Your network is your tiles and cities next to your links. Connected means a path of anyone’s links.](/images/rules/brass-birmingham/network.svg)

**Your network:** a location with **your** industry, **or** adjacent to **your** link.

**Connected:** a path of **anyone’s** links from one location to another. Needed for coal, selling to merchants, and drinking **opponent** beer.

| Era | Links |
|-----|--------|
| Canal | Canals only. **1** link for **£3**. No rails. |
| Rail | Rails only. **1** rail for **£5** + **1 coal**; or **2** rails for **£15** + **1 brewery beer** (not merchant beer) + **1 coal per rail**. Place each rail separately; each must connect to coal **after** it is placed. Opponent beer for a double rail must connect to the **second** rail. |

You can Network twice in one turn (e.g. 1–2 rails, then 1–2 more).
<!-- rule-section: 3-develop -->
### 3. Develop

1. Discard any card.
2. Remove **1 or 2** tiles from your mat (any mix of industries). Each must be the **current lowest** of its industry as you remove it.
3. Consume **1 iron per tile**.

Lightbulb **pottery** **cannot** be developed — you must **Build** it to uncover higher pottery.
<!-- rule-section: 4-sell -->
### 4. Sell

You cannot start Sell if you cannot pay the beer.

1. Discard any card.
2. Choose one of your unflipped cotton mills, manufacturers, or potteries that is **connected** to a merchant showing that industry’s icon.
3. Consume the beer printed on the **top right** of the tile. You may drink **1 merchant beer** beside the merchant you sell to (and take that merchant’s bonus). Extra barrels may come from other sources.
4. **Flip** the tile and advance income by the printed **spaces**.
5. Repeat steps 2–4 for other unflipped goods tiles (including other industries) as part of the **same** Sell.

**Merchant beer bonuses** (Sell only):

| Location | Bonus |
|----------|--------|
| Gloucester | Develop 1 lowest tile, **no iron** (not lightbulb pottery) |
| Oxford | **+2 income spaces** |
| Nottingham / Shrewsbury | VP printed beside that merchant |
| Warrington | **£5** |
<!-- rule-section: 5-loan -->
### 5. Loan

Discard any card. Take **£30**. Move income **3 levels** backward (not 3 spaces) and sit on the **highest space** of the new level. Never repaid. Illegal if it would drop you below income **−10**.

**Spaces vs levels:** flipping a mill might say “+3” — that is **3 spaces** along the track. A loan always drops you **3 entire income bands**.
<!-- rule-section: 6-scout -->
### 6. Scout

Discard the action card **plus 2 more** (three cards total). Take **1 wild location** and **1 wild industry**. **Illegal if you already have a wild in hand.**

<!-- rule-section: resources -->
## Resources

![Coal needs a connection; iron does not; beer depends on whose brewery](/images/rules/brass-birmingham/resources.svg)
<!-- rule-section: coal -->
### Coal

Needed for rails and some builds. After placing the tile/link, it must connect to coal:

1. Closest connected **unflipped coal mine** (any owner) — free. Ties: you choose. If it empties, use the next closest.
2. Else **coal market**, cheapest space first. You need a connection to a merchant **coal** icon: Warrington, Shrewsbury, Nottingham, Gloucester, Oxford. Empty market: **£8** per cube.
<!-- rule-section: iron -->
### Iron

No connection required.

1. Any unflipped **iron works** (any owner) — free; mix sources if you need several.
2. Else iron market, cheapest first. Empty market: **£6** per cube.
<!-- rule-section: beer -->
### Beer

Used to sell goods and for the Rail **double** network.

1. **Your** unflipped brewery — **no** connection needed (even across the map).
2. **Opponent** unflipped brewery — **must** be connected to where beer is spent.
3. Merchant beer — **only** when selling to that merchant.

If several barrels are required, each may come from a different source. Consumed barrels return to supply.
<!-- rule-section: flipping-and-income -->
### Flipping and income

| Flip when | Industries |
|-----------|------------|
| You **Sell** | Cotton mill, manufacturer, pottery |
| Last cube/barrel is removed (often on someone else’s turn) | Coal mine, iron works, brewery |

On flip, advance income by the printed **spaces** (not levels). Income cannot go above level **30**.
<!-- rule-section: farm-breweries -->
## Farm breweries

Two unnamed spots with only a brewery icon.

- Build only with a **brewery** industry card or a **wild industry** (not a wild location).
- Cannock’s farm needs a link on the line to Cannock.
- A link between **Kidderminster and Worcester** also connects their farm; you may **not** place a second link there.
<!-- rule-section: overbuilding -->
## Overbuilding

Replace a tile with a **higher level of the same industry** (still pay the new tile’s cost). Remaining cubes/barrels on the old tile go to **supply**.
<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->
- <!-- rule-item: overbuilding-item-1 -->
  **Your** tile: any industry.
- <!-- rule-item: overbuilding-item-2 -->
  **Opponent** tile: **only** coal mine or iron works, and **only** if **no** cubes of that type remain on **any** tile **or** in that market.

Overbuilt tiles leave the game (they will not score). Income and VP already earned stay.
<!-- rule-section: end-of-era -->
## End of Era

Each era ends after the round in which hands empty.

1. **Links:** for each of your links, **1 VP per industry icon** in the two adjacent locations (count every icon printed in those cities). **Remove** the link as you score it.
2. **Flipped industries:** score the VP in the bottom-left. Unflipped tiles score **0**. If you pass 100 VP, loop the track.

**Example:** Your canal sits between a city with 2 icons and a city with 3. That link is **5 VP**.

**Canal Era extra:**

3. Remove **all level 1** industries from the **board** (tiles still on mats stay). Level 2+ remain and can score **again** in the Rail Era.
4. Reset merchant beer on empty spaces beside non-blank merchants.
5. Flip the face-down bottom card of each discard pile, shuffle **all** discards into a new deck.
6. Each player draws **8**.
<!-- rule-section: winning -->
## Winning

After **Rail Era** scoring, most VP wins. Ties: highest **income**, then most **money**. Still tied: draw.
<!-- rule-section: remember -->
## Remember

- Same action twice in a turn is legal.
- Discard 1 card for every action, including pass.
- Loans are never repaid; end-game income is **not** worth VP.
- You do not need a connection to drink **your** beer.
- A location must be in your network to build there **unless** you play that city’s location card (or a wild location).
<!-- rule-section: introductory-game-optional -->
## Introductory game (optional)

Play **only the Canal Era**, including normal Canal scoring, then add:

1. **1 VP per £4** (max **15** VP).
2. VP equal to your **income level** (negative income loses VP).
3. Score **level 2+** industries **a second time**.

  Two places are **connected** if a path of **anyone’s** links joins them. Connection matters for coal, selling to merchants, and drinking **opponent** beer.
  
  Your **own** brewery beer needs **no** connection.

  ![Your network is your tiles and cities next to your links. Connected means a path of anyone’s links.](/images/rules/brass-birmingham/network.svg)

  Related topics: 
  - [Network Action](#flow-network)
  - [Build](#flow-build)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-network -->
  **Network**

  Discard any card. New link must touch **your network** (unless you have nothing on the board).
  
  **Canal:** canals only, **1** link for **£3**.
  **Rail:** rails only. **1** for **£5 + 1 coal**, or **2** for **£15 + 1 brewery beer** (not merchant) **+ 1 coal each**. Place each rail separately; each must reach coal **after** it is placed. Opponent beer for a double rail must connect to the **second** rail.

  Related topics: 
  - [Resources](#flow-resources)
  - [Your Network](#flow-network-concept)
  - [Sell](#flow-sell)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-develop-loan-scout -->
  **Develop, Loan, Scout**

  **Develop:** discard any card. Remove **1 or 2** lowest tiles from your mat (any industries) and pay **1 iron each**. Lightbulb pottery **cannot** be developed.
  
  **Loan:** discard any card. **£30**, income **3 levels** back (sit on the highest space of the new level). Never repaid. Illegal below income **−10**.
  
  **Scout:** discard **3 cards** (action + 2 more). Take 1 wild location + 1 wild industry. **Illegal if you already hold a wild.**

  Related topics: 
  - [Resources](#flow-resources)
  - [Sell](#flow-sell)
  - [Your Turn](#flow-turn)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-sell -->
  **Sell**

  Discard any card. Sell each unflipped mill / manufacturer / pottery that is **connected** to a merchant with that icon: pay beer, **flip**, gain printed **income spaces**, then you may sell another.
  
  You may drink **1 merchant beer** at the merchant you sell to.
  
  **Bonuses:** Gloucester = free develop (no iron; not lightbulb pottery) · Oxford = **+2 income spaces** · Nottingham / Shrewsbury = printed VP · Warrington = **£5**.
  
  Cannot Sell if you cannot pay the beer.

  Related topics: 
  - [Resources](#flow-resources)
  - [Era Scoring](#flow-scoring)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-resources -->
  **Coal, Iron, Beer**

  **Coal** (after placing): closest connected unflipped mine (any owner, free), else coal market via a merchant coal icon (**£8** if empty).
  
  **Iron** (no connection): any unflipped iron works (free), else iron market (**£6** if empty).
  
  **Beer:** your unflipped brewery (no connection) · opponent brewery (must connect) · merchant beer on Sell only. Double rail needs **brewery** beer, not merchant beer.
  
  Mills/manuf/pottery flip on **Sell**. Mines/works/breweries flip when the **last** resource leaves. Flip = income **spaces**, cap level **30**.

  ![Coal needs a connection; iron does not; beer depends on whose brewery](/images/rules/brass-birmingham/resources.svg)

  Related topics: 
  - [Build](#flow-build)
  - [Sell](#flow-sell)
  - [Network](#flow-network)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-overbuild -->
  **Overbuild**

  Replace a tile with a **higher level of the same industry** (still pay the new cost).
  
  **Yours:** any industry. Leftover cubes/barrels go to supply.
  **Opponent:** **only** coal or iron, and **only** if **no** cubes of that type remain on tiles **or** in that market.
  
  Overbuilt tiles leave the game (no era VP). Income and VP already earned stay.

  Related topics: 
  - [Build](#flow-build)
  - [Era Scoring](#flow-scoring)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-farms -->
  **Farm Breweries**

  Two unnamed brewery-only spots. Build with a **brewery** or **wild industry** card only (not a wild location).
  
  Cannock’s farm needs a link on the line to Cannock.
  A link between **Kidderminster and Worcester** also connects their farm — do not place a second link there.

  Related topics: 
  - [Build](#flow-build)
  - [Network](#flow-network)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-scoring -->
  **Era Scoring**

  When hands empty:
  
  1. Each of **your links:** 1 VP per **industry icon** in the two adjacent cities; **remove** the link. A 2-icon city plus a 3-icon city = **5 VP** for that canal/rail.
  2. **Flipped** industries: VP in the bottom-left. Unflipped = 0. Loop the track past 100.
  
  **Canal extra:** remove all **level 1** tiles from the **board** (level 2+ stay and can score again); reset merchant beer; flip facedown discard bottoms; shuffle all discards; draw 8.
  
  After **Rail** scoring: most VP wins. Ties: highest income, then most money.

  Related topics: 
  - [Introductory Game](#flow-intro)
  - [Your Turn](#flow-turn)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-cards -->
  **Cards**

  **Location:** any industry at that named city, even off-network.
  **Wild location:** any city **except** the two farm breweries.
  **Industry:** that type, **in your network** only.
  **Wild industry:** any industry (farms allowed).
  
  Wilds return to their piles, not your discard. Only Build needs a matching card.

  Related topics: 
  - [Build](#flow-build)
  - [Scout](#flow-develop-loan-scout)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-intro -->
  **Introductory Game**

  Play **Canal Era only**, including normal Canal scoring, then add:
  
  1. **1 VP per £4** (max **15**).
  2. VP equal to your **income level** (negative income loses VP).
  3. Score **level 2+** industries **again**.

  Related topics: 
  - [Era Scoring](#flow-scoring)
  - [Setup](#flow-setup)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-turn -->
  **Your Turn**

  **2 actions** (same action twice OK). **Canal round 1: 1 action.**
  
  Each action or pass discards **1 card**. Wilds return to their piles.
  
  Only **Build** needs a matching card. Then draw back to **8**. Spent money sits **on your character**.
  
  **End of round:** least spent goes first (ties keep relative order), cash to bank, then **income** (skip after the last round of the game).
  
  Negative income: pay the bank. If short, sell your own industries (not links) at **half printed cost, rounded down**, and stop as soon as you can pay. Still short: **−1 VP per £1**.
  
  **8 / 9 / 10** rounds per era at **4 / 3 / 2** players.
