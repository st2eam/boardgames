# Brass: Birmingham Rules

## Overview

**Brass: Birmingham** (Matt Tolman, Martin Wallace, Gavan Brown; Roxley, **2018**) is an economic strategy sequel to Wallace’s 2007 *Brass*. You compete as entrepreneurs in Birmingham during the Industrial Revolution, **1770–1870**.

Develop, build, and network your industries to exploit low or high demand. The game has two halves: the **Canal Era** (1770–1830) and the **Rail Era** (1830–1870). Score victory points (VPs) at the end of each half for your canals/rails and **flipped** industry tiles. Highest VP after the Rail Era wins.

Each round, take turns in turn-order-track order. You get **two actions** (the first Canal round: **one action**). Actions from the original game:

1. **Build** — pay resources and place an industry tile.
2. **Network** — add a canal or rail link.
3. **Develop** — skip lower-level tiles to unlock higher VP industries.
4. **Sell** — sell cotton, manufactured goods, and pottery.
5. **Loan** — take £30 and reduce your income.

Birmingham adds a sixth action, which replaces original Brass’s double-build:

6. **Scout** — discard **three** cards and take 1 wild location + 1 wild industry.

## Components

- 1 double-sided board (day / night)
- 4 player mats, 4 character tiles, 4 player aids
- 56 link tiles (14 per colour)
- 180 industry tiles (45 per colour): cotton mills, coal mines, iron works, manufacturers, potteries, breweries
- 64 location / industry cards + **8 wilds** (4 location, 4 industry)
- 9 merchant tiles
- Coal cubes, iron cubes, beer barrels, money
- Income markers and VP markers
- Rulebook (v2018.11)

## Setup

### Board

1. Place the board (day or night).
2. If fewer than 4 players, return cards and merchant tiles whose **player-count icon is higher than your count**.
   - **2 players:** no blue or teal location cards; no merchants in **Warrington** or **Nottingham**.
   - **3 players:** no teal location cards; no merchant in **Nottingham**.
3. Place wild location and wild industry cards **face up** on two card-draw areas.
4. Shuffle remaining cards as the **draw deck**.
5. Shuffle remaining merchant tiles onto merchant spaces marked for your player count.
6. Put **1 beer** on each beer space beside a **non-blank** merchant.
7. **Coal market:** fill every space except **one** of the £1 spaces.
8. **Iron market:** fill every space except **both** £1 spaces.
9. Remaining cubes, barrels, and money form the general supply and bank (supply is unlimited).

### Player area

1. Player mat, **£17**, matching character, links, and industry stacks (unflipped / black-top **down**).
2. VP marker on **0**; income marker on **10**.
3. Draw **8** cards (secret). Draw **1 extra** and place it **face down** as your discard pile.
4. After everyone is ready, shuffle character tiles onto the turn-order track at random.

## How to Play

Two eras. Each era has **8 / 9 / 10** rounds at **4 / 3 / 2** players (deck and hands empty together).

### Your turn

Perform **2 actions** (same action twice is allowed). **Canal Era, round 1:** only **1 action**.

- Every action — including **pass** — discards **1 card** face up. Wilds return to their draw areas, not your discard.
- After both actions, draw back to **8**. Once the deck is empty, hands shrink until empty.
- Money spent this turn sits **on your character tile** (not the bank). That pile sets next-round turn order.

Only **Build** needs a matching card. Other actions discard any card.

### End of round

1. **Turn order:** least money spent goes first next round; equal spenders keep their relative order. Then dump the piles into the bank.
2. **Income:** take (or pay) the amount shown beside your income marker. **Skip income after the last round of the game.**
   - Negative income: pay the bank. If you cannot, sell your own **industry** tiles (not links) for **half cost, rounded down**, removing them from the game, until you cover the shortfall. Keep leftover cash. If you still cannot pay, **−1 VP per £1** short.

## Actions

### 1. Build

Discard a **location** card (build any industry at that named city — even off-network), a **wild location** (any city **except** the two farm breweries), an **industry** card (that industry, **in your network**), or a **wild industry**.

Take the **lowest-level** tile of that industry from your mat. Pay the cost printed left of its slot; consume any required coal / iron.

| Era | Placement |
|-----|-----------|
| Canal | At most **1 of your** tiles per location (opponents may share the city). Canal-only tiles (canal icon left of the slot) only. |
| Rail | Multiple of yours per city allowed. Canal-only tiles **cannot** be built — develop them off the mat instead. **Exception:** level 1 **pottery** has no canal icon and **may** be built in the Rail Era. |

**Farm breweries** (two unnamed brewery-only spots): brewery or wild industry only. Cannock’s farm needs a link on the line to Cannock. A link between **Kidderminster and Worcester** also connects their farm; you may not place a second link there.

Place coal / iron cubes on a new mine or works equal to the icon on the tile. A **brewery** gets **1 beer** in the Canal Era or **2** in the Rail Era.

**Selling to market (only when built):** if a **coal mine is connected to any merchant space** (even empty), or you built an **iron works** (connection not required), immediately move as many cubes as possible onto the market (highest price first) and take that cash. If the last cube leaves the tile, **flip** it and gain the printed income spaces. Cubes are **never** sold to market on later turns.

**Empty board:** no industries and no links → an industry card may build that industry on any legal space; any card may place a link on any legal line (still pay coal for rails).

### 2. Network

Discard any card. The new link must touch **your network**, unless the board is empty of your tiles.

**Your network:** a location with **your** industry, or adjacent to **your** link. **Connected** (for coal, selling, opponent beer, etc.) means a path of **anyone’s** links.

| Era | Links |
|-----|--------|
| Canal | Canals only. **1** link for **£3**. No rails. |
| Rail | Rails only. **1** rail for **£5** + **1 coal**; or **2** rails for **£15** + **1 brewery beer** (not merchant beer) + **1 coal per rail**. Each rail is placed separately and must connect to coal **after** it is placed. Opponent beer for the double rail must connect to the **second** rail. |

### 3. Develop

Discard any card. Remove **1 or 2** lowest-level tiles from your mat (any mix of industries) and consume **1 iron each**. Lightbulb **pottery** **cannot** be developed — you must **build** it to uncover higher pottery.

### 4. Sell

Discard any card. For each unflipped cotton mill, manufacturer, or pottery that is **connected** to a merchant showing that icon: consume the beer on the tile, **flip**, gain the printed **income spaces**, then you may sell another.

You may use **1 merchant beer** beside the merchant you sell to (and take that merchant’s bonus). You cannot start Sell if you cannot pay the beer.

**Merchant beer bonuses** (Sell only):

| Location | Bonus |
|----------|--------|
| Gloucester | Develop 1 lowest tile, **no iron** (not lightbulb pottery) |
| Oxford | **+2 income spaces** |
| Nottingham / Shrewsbury | VP printed beside the merchant |
| Warrington | **£5** |

### 5. Loan

Discard any card. Take **£30**. Move income **3 levels** backward (not spaces) and sit on the **highest space** of the new level. Never repaid. Illegal if it would drop you below income **−10**.

### 6. Scout

Discard the action card **plus 2 more** (three cards total). Take **1 wild location** and **1 wild industry**. **Illegal if you already have a wild in hand.**

## Resources

### Coal

Needed for rails and some builds. After placing the tile/link, it must connect to coal:

1. Closest connected **unflipped coal mine** (any owner) — free. Ties: you choose. If it empties, use the next closest.
2. Else **coal market** (need a connection to a merchant **coal** icon: Warrington, Shrewsbury, Nottingham, Gloucester, Oxford). Empty market: **£8** per cube.

### Iron

No connection required.

1. Any unflipped **iron works** (any owner) — free; mix sources if you need several.
2. Else iron market. Empty market: **£6** per cube.

### Beer

Used to sell goods and for the Rail **double** network. Sources:

1. **Your** unflipped brewery — **no** connection needed.
2. **Opponent** unflipped brewery — **must** be connected to where beer is spent.
3. Merchant beer — **only** when selling to that merchant.

If several barrels are required, each may come from a different source.

### Flipping and income

Mills, manufacturers, and potteries flip on **Sell**. Mines, iron works, and breweries flip when the **last** cube/barrel is removed (often on someone else’s turn). On flip, advance income by the printed **spaces** (not levels). Income cannot go above level **30**.

## Overbuilding

Replace a tile with a **higher level of the same industry** (still pay the new tile’s cost).

- **Your** tile: any industry. Remaining cubes/barrels go to supply.
- **Opponent** tile: **only** coal mine or iron works, and **only** if **no** cubes of that type remain on **any** tile **or** in that market.

Overbuilt tiles leave the game (they will not score). Income and VP already earned stay.

## End of Era

Each era ends after the round in which hands empty.

1. **Links:** for each of your links, **1 VP per icon** in the two adjacent locations. **Remove** the link as you score it.
2. **Flipped industries:** score the VP in the bottom-left. Unflipped tiles score **0**. If you pass 100 VP, loop the track.

**Canal Era extra:**

3. Remove **all level 1** industries from the **board** (not leftover tiles on mats).
4. Reset merchant beer on empty spaces beside non-blank merchants.
5. Flip the face-down bottom card of each discard pile, shuffle all discards into a new deck.
6. Each player draws **8**.

## Winning

After **Rail Era** scoring, most VP wins. Ties: highest **income**, then most **money**. Still tied: draw.

## Introductory game (optional)

Play **only the Canal Era**, then add:

1. **1 VP per £4** (max **15** VP).
2. VP equal to your **income level** (negative income loses VP).
3. Score **level 2+** industries **a second time**.
