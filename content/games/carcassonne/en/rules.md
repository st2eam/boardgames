# Carcassonne Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

Carcassonne is a classic tile-laying game set in the medieval French countryside. Players take turns drawing and placing tiles to build cities, roads, monasteries, and fields. Players deploy their followers (meeples) to claim features and score points when they are completed. The player with the most points wins.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Carcassonne, you are shaping a medieval landscape one tile at a time. Your goal is to claim and complete valuable cities, roads, monasteries, and fields for the highest score.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components
<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->
- <!-- rule-item: components-item-1 -->
  **72 land tiles** — Featuring cities, roads, monasteries, and fields
- <!-- rule-item: components-item-2 -->
  **40 followers (meeples)** — 8 in each of 5 colors
- <!-- rule-item: components-item-3 -->
  **1 scoreboard**
- <!-- rule-item: components-item-4 -->
  **5 abbots** (1 per color, for gardens)
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

1. Place the starting tile (dark back) in the center of the table.
2. Shuffle the remaining tiles and place face down as the draw pile.
3. Each player takes 8 meeples of their chosen color (7 followers + 1 abbot).
4. Place one meeple of each color on the scoreboard (0).
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: how-to-play -->
## How to Play

![City edge to city edge, then maybe a meeple](/images/rules/carcassonne/turn.svg)

On your turn, you must perform the following actions in order:
<!-- rule-section: 1-draw-and-place-a-tile -->
### 1. Draw and Place a Tile
Draw one tile and place it adjacent to existing tiles. The new tile must match all adjacent edges:
- Roads must connect to roads
- City walls must connect to city walls
- Fields must connect to fields

If a tile cannot be legally placed anywhere, it is discarded and a new tile is drawn.
<!-- rule-section: 2-deploy-a-follower-optional -->
### 2. Deploy a Follower (Optional)
After placing your tile, you may place one meeple on that tile on one of these features:
- **Road** — As a Highwayman
- **City** — As a Knight
- **Monastery** — As a Monk
- **Field** — As a Farmer (laid on its side)

You CANNOT place a meeple on a feature already claimed by another player, even on a different tile. However, features can become shared if two separate claimed features are later connected.
<!-- rule-section: 3-score-completed-features -->
### 3. Score Completed Features
If the tile you placed completes a feature, score it immediately and return any meeples on it:

| Feature | Scoring |
|---------|---------|
| **Road** | 1 point per tile in the completed road |
| **City** | 2 points per tile + 2 points per coat of arms (pennant/shield) |
| **Monastery** | 1 point per surrounding tile + 1 for the monastery itself (max 9 points) |

Ties for cities and roads: the player with the most meeples on the feature scores all points. If tied, all tied players score full points.
<!-- rule-section: end-game-final-scoring -->
## End Game & Final Scoring

When the last tile is placed, the game ends. Uncompleted features score partial points:

| Feature | Final Scoring |
|---------|--------------|
| **Road** | 1 point per tile |
| **City** | 1 point per tile + 1 point per coat of arms |
| **Monastery** | 1 point per surrounding tile + monastery |
| **Field** | 3 points per completed city adjacent to the field |
<!-- rule-section: farmer-scoring -->
## Farmer Scoring

![Farmer on the field scores connected cities](/images/rules/carcassonne/farmer.svg)

Farmers are never returned during the game. At the end, each field supplies each completed city it touches. Each city scores 3 points for **each field** that connects to it. The player(s) with the most farmers in each field earn those points.
<!-- rule-section: the-abbot -->
## The Abbot

The abbot can only be placed on a monastery or garden. At the start of any turn, you may choose to **retrieve your abbot** instead of placing a tile. The abbot's monastery/ garden scores immediately as if it were the end of the game, and you forfeit your tile placement that turn.
<!-- rule-section: winning -->
## Winning

The player with the highest total score after final scoring wins.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-turn -->
  **Turn Structure**

  On your turn, perform these steps **in order**:
  
  ### 1. Draw & Place a Tile
  Draw one tile and place it adjacent to existing tiles. See **Placing Tiles** for matching rules.
  
  ### 2. Optionally Place a Meeple
  You may place **one** meeple on the tile you just placed. See **Placing Meeples** for valid locations.
  
  ### 3. Score Completed Features
  If your tile completes a road, city, or monastery, score it immediately and return meeples on that feature.
  
  **Tip:** You can skip meeple placement to save followers for later — but you only get one chance per tile.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-placing-tile -->
  **Placing Tiles**

  Every new tile must be placed **adjacent** to at least one existing tile.
  
  **Edge matching rules:**
  - **Roads** must connect to roads
  - **City walls** must connect to city walls
  - **Fields** must connect to fields
  
  **Illegal placement?** If a drawn tile cannot be legally placed anywhere on the board, discard it and draw a new one.
  
  **Tips:**
  - Extend features you already control to score sooner
  - Block opponents by closing features before they can place meeples
  - Split or merge cities and roads by how you connect tiles

- <!-- rule-item: flow-placing-meeple -->
  **Placing Meeples**

  After placing a tile, you may deploy **one meeple** on that tile:
  
  | Feature | Role | Notes |
  |---------|------|-------|
  | **Road** | Highwayman | Scored when road is closed at both ends |
  | **City** | Knight | Scored when city walls fully enclose the city |
  | **Monastery** | Monk | Scored when monastery is fully surrounded |
  | **Field** | Farmer | Laid on its side; scored only at game end |
  
  **Cannot place if:**
  - The feature is already claimed by any player (even on a different tile)
  - You have no meeples left in your supply
  
  **Shared features:** Two separately claimed features that later connect become one feature. Majority rules apply at scoring.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-city-scoring -->
  **City Scoring**

  A city is **completed** when it is fully enclosed by city walls with no open edges.
  
  ### Completed City (during game)
  - **2 points** per tile in the city
  - **+2 points** per coat of arms (pennant/shield)
  - Meeples are returned immediately
  
  ### Incomplete City (end of game)
  - **1 point** per tile
  - **+1 point** per coat of arms
  
  ### Majority
  The player with the **most knights** in the city scores. If tied, **all tied players** score the full amount.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-road-scoring -->
  **Road Scoring**

  A road is **completed** when both ends terminate at a city, monastery, crossing, or dead end (no open road edges).
  
  ### Completed Road (during game)
  - **1 point** per tile in the road
  - Meeples are returned immediately
  
  ### Incomplete Road (end of game)
  - **1 point** per tile
  
  ### Majority
  The player with the **most highwaymen** on the road scores. If tied, **all tied players** score the full amount.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-monastery-scoring -->
  **Monastery Scoring**

  A monastery is **completed** when it is surrounded on all **8 sides** by tiles (including diagonals).
  
  ### Completed Monastery (during game)
  - **9 points** total (1 for the monastery + 1 per surrounding tile)
  - Meeple is returned immediately
  
  ### Incomplete Monastery (end of game)
  - **1 point** for the monastery tile
  - **+1 point** per tile placed adjacent to it (including diagonals)
  
  Only the player who placed the monk on that monastery scores — there is no sharing.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-field-scoring -->
  **Field Scoring**

  Farmers are placed **on their side** and are **never returned** during the game. They score **only at game end**.
  
  ### How Fields Score
  - Each **completed city** touching a field is worth **3 points** to that field
  - A city touching multiple fields gives **3 points to each field** separately
  
  ### Majority
  The player with the **most farmers** in a field scores that field's points. If tied, **all tied players** score the full amount.
  
  **Strategy:** Farmers are a long-term investment — place them early on large fields adjacent to many cities.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-final-scoring -->
  **Final Scoring**

  The game ends when the **last tile** is placed. Score all **incomplete** features, then score **fields**.
  
  ### End-of-Game Summary
  
  | Feature | Points |
  |---------|--------|
  | **Road** (incomplete) | 1 per tile |
  | **City** (incomplete) | 1 per tile + 1 per coat of arms |
  | **Monastery** (incomplete) | 1 + surrounding tiles |
  | **Field** | 3 per completed city touching the field |
  
  **Winning:** The player with the **highest total score** wins. In case of a tie, the player with the most meeples on the board wins (then shared victory).
