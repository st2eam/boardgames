# Diamant Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

In **Diamant**, 3–8 explorers push deeper into Tacora Cave, revealing Treasure and Trap cards. Share the treasure while you are still in the cave, then decide whether to keep exploring or return to camp and lock your gains in your Chest.

The game takes about 30 minutes. It uses five expeditions, one for each Cave Entrance.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

You are an explorer trying to bring the most treasure safely out of Tacora Cave. Keep enough courage to collect more, but return before a duplicate Trap wipes out the Gems you gathered during the expedition.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- 1 game board with 5 Cave Entrances
- 5 Cave Entrance tiles, numbered 1–5
- 35 Expedition cards: 15 Treasure cards and 15 Trap cards in the base game, plus 5 Relic cards for the optional Relic variant
- 100 Gems: 60 Rubies worth 1 point and 40 Diamonds worth 5 points
- 16 Decision cards: 8 Continue cards and 8 Return cards
- 8 Explorer figures and 8 Chests
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

1. Place the board in the middle of the table. Put the five Cave Entrance tiles by the five entrances, using the numbers on their backs to track the five rounds.
2. Shuffle the 15 Treasure cards and 15 Trap cards into a face-down Expedition deck. Leave the Relic cards out unless you are using the optional variant below.
3. Each player chooses a color and takes the matching Explorer, Chest, Continue card, and Return card. Put every Explorer on the board and every empty Chest in front of its owner.
4. Put all Rubies and Diamonds beside the board as the Gem reserve.
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: how-to-play -->
## How to Play
<!-- rule-section: reveal-1-card-choose-continue-or-return -->
### Reveal 1 card → choose Continue or Return

The game lasts five rounds. Each round repeats this loop: reveal an Expedition card, resolve it, then every Explorer still in the cave secretly chooses **Continue** or **Return** and reveals that choice simultaneously.

![A Treasure 9 is split among five explorers, leaving four Rubies on the card](/images/rules/diamant/treasure-split.svg)
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: reveal-an-expedition-card -->
### Reveal an Expedition card

Reveal the top card and add it face up to the end of the cave path. Move the Explorers who are still in the cave onto the new card.
<!-- rule-section: treasure-card -->
#### Treasure card

Take as many Rubies from the reserve as the number on the card. Divide them equally among all Explorers still in the cave. Put any remainder on that Treasure card.

The Rubies you receive during the expedition stay beside your Chest. They are not safe until you return to camp. You may exchange **5 Rubies for 1 Diamond** at any time.
<!-- rule-section: trap-card -->
#### Trap card

The first Trap of a type in a round has no immediate effect. If the same Trap appears for the second time, the expedition ends immediately: every Explorer still in the cave returns to camp and puts all Gems gathered during this expedition back into the reserve.

![The second identical Trap ends the expedition and wipes out unbanked Gems](/images/rules/diamant/duplicate-trap.svg)

Remove the second identical Trap from the game. The other revealed cards are handled during the end of the round.
<!-- rule-section: decide-together-continue-or-return -->
### Decide together: Continue or Return

Before another Expedition card is revealed, every Explorer still in the cave chooses one of their two Decision cards face down. Reveal all choices at the same time.

![Explorers secretly choose Continue or Return and reveal simultaneously](/images/rules/diamant/simultaneous-decision.svg)

- **Continue:** Keep your Explorer in the cave. Reveal another Expedition card.
- **Return:** Move your Explorer to camp. You are out of the expedition and do not take part in later card resolutions this round.
<!-- rule-section: returning-to-camp -->
#### Returning to camp

When you return:

1. Collect the Rubies left on all Treasure cards in the path. If several players return together, divide those Rubies equally among them; leave any remainder on a Treasure card.
2. Put all Rubies you gathered during the expedition into your Chest. Everything in your Chest is safe until the end of the game.
3. If everyone has returned, the round ends. Otherwise, the remaining Explorers decide again after the next card.
<!-- rule-section: relic-variant -->
## Relic Variant

The current edition includes five Relic cards valued **5, 7, 8, 10, and 12**. Use them as an optional variant:

1. Sort the Relic cards from lowest to highest. At the beginning of each round, shuffle the top Relic into the Expedition deck.
2. When a Relic is revealed, place it on the cave path. It gives no Gems; proceed directly to the next simultaneous decision.
3. If exactly one player returns, that player takes every Relic currently on the path and puts them under their Chest. If multiple players return at the same time, nobody takes the Relics.
4. Relics secured under a Chest cannot be lost. Relics left in the cave when the round ends are removed from the game. Relics that have not been revealed remain in the deck.

![A solo returner claims the Relic and leftover Rubies; simultaneous returners do not claim Relics](/images/rules/diamant/relic-solo-exit.svg)

At the end of the game, add each secured Relic's printed value to your Gem score.
<!-- rule-section: end-of-a-round -->
## End of a Round

A round ends when all players have returned to camp or when a second Trap of the same type appears.

1. Remove the Cave Entrance tile for the round that just ended.
2. Return all Rubies still on Treasure cards to the reserve.
3. If a duplicate Trap ended the expedition, remove the second copy of that Trap from the game.
4. In the Relic variant, remove every Relic still on the cave path.
5. Shuffle the revealed Expedition cards back into the Expedition deck, except cards removed above.

Start the next round with the next Cave Entrance.
<!-- rule-section: game-end-and-scoring -->
## Game End and Scoring

After the fifth round, open the Chests and count the safe treasure:

| Treasure | Value |
|---|---:|
| Ruby | 1 point |
| Diamond | 5 points |
| Secured Relic (optional variant) | Printed value |

The player with the highest total wins. Tied players share the victory.
<!-- rule-section: quick-reminders -->
## Quick Reminders

- Only Gems in your Chest are safe.
- Treasure is split among players still in the cave, not among everyone who started the round.
- A duplicate Trap matters only within the current round.
- Return choices are simultaneous, so nobody can wait to see another player's decision.
