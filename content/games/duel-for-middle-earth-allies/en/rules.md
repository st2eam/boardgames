# The Lord of the Rings: Duel for Middle-earth – Allies Rules

## Overview

**Allies** is an expansion for *The Lord of the Rings: Duel for Middle-earth*; the base game is required. Each side secretly prepares three allies, gains **Power tokens** from selected Chapter cards, then spends them to summon allies for powerful one-off effects. All base-game victory conditions remain unchanged.

*Allies* was nominated for the **2025 Golden Geek Best Expansion** award.

## Components

- 7 Fellowship Ally cards
- 7 Sauron Ally cards
- 8 Power tokens
- 2 player aids
- This rules sheet

## Setup

1. Separate the Ally cards by back: Fellowship and Sauron.
2. Each player shuffles the 7 Ally cards for their side, draws **3** at random, and keeps them face down in front of them. Do not reveal them to the opponent.
3. Set the remaining Ally cards aside; they are not used this game.
4. Each player takes their player aid and **1 Power token**, placing the token in front of them.
5. At the start of **each Chapter**, place **2 Power tokens** on the marked Chapter-card positions shown in the expansion setup diagram. They are part of that Chapter's central display.

## Taking a Power Token

![Take the Power token from a Chapter card before resolving the card](/images/rules/duel-for-middle-earth-allies/power-token.svg)

Whenever you take a Chapter card from the central play area that has a Power token on it, take that token first and place it in front of you. Then resolve the card normally: play it or discard it as allowed by the base game.

Power tokens are a personal supply. They are spent only to use Ally effects.

## Summoning an Ally

![Spend one token to reveal an Ally and resolve its entry effect; later spend two tokens to use its exit effect and set it aside](/images/rules/duel-for-middle-earth-allies/summon-ally.svg)

At any point during your turn, including after you reveal a new Chapter card in the central display, you may **summon one Ally** as an extra action. You may summon at most **one Ally per turn**.

Each Ally card has two effects:

| Effect | Cost and timing |
|---|---|
| **Entry effect** (left) | Spend **1 Power token**, flip one of your face-down Allies face up in front of you, then resolve its entry effect. |
| **Exit effect** (right) | Later, spend **2 Power tokens** to resolve the exit effect of an Ally already face up in front of you. Set that Ally aside; it cannot be used again this game. |

You must resolve an Ally's entry effect before you may use its exit effect. The player aids explain every individual Ally effect.

## Chapter Tokens

At the beginning of every Chapter, add the two marked Power tokens while preparing that Chapter's display. A token remains on its card until someone takes that card. If a player takes the final card of the Chapter, resolve its Power token before ending the Chapter and setting up the next one.

## What Does Not Change

- The normal turn still consists of taking a Chapter card or a Landmark tile.
- Summoning is an **extra** action, not a replacement for that normal choice.
- The Quest of the Ring, Races, regions, conflicts, and all victory conditions use the base-game rules.
- This expansion does not change how either side wins.

## Reference

Keep the player aid visible. It is the complete reference for the 14 Ally effects. When an effect contradicts a general base-game rule, follow the Ally effect.
