# The Lord of the Rings: Duel for Middle-earth Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

A two-player strategy duel inspired by *7 Wonders Duel*, set in Middle-earth. One player is the **Fellowship**, racing to destroy the One Ring; the other is **Sauron**, hunting Frodo and Sam while conquering the land. Over **three chapters**, draft cards and Landmark tiles to advance the Quest of the Ring, rally Races, and occupy regions. Win immediately by completing one of three paths — or control the most regions at the end of Chapter 3.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In The Lord of the Rings: Duel for Middle-earth, you command either the Fellowship or Sauron. Your goal is to trigger your side’s victory through the Ring, alliances, or control of Middle-earth.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- Central board (7 regions of Middle-earth)
- Quest of the Ring track + Fellowship / Nazgûl markers
- Chapter cards (sorted into chapters 1, 2, and 3)
- Landmark tiles
- Alliance tokens (by Race)
- Coins
- Unit pawns and Fortress pawns for each side
- Player aid
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

1. Choose sides: **Fellowship** or **Sauron**. Each takes their Unit and Fortress pawns.
2. Place the **central board** between you. Put **2 Fellowship Units on Arnor** and **2 Sauron Units on Mordor**.
3. Assemble the **Quest of the Ring** track below the board (Nazgûl token sits on top of Frodo & Sam).
4. Starting coins: Fellowship **3**, Sauron **2**. Remaining coins form the reserve.
5. Sort **Alliance tokens** by Race back; shuffle each stack and place them facedown in their notches above the board.
6. Shuffle **Landmark tiles**; reveal **3** face up; rest form a facedown stack.
7. Separate **Chapter cards** into decks 1 / 2 / 3 by the rune on the back; shuffle each.
<!-- rule-section: chapter-layout -->
### Chapter layout

At the start of each chapter, arrange that chapter’s cards in the central play area as shown in the rulebook / box side (some face up, some face down). Place any leftover three cards of that chapter facedown in the discard.

**Sauron takes the first turn.**

<!-- rule-section: how-to-play -->
## How to Play

![Only take an uncovered card](/images/rules/duel-for-middle-earth/take-card.svg)

Players alternate turns. On your turn, choose **one**:
<!-- rule-section: a-take-a-chapter-card -->
### A. Take a Chapter card

Choose an **available** card (not partly covered). Then either:

- **Play it** — pay its cost (if any), place it in your play area by color (keep effects visible), resolve its effect; **or**
- **Discard it** — send it to the discard pile without playing.

Then reveal any newly available cards.
<!-- rule-section: b-take-a-landmark-tile -->
### B. Take a Landmark tile

Choose one face-up Landmark, pay its cost, place it in your play area, put a **Fortress** on the matching region, and resolve its other effects. **Do not** reveal a replacement tile this turn.
<!-- rule-section: costs -->
### Costs

- **Coins**: pay the printed amount to the reserve when required.
- **Skills**: most cards/tiles need Skill symbols already in your area. For each missing Skill, you may pay **1 Coin** instead (no limit per turn).
- **Chaining** (from Chapter 2): if you already have a matching chaining symbol, you may play the new card **for free** (skips Skill and missing-Skill Coin costs).
- **Landmarks** also cost +1 Coin for **each Fortress you already have** on the board (first Landmark has no extra).
<!-- rule-section: end-of-a-chapter -->
### End of a chapter

When the last Chapter card of the current layout is taken, the chapter ends: set up the next chapter’s layout, refill Landmark tiles to 3 face up if possible, then continue. The player who ended the chapter does **not** start the next one (unless an effect grants an extra turn).
<!-- rule-section: card-colors-effects -->
## Card Colors & Effects

| Color | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Grey** | Skills — needed to play other cards/tiles. Slash (`/`) = choose one per turn. |
| **Yellow** | Immediate Coins |
| **Blue** | Advance on the Quest of the Ring (1 space per Ring symbol) |
| **Green** | Race symbols for alliances |
| **Red** | Place Units in one of the two regions shown |
| **Purple** | Chapter 3 maneuvers (e.g. move Units) |

**Landmark tiles:** place a Fortress in the shown region + immediate benefit (see Player Aid).
<!-- rule-section: quest-of-the-ring -->
## Quest of the Ring

Advance your piece along the track. Bonus spaces grant immediate effects when reached or passed.

Important: the Nazgûl token sits **on** Frodo & Sam. When the Fellowship moves, the Nazgûl moves with them — the gap between them only **shrinks**, never grows.
<!-- rule-section: alliances-green-cards -->
## Alliances (Green cards)

- **2 matching Race symbols:** take the top 2 Alliance tokens of that Race, reveal both, keep **1**, return the other facedown on its stack.
- **3 different Race symbols** (once per game): take the top token of each of those 3 Races, reveal all, keep **1**, return the other two.
- One symbol may count toward both conditions. Tokens are public.
<!-- rule-section: units-conflicts-presence -->
## Units, Conflicts & Presence

When you place or move Units into a region with enemy Units, resolve a **conflict**: each side removes one Unit from that region until at least one side has none left. Enemy Fortresses do **not** block placement or cause conflicts — both sides can be present.

**Presence** in a region = at least 1 Unit and/or a Fortress of yours.

Movements (e.g. Purple): move Units to adjacent regions one step at a time; resolve conflicts after each move.
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: game-end -->
## Game End

![Win immediately via the Ring, 6 races, or all 7 regions; else most regions](/images/rules/duel-for-middle-earth/win-conditions.svg)
<!-- rule-section: immediate-wins-check-after-relevant-effects -->
### Immediate wins (check after relevant effects)

1. **Quest of the Ring** — Fellowship: Frodo & Sam reach Mount Doom. Sauron: Nazgûl catch Frodo & Sam.
2. **Support of the Races** — 6 **different** Race symbols on your Green cards (the Eagle symbol on one Alliance token counts).
3. **Dominate Middle-earth** — present in **all 7** regions.
<!-- rule-section: final-scoring -->
### Final scoring

If Chapter 3 ends with no immediate win: the player present in the **most regions** wins. Tie → shared victory.
