# Dune: Imperium – Uprising Rules

![At-a-glance Chinese round flow: Agents, Reveal, Combat, round end, and the 10-VP endgame](/images/rules/dune-imperium-uprising/round-at-a-glance.svg)

## Overview

*Dune: Imperium – Uprising* is a standalone deck-building and worker-placement game. It shares the core Agent / Reveal structure of *Dune: Imperium* but adds **Spies**, **Contracts**, and **Sandworms**. This guide covers the standard competitive 1–4 player game; six-player team rules use the separate supplement.

## Game Objective

Command a Great House through the war for Arrakis. Earn **Victory Points** through conflicts, faction alliances, objectives, and card effects. Once someone reaches 10 points, finish the round and total all final points.

## Components

- Double-sided board, Leaders, player pieces, Agents, troops, Spies and Sandworms
- Imperium, Intrigue, Conflict, Contract and Objective decks; five-card Imperium Row
- Resources: spice, water and Solari; faction Influence tracks and Alliance tokens
- Player decks, combat and score markers, First Player marker, Maker hooks and control markers

## Setup

1. Use the 1–4 player side of the board. Prepare faction tracks, Alliance tokens, the Imperium Row, Conflict deck, and reserve cards.
2. Shuffle Intrigue, Contract, Objective, Imperium and Conflict decks. Reveal five Imperium cards and the first Conflict.
3. Each player picks a Leader, takes their pieces and starting deck, and sets score and combat strength to 0.
4. Set out Contracts and their rewards as directed by the board; draw five cards each. Randomly choose First Player.

## How to Play

Every round is **Start → Agent turns → Reveal turns → Combat → Makers → Recall**.

### Agent Turns

**Play 1 card → place 1 Agent → resolve the location.** A card's Agent icons determine where its Agent can go. The location gives its printed benefit and may permit troop deployment to the Conflict. Once all players have placed their available Agents, they take Reveal turns.

### Spies

![Place a Spy now; later send an Agent there even if an opponent occupies that space.](/images/rules/dune-imperium-uprising/spy-access.svg)

Some effects place one of your **Spies** on a board space. Later, when you send an Agent to a space holding your Spy, you may use that Spy: remove it and place your Agent there even if the space is occupied. If you do not use the Spy, it stays for a later turn. A Spy never itself grants the location's reward.

### Reveal Turns and Deck-building

Reveal all cards left in hand, resolve their **Reveal** effects, and add their swords to your combat strength. You may then acquire one Imperium Row or Reserve card if you can pay its cost; put it in your discard pile. Refill a taken Imperium card immediately as instructed.

### Influence, Alliances, Contracts and Intrigue

Faction Influence earns printed rewards; at 2 Influence you draw Intrigue, and at 4 you may hold that faction's Alliance for 1 point. A player who passes the current ally at 4+ takes the Alliance.

Contracts give an ongoing or immediate objective. When you complete a Contract's requirement, claim its reward as its text states. Keep Intrigue cards hidden until their stated Agent, Reveal, Combat, or Endgame timing.

## Combat and Sandworms

![A Sandworm is committed with troops and doubles the reward earned by its controller.](/images/rules/dune-imperium-uprising/sandworm-conflict.svg)

Combat strength is deployed troops plus revealed swords and legal effects. Rank players from highest to lowest and give Conflict rewards in that order. A player who committed at least one **Sandworm** doubles the Conflict reward they earn; follow the reward's own text for what can be doubled. Return committed troops and Sandworms as directed after Combat.

## Makers, Recall and Game End

Advance the Maker hooks. When a hook reaches a Sandworm icon, harvest spice from its associated Desert spaces according to the board; then reset that hook. Recall Agents, discard played and revealed cards, draw five cards, and pass First Player.

When someone has **10 or more Victory Points**, complete the round. Add points from the score track, Alliances, Objectives, and endgame effects. Break ties by spice, then Solari, then player order.

## Important Rules

- *Uprising* is a full standalone game: it does **not** require the original *Dune: Imperium*.
- Spies do not let you ignore the card icons or costs required by a space; they only bypass normal occupancy.
- A Sandworm has no combat strength by itself unless an effect says so; it modifies that player's Conflict reward.
- Card text overrides the general rules. The official six-player and solo rules have extra setup and timing rules.
