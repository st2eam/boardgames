# Dune: Imperium Rules

<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

*Dune: Imperium* combines **deck-building** with **worker placement**. Cards in your hand decide which board spaces your Agents may visit; the cards left over then fuel your Reveal turn. The game is for 1–4 players; this guide covers the competitive 3–4 player game.

<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

Lead one of the Great Houses on Arrakis. Gain **Victory Points** from faction alliances, the High Council, conflicts, and end-game cards. When a player reaches 10 points, finish that round; the highest score wins.

![At-a-glance Chinese round flow: Agents, Reveal, Combat, round end, and the 10-VP endgame](/images/rules/dune-imperium/round-at-a-glance.png)

<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- Main board, conflict deck, Imperium deck and five-card Imperium Row
- Intrigue deck, reserve cards (**Arrakis Liaison** and **The Spice Must Flow**)
- Leaders, player decks, Agents, troops, cubes, score markers and combat markers
- Spice, water, Solari and faction Influence markers

<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

1. Place the board, put an Alliance token on the 4-space of each faction track, and prepare the resources.
2. Shuffle the Imperium, Intrigue, and Conflict decks. Reveal five Imperium cards and reveal the top Conflict card.
3. Each player chooses a Leader, takes that color's pieces and a 10-card starting deck, and puts score and combat markers at 0.
4. Give each player their starting resources and draw five cards. Randomly choose the First Player.

## How to Play

Each round has **Start → Agent turns → Reveal turns → Combat → Makers → Recall**.

![An Agent card opens a board space; remaining cards are revealed afterward.](/images/rules/dune-imperium/agent-reveal-cycle.svg)

<!-- rule-section: start-of-round -->
### Start of Round

Reveal a new Conflict after the previous one is cleared. At the start of rounds 2–5, add the shown amount of spice to each Desert board space. Refresh any effects that say they happen at the start of a round.

<!-- rule-section: agent-turns -->
### Agent Turns

**Play 1 card → send 1 Agent to its matching board space → resolve that space.** In turn order, take Agent turns until every player has placed all available Agents or has none left. A card's **Agent box** shows its faction icons and any immediate effect; its icons determine legal destinations.

Most spaces accept only one Agent. Pay any printed cost, then gain the shown benefits. Board spaces can grant resources, cards, Influence, extra Agents, or troops. When you send an Agent to a **Combat** space, you may move any number of troops from your garrison into the Conflict.

<!-- rule-section: reveal-turns -->
### Reveal Turns

After placing all Agents, take one **Reveal turn**. Reveal every card still in your hand and resolve their **Reveal boxes** in any order. Their sword icons add combat strength; other effects may grant resources or let you buy cards. Then you may acquire one card from the Imperium Row, **Arrakis Liaison**, or **The Spice Must Flow**, paying its spice cost. New cards go to your discard pile.

<!-- rule-section: influence-alliances-and-intrigue -->
### Influence, Alliances and Intrigue

Moving up a faction track gives the printed rewards. At **2 Influence**, you may draw an Intrigue card; at **4**, take that faction's Alliance token if available, earning its point. If another player later reaches 4 or more Influence and passes you, they take that alliance and its point.

Intrigue cards are played only at the timing printed on them: Agent, Reveal, Combat, or Endgame. Keep their information hidden until played.

<!-- rule-section: combat-and-round-end -->
## Combat and Round End

![Swords from revealed cards add to deployed troops; highest strength takes the top reward.](/images/rules/dune-imperium/combat-strength.svg)

Add sword icons from revealed cards to the strength from troops committed to the Conflict. Players may play Combat Intrigue cards when allowed. Award Conflict rewards from left to right by descending combat strength; ties are resolved in player order. Return all troops in the Conflict to their owners' garrisons.

Move the **Maker** marker one space. If it reaches a Sandworm icon, each player with an Agent in a Desert space harvests spice there; then reset the marker. Recall all Agents, discard played and revealed cards, draw five cards, and pass the First Player marker left.

<!-- rule-section: game-end-and-scoring -->
## Game End and Scoring

When a player reaches **10 Victory Points** during a round, complete the round. Add points from the score track, faction alliances, **The Spice Must Flow**, and revealed end-game Intrigue cards. Break ties by the most spice, then the most Solari, then turn order.

<!-- rule-section: important-rules -->
## Important Rules

- You must play a card to send an Agent; you cannot occupy a space solely because you have an Agent available.
- A card effect can override a normal rule. Resolve its text as specifically as possible.
- Resources and cards are public unless a rule says otherwise; decks, hands, and unplayed Intrigue cards are hidden.
- The official solo and two-player modes add House Hagal rules; use their dedicated rules supplement.
