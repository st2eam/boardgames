# Durian Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

Everyone can see the other clerks’ fruit cards but not their own. Keep taking fruit orders without asking for more fruit than the shop has — and ring the bell when you think the last order went too far.

Durian supports 2–7 players and takes about 20 minutes.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Durian, you are a fruit-shop clerk who can see everyone’s inventory except your own. Your goal is to judge when orders have gone too far, ring the bell at the right time, and finish with the least manager anger.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- 28 Fruit cards
- 3 Mischievous Gorilla cards
- 1 Order board
- 7 card stands
- 7 Angry Manager chips
- 1 handbell
<!-- rule-section: set-up -->
## Set Up

![Every clerk has one inventory card; you can see all other cards but never your own card](/images/rules/durian/hidden-inventory.svg)

1. Give each player a stand and one card from the shuffled deck. Put it in the stand facing **away** from its owner: everyone else can see it, but the owner cannot.
2. Put the Order board in the middle, with the bell beside it. The space marked with a check is the side of each Fruit card that counts as an order.
3. Stack the Angry Manager chips in number order, with the lowest value available first. Put the remaining cards face down as the draw deck.

Use the two-player setup from the rulebook when playing with two players: it uses one extra visible inventory card.

## Your Turn: Take an Order or Ring the Bell
<!-- rule-section: take-an-order -->
### Take an order

![Draw one card, then choose exactly one of its two fruit sides as the new order; the other side does not count](/images/rules/durian/take-order.svg)

Draw the top card.

- **Fruit card:** It shows two fruit groups. Choose **one** side as the new order and slide that side beneath the check on the Order board. The fruit on the other side is ignored.
- **Gorilla card:** Put it next to an earlier Fruit order, then flip that Fruit card around. The order now uses its other side instead.

After taking an order, the next player takes a turn. You never secretly look at your own inventory card.
<!-- rule-section: ring-the-bell -->
### Ring the bell

Anyone may ring the bell when they think the current orders are more than the fruit in stock. Stop immediately and check.

![Ring the bell to compare all ordered fruit against total stock: too many orders blames the last order-taker; enough stock blames the bell-ringer](/images/rules/durian/ring-bell.svg)

1. Reveal all inventory cards and count the fruit in stock.
2. Count the fruit that is currently ordered (the sides under the check).
3. Apply any Gorilla effects that are in the inventory before comparing the totals.

| Check result | Who takes the next Angry Manager chip? |
|---|---|
| Orders exceed stock | The player who took the most recent order. |
| Stock covers every order | The player who rang the bell. |

After the check, collect the cards, shuffle, and deal fresh hidden inventory cards. Continue from the next turn.
<!-- rule-section: the-three-gorilla-siblings -->
## The Three Gorilla Siblings

When a Gorilla card appears as an **order**, it flips an earlier Fruit order as described above. When a Gorilla card is sitting in the hidden inventory, reveal it during a bell check and use its sibling effect:

| Gorilla | During the check |
|---|---|
| **Mitsuhiko** | Ignore every order side that shows **3 fruits**. |
| **Nana** | Ignore every **banana** order. |
| **Moo** | No special effect. |
<!-- rule-section: game-end -->
## Game End

Angry Manager chips are worth increasing amounts. When a player’s total Angry Manager value reaches **7 or more**, the game ends immediately. The player with the **lowest** total anger wins.
<!-- rule-section: quick-reminders -->
## Quick Reminders

- You may ring the bell even when it is not your turn.
- The owner of an inventory card must not see it until a bell check reveals it.
- Only the chosen side of each Fruit order counts; an order flipped by a Gorilla immediately uses its new side.

  Reveal every inventory card. First apply any Gorilla effects in the inventory. Then compare total ordered fruit with total stock: if orders are higher, the most recent order-taker gets angry; otherwise the bell-ringer gets angry.

  ![Ring the bell to compare all ordered fruit against total stock: too many orders blames the last order-taker; enough stock blames the bell-ringer](/images/rules/durian/ring-bell.svg)

  Related topics:

  Related topics: 
  - [Nobody has 7 anger](#flow-turn)
  - [Someone reached 7](#flow-winner)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-winner -->
  **The Calmest Clerk Wins**

  When a player reaches 7 or more anger, stop immediately. Compare Angry Manager values: the lowest total wins.
