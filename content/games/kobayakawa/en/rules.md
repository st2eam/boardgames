# Kobayakawa Rules

## Overview

In **Kobayakawa**, you hold one card and decide whether it is strong enough to fight for the pot. The central Kobayakawa card is added to the **lowest** fighting hand, making a weak card unexpectedly dangerous.

## Game Objective

After five rounds, have the most coins.

## Components

- 15 number cards, coin/medal chips, and a Kobayakawa space

## Setup

Give each player the starting coins. Shuffle the 15 cards, deal one face-down card to each player, and place one face-up card in the centre as **Kobayakawa**. Make a draw deck from the rest.

## How to Play

### Draw or replace once → decide to fight → reveal and pay the winner

Starting with the first player, each player chooses **one** action: draw the top card, choose one of their two cards to keep and discard the other face up; or replace the centre Kobayakawa card with the top deck card, taking the old Kobayakawa card into hand. You finish with one hand card.

![Choose once: redraw your hand or replace the central Kobayakawa card](/images/rules/kobayakawa/choice.svg)

Then, in turn order, either **retreat** or **fight**. A fighter puts one coin into the pot. At least two players must fight for a showdown; otherwise follow the round reset rules.

![Kobayakawa is added only to the lowest fighting hand](/images/rules/kobayakawa/showdown.svg)

### Showdown

Fighters reveal their hands. Find the lowest hand value among fighters and add the Kobayakawa card's value to that hand only. Highest resulting value wins the pot. If tied, the player who entered the fight later wins. The winner takes all coins bet this round.

## End of Round and Game

Discard used cards, deal fresh hands, and begin the next round with the next starting player. Play five rounds, then the player with the most coins wins.

## Special Rules

- Watching what opponents discard or whether they replace Kobayakawa is valuable information, but bluffing is the point.
- Only the lowest fighting hand receives Kobayakawa's bonus; it can overturn an apparently stronger card.
