# Las Vegas Royale Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

**Las Vegas Royale** (Rüdiger Dorn, alea / Ravensburger, 2019) is the deluxe reprint of *Las Vegas* (2012). Over **three rounds**, you roll dice into an arena and commit one number to one of six casinos. The player with the most money after three rounds wins.

This box includes a **basic game** (dice, chips, Biggy) and the **main game** (add random casino tiles to casinos 1–3). Play the main game unless you are teaching first-timers.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Las Vegas Royale, you are a gambler choosing where to commit whole groups of dice. Your goal is simple: after three rounds, have more cash than every rival.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- 1 dice arena + 1 casino ring (six casinos, numbered **1–6**)
- **90 money cards**: 11× $30,000; 11× $40,000; 13× $50,000; 15× $60,000; 13× $70,000; 11× $80,000; 9× $90,000; 7× $100,000
- **35 small dice** (7 per player color) and **5 Biggy dice** (1 per color; counts as **two** small dice)
- **2 black extra dice** and **9 blank gray dice** (tiles only)
- **20 chips** (leftover chips = **$10,000** each at game end)
- **8 double-sided casino tiles** (16 effects)
- Counters and tokens for the tiles
<!-- rule-section: setup-every-round -->
## Setup (every round)

1. Place the **dice arena** in the center; assemble the **casino ring** around it.
2. Shuffle the money cards. Reveal **6 pairs** (12 cards). Put the pair with the **highest total** next to casino **6**, the next-highest next to **5**, and so on down to casino **1**. If two pairs tie on total, the pair with the **higher single card** goes to the **higher-numbered** casino. Offset the two cards so both values stay visible.
3. Each player takes **7 small dice + 1 Biggy** in one color, plus **2 chips**.
4. Unused chips stay in a supply for later rounds.
<!-- rule-section: main-game-extras -->
### Main game extras

At the start of **each** round:

1. Shuffle the 8 tiles (flip them so a random side is up). Place **3 random tiles** next to casinos **1, 2, and 3**. Casinos **4–6** have no tiles.
2. Set aside **24 money cards** face down for rounds 2 and 3 (6 pairs × 2 remaining rounds). Remaining cards form a face-up **bank**.
3. Keep the black dice, gray dice, and tokens nearby — you need them only when the matching tile is in play.

<!-- rule-section: how-to-play -->
## How to Play

![Choose 3: every 3 goes to casino 3](/images/rules/las-vegas-royale/place-dice.svg)

Three rounds. Oldest player starts round 1; later rounds start with whoever won the **higher** card at casino **6** (or the next lower casino if nobody won there). Play clockwise.
<!-- rule-section: on-your-turn -->
### On Your Turn

1. Roll **all remaining dice** into the arena (8 at first, then fewer).
2. Optionally **pay 1 chip** to **pass**: return all rolled dice to your supply and skip placing. You keep those dice for later turns.
3. Otherwise choose **exactly one number** and place **every die showing that number** on the matching casino — including the Biggy if it shows that number. No partial placement.
4. **Main game:** placing any dice on casinos **1, 2, or 3** **activates** that casino’s tile.

If you have no dice left, you skip. Near the end, one player may take several turns in a row.

**Example:** Ben rolls four 3s and a 5. He must place all four 3s on casino **3**, or the single 5 on casino **5**.
<!-- rule-section: the-biggy -->
### The Biggy

The larger die counts as **two** small dice for majority, ties, High Five, and similar checks. On **Knockout?** it still occupies only **one** of the two bar slots.
<!-- rule-section: chips -->
### Chips

- After a roll you dislike, pay **1 chip** to sit out (dice go back to your supply).
- Unused chips stay in front of you between rounds.
- Each leftover chip is worth **$10,000** at game end. The bank never pays chips as change.
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: payout -->
## Payout

![Ties leave empty-handed; most dice take the higher bill, second takes the other](/images/rules/las-vegas-royale/payout.svg)

When the last player places their last die:
<!-- rule-section: step-1-eliminate-ties -->
### Step 1 — Eliminate ties

At each casino, if two or more players have the **same dice count** (Biggy = 2), **every tied player** takes **all** their dice back and wins **nothing** there.

**Example A:** Anna 5, Ben 3, Carla Biggy+1 (=3), Denny 1 → Ben and Carla both leave.

**Example B:** Anna Biggy (=2), Carla 2, Ben 1, Denny 1 → **all four** leave.
<!-- rule-section: step-2-pay-each-casino -->
### Step 2 — Pay each casino

Among remaining dice, **most dice** takes the **higher-value** card; **second-most** takes the other card. Everyone else gets nothing. Leftover cards go **face down under** the money pile. Hide won money until the end.
<!-- rule-section: next-round -->
## Next Round

1. Take all dice back.
2. Each player gains **2 more chips** (keep leftovers).
3. Deal 6 new money pairs as in setup.
4. **Main game:** replace the three tiles (same tile/side may reappear unless you house-rule otherwise).
5. Start player = winner of casino 6’s higher card (or next casino down).
<!-- rule-section: game-end -->
## Game End

After **three rounds**, total your money cards plus **$10,000 per leftover chip**. Highest total wins.

**Tiebreaker:** most money cards **and** chips combined. Still tied → shared victory.
<!-- rule-section: 2-player-variant -->
## 2-Player Variant

At the start of each round, roll **all 8 dice** of an unused color and place them on matching casinos. They stay until payout and count as an **imaginary player**. Money that “player” would win goes **under the pile**.

Gray blank dice from tiles are a **second**, separate imaginary player.
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: casino-tiles-main-game -->
## Casino Tiles (main game)

Whenever you place dice on casinos 1–3, resolve that tile. Variant: agree to use fewer or more tiles (max **6**, one per casino).
<!-- rule-section: lucky-punch-a1 -->
### Lucky Punch (A1)

Put the 3 hexagonal tokens on the tile. Secretly hold **1–3** in your right fist. The player to your left guesses the count. **Wrong guess:** take 2 chips, **or** $30,000, **or** $40,000. **Correct:** nothing. Return all 3 tokens.
<!-- rule-section: jackpot-a2 -->
### Jackpot (A2)

Put the Jackpot counter on **$30,000**; ready the 2 black dice. Roll both: **7 or doubles** → take the current amount from the bank and reset the counter to $30,000. Otherwise advance the counter (max **$80,000**).
<!-- rule-section: prime-time-b1 -->
### Prime Time (B1)

Ready the 2 black dice. **Before payout**, if you are about to win this casino’s **higher** card, roll both. You may place **0, 1, or both** on matching casinos (even on doubles). They count as yours for the upcoming payout.
<!-- rule-section: fifty-fifty-b2 -->
### Fifty Fifty (B2)

Ready the 2 black dice; counter on the first space. Roll both and move the counter one space. Take the reward under the counter (**$0–$60,000**) **or** gamble: bet **higher or lower**, roll again, advance. Win → take or gamble again. Lose, or roll the **same total twice in a row** → nothing. Reset the counter when done.
<!-- rule-section: high-five-c1 -->
### High Five (C1)

Place the High Five token on the tile. When you place your **fifth** die here (or **3 + Biggy**), take the token. During payout, cash it for **$100,000** from the bank.
<!-- rule-section: bad-luck-c2 -->
### Bad Luck (C2)

**Before** removing ties (and counting players with **zero** dice here), each player with the **fewest** dice on this casino pays **$50,000** to the bank. Short on cash? Pay all you have (chips count as $10,000). The bank can make change in money, never chips.
<!-- rule-section: pay-day-d1 -->
### Pay Day (D1)

Count casinos (**not tiles**) that contain at least one die of your color, including this one. × $10,000. **$10,000 / $20,000** → 1 / 2 chips. **$30,000–$60,000** → take that money from the bank.
<!-- rule-section: power-play-d2 -->
### Power Play (D2)

After placing, if **you alone** have more dice here than anyone else, take the token. On your **next** turn, if you still have it, you may skip rolling: set **one** remaining die to any number, place it, and resolve that casino. If nobody has a clear majority here, return the token to the tile.
<!-- rule-section: no-entry-e1 -->
### No Entry! (E1)

Place the No Entry token on the tile and the round counter on the first track space. Choose **another** casino and put the token on its die art — that casino is **closed** (no dice in or out; no tile activations). Advance the counter and take the new space’s reward (empty spaces pay nothing). Later activations **may** move the token; if you don’t, you also skip the counter and the reward.
<!-- rule-section: knockout-e2 -->
### Knockout? (E2)

Each opponent puts **one remaining die** into this tile’s bar (unless they already have **2** there). You retrieve **your** dice from the bar. Bar dice do not count at payout. The Biggy counts as **one** of the two bar slots.
<!-- rule-section: block-it-f1 -->
### Block It! (F1)

Place the 9 blank dice on the tile’s clusters (two singles, two pairs, one triple). Choose one cluster, take those blanks, and put them on **one** casino. Blanks belong to an **imaginary player** for the rest of the round and payout.
<!-- rule-section: handicap-f2 -->
### Handicap (F2)

Start with 1 blank in casinos 1–3 and 2 blanks in 4–6. You **may** move one blank from any casino onto an empty picture on this tile and take the shown reward (chip, $30,000, or: set one remaining die and place it **without** activating, **or** return one of your casino dice to your supply). Blanks in casinos count as an imaginary player. You need not take a blank, but you only score a reward if you do.
<!-- rule-section: black-box-g1 -->
### Black Box (G1)

Six face-up tokens on the tile. **During payout**, the player to the **left** of this casino’s winner splits the 6 tokens into two face-down piles (**3+3**, **4+2**, or **5+1**). The winner picks one pile: 2 chips and/or $40,000 / $60,000 / $80,000 / $100,000.
<!-- rule-section: double-down-g2 -->
### Double Down (G2)

You **may** move any number of your dice from this casino onto the tile (just placed or earlier). At payout this tile pays like a casino: winner **$60,000**, runner-up **$30,000** from the bank. Other effects cannot add or remove dice here.
<!-- rule-section: nice-dice-h1 -->
### Nice Dice (H1)

You **may** park a die you just rolled, or one you just placed here, on the matching number space. If that space is occupied, send the old die to its matching casino (**does not** activate that casino). At payout, remove your dice from the tile and take the printed rewards (1–2 chips or $30,000–$60,000).
<!-- rule-section: my-choice-h2 -->
### My Choice (H2)

Ready the 2 black dice. Roll both and pick **one** result:

- Gain 1 chip, 2 chips, or $30,000
- Activate **any other** tile as if you had just placed dice there (it may do nothing)
- Set one remaining die to any number and place it **without** activating, **or** return one of your casino dice
- Place one remaining die on the gold-framed gray space (replace any occupant). At payout, that die scores **$60,000** from the bank

  **Tiles D–E**

  **No Entry!:** close another casino (no dice / no activations). Advance track for a reward. Later activations may move the token.
  
  **Knockout?:** each foe puts 1 remaining die in the bar (max 2). You retrieve yours. Bar dice skip payout. Biggy = 1 slot.
