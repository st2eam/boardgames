# Magical Athlete Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

**Magical Athlete** is a fast, chaotic racing game. Draft four absurd racers, then send one into each of four races. Every racer breaks the usual roll-and-move expectations with a unique power, so the key is choosing the right racer for the coming track and saving the rest for later.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

Win the most points over four races. A race stops as soon as its second racer finishes; the top two take that race's gold and silver point chips. After race four, add all your chips. The highest total wins.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- Double-sided racetrack: **Mild Mile** and **Wild Wilds**
- 36 racer cards and 36 matching racer tokens
- 6 dice
- Four gold first-place chips, four silver second-place chips, and bronze point chips
- Rulebook
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

1. Put the **Mild Mile** side of the track in the centre. Separate the point chips; stack gold trophy and silver rosette chips with the largest value at the bottom. Shuffle racer cards and place racer tokens nearby.
2. Give every player one die. The standard game uses 3–6 players; use the special double-racer setup for 2 players.
3. Draft a team of four racers. Reveal **twice the player count** of racer cards. Roll to determine first pick, then snake draft: pick clockwise, reverse after the last player, and continue until every player has two. Repeat another snake draft beginning with the player left of the first drafter so everyone has four racers.
4. For an easier first game, deal each player four random racers instead. Keep racers secret in hand.

## How to Play
<!-- rule-section: before-each-race-choose-1-unused-racer-reveal-together-put-all-racers-at-start -->
### Before each race: choose 1 unused racer → reveal together → put all racers at Start

Before the first race, roll to choose the first player. Every player secretly chooses one unused racer, then reveals simultaneously and places its matching token on **Start**. Each racer may race only once. Later races are worth more, so preserve useful racers deliberately.

![Each player commits one unused racer before the race begins](/images/rules/magical-athlete/choose-racer.svg)
<!-- rule-section: your-turn-roll-1-die-move-that-many-spaces-resolve-powers -->
### Your turn: roll 1 die → move that many spaces → resolve powers

Roll your die and move your racer that many spaces: this is its **main move**. Then resolve all relevant racer powers. Some powers are optional only when they say **can**; all other powers are mandatory. Continue clockwise.

Powers can be passive, happen at a stated time, or trigger from movement, rolls, sharing a space, passing, or other effects. If several powers trigger at once, resolve them in this order: **current player's racer**, then other players clockwise, then track-space effects. Complete a move before resolving an effect triggered by that move unless a card states otherwise.

![A main move can trigger racers and a track effect in a fixed priority order](/images/rules/magical-athlete/move-and-triggers.svg)
<!-- rule-section: track-effects-and-important-terms -->
### Track effects and important terms

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Start** | Start counts as a space; beyond the finish line does not. |
| **Pass** | Start a move behind a racer and end it ahead of that racer. |
| **Share a space** | Racers must be stopped on the same space; briefly crossing through does not count. |
| **Trip** | Finish the current move, lay the token down, then skip the next **main move**. Powers may still work and may still move the racer. |
| **Warp** | Place the racer on the target space, but it does not count as moving, passing, or triggering movement effects. |
| **Star / arrow (Wild Wilds)** | Stop on a star: take a bronze 1-point chip. Stop on an arrow: make the indicated separate move. Stop on TRIP: trip. |

Moving 0 does not count as moving. A reroll replaces only the most recent result, as if that prior number had never been rolled. A racer normally stops using its powers once it finishes or otherwise leaves the race.
<!-- rule-section: end-a-race -->
### End a race

As soon as the **second** racer crosses the finish line, end the race immediately. First place takes the top gold chip; second takes the top silver chip. Remove all racers and cards used in that race from the game.

Everyone simultaneously reveals their next racer and puts it at Start. The player whose racer was farthest behind in the previous race (or was eliminated first) becomes the first player. Flip the track to its other side: the standard sequence is **Mild, Wild, Mild, Wild**. Decide any alternative schedule before play begins.
<!-- rule-section: game-end-and-scoring -->
## Game End and Scoring

After the fourth race, add all gold, silver, and bronze point chips. The player with the most points wins. A tie remains a tie—celebrate together.
<!-- rule-section: special-rules -->
## Special Rules

- If an interaction creates an infinite loop, perform the loop once in its natural order, then stop it. If no racer can finish because of a loop, the race ends and no one receives remaining points.
- When a power says a racer is ahead, behind, in last place, or in the lead, compare where racers are **stopped**. Racers sharing a space are neither ahead nor behind each other.
- In the **2-player variant**, each player drafts eight racers and chooses two per race; take both of your racers' turns in either order. A 3-player double-racer variant uses the same idea.
- The card text and full racer reference settle individual powers; if a rare dispute remains, the official rules suggest a roll-off.
