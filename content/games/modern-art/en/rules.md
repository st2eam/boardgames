# Modern Art Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

Modern Art is an auction game designed by Reiner Knizia. Players take on the role of art dealers buying and selling paintings from five different artists over four rounds. Each round, paintings are auctioned off using different auction types. At the end of each round, paintings are sold to the bank at market value. The player with the most money at the end wins.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Modern Art, you are an art dealer trading the work of five painters. Your goal is to buy and sell at the right moments, then finish four seasons with more money than anyone else.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- 70 painting cards (5 artists)
- Money tokens (in various denominations)
- 1 market value board
- 12 artist value tiles
- 5 player screens
<!-- rule-section: artists-card-distribution -->
## Artists & Card Distribution

| Artist | Cards |
|--------|-------|
| Manuel Carvalho (Lite Metal) | 12 |
| Sigrid Thaler (Yoko) | 13 |
| Daniel Melim (Christin P.) | 14 |
| Ramon Martins (Karl Gitter) | 15 |
| Rafael Silveira (Krypto) | 16 |

*(Artist names vary by edition; card counts remain the same.)*
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

1. Each player receives **100** in starting money (hidden behind their screen).
2. Place the market value board in the center.
3. Shuffle all 70 painting cards and deal starting hands based on player count:
   - **3 players**: 10 cards each
   - **4 players**: 9 cards each
   - **5 players**: 8 cards each
4. Place remaining cards face down as the draw pile.
5. The youngest player goes first as the Auctioneer.
<!-- rule-section: how-to-play -->
## How to Play

On your turn, play one painting card from your hand and conduct the auction indicated on the card.
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: auction-types -->
## Auction Types

![The icon picks open, once-around, sealed, or fixed](/images/rules/modern-art/auction-types.svg)
<!-- rule-section: open-auction -->
### Open Auction
Starting from the player to the left of the Auctioneer, players bid freely. Each bid must exceed the previous. The highest bidder pays the Auctioneer and takes the painting. If no one bids, the Auctioneer gets it for free.
<!-- rule-section: once-around -->
### Once Around
Starting left of the Auctioneer, each player gets exactly one chance to bid or pass. The Auctioneer bids last. Highest bid wins.
<!-- rule-section: sealed-auction -->
### Sealed Auction
All players (including the Auctioneer) secretly hold money in their fist. Reveal simultaneously. Highest bidder wins. Ties go to the player closest to the Auctioneer's left.
<!-- rule-section: fixed-price -->
### Fixed Price
The Auctioneer sets a price. Starting left, each player may buy at that price or pass. If no one buys, the Auctioneer **must** buy it at that price from the bank.
<!-- rule-section: double-auction -->
### Double Auction
The Auctioneer plays the card and may pair it with a second painting **from the same artist** in their hand. If they cannot or choose not to, the next player clockwise may contribute a matching card. The pair is auctioned as one lot using Open Auction rules. The player who contributed the second card splits the payment with the Auctioneer.
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: rounds-scoring -->
## Rounds & Scoring

![Fifth painting of an artist ends the round; sold works pay 30 / 20 / 10](/images/rules/modern-art/round-scoring.svg)

A round ends immediately when the **5th painting** of any single artist is played. That 5th card is **not** auctioned — it is discarded.
<!-- rule-section: market-value -->
### Market Value

After each round, rank the top 3 artists by number of paintings sold (auctioned) that round:
- **1st place**: 30,000 per painting
- **2nd place**: 20,000 per painting
- **3rd place**: 10,000 per painting
- **4th–5th place**: 0 (worthless)

Place the corresponding value tile (30/20/10) on the market value board in that artist's column for the current round. Ties are broken by the order of artists on the board.
<!-- rule-section: cumulative-value -->
### Cumulative Value

An artist's market value is the **sum of all value tiles** in their column across all rounds played so far. So an artist who placed 1st in round 1 (30) and 2nd in round 2 (20) would be worth 50 per painting in round 2.
<!-- rule-section: selling-to-the-bank -->
### Selling to the Bank

After determining values, each player sells all paintings they purchased this round to the bank at their current market value. Unsold paintings from your hand are worthless.
<!-- rule-section: new-round -->
### New Round

Deal additional cards from the draw pile:
- **Rounds 2 & 3** — 3 players: 6 cards; 4 players: 4 cards; 5 players: 3 cards
- **Round 4** — No new cards dealt
<!-- rule-section: end-game -->
## End Game

After 4 rounds, the player with the **most total money** (including all earnings from painting sales) wins.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-turn -->
  **Your Turn**

  As **Auctioneer**, play **one painting card** from your hand face up. Conduct the auction type shown on that card.
  
  **After the auction:**
  - Winner pays the Auctioneer and takes the painting
  - If no one bids (where allowed), the Auctioneer may keep it for free
  - Play passes clockwise — the buyer becomes the next Auctioneer
  
  **Auction types on cards:** Open, Once Around, Sealed, Fixed Price, or Double (see individual rules).

- <!-- rule-item: flow-open-auction -->
  **Open Auction**

  Starting with the player to the **left of the Auctioneer**, players bid freely in turn order.
  
  **Rules:**
  - Each bid must **exceed** the previous bid
  - Players may **pass** — once you pass, you cannot re-enter
  - Continue until only one bidder remains
  
  **Result:**
  - **Highest bidder** pays the Auctioneer and takes the painting
  - If **no one bids**, the Auctioneer keeps the painting **for free**

- <!-- rule-item: flow-once-around -->
  **Once Around Auction**

  Starting left of the Auctioneer, each player gets **exactly one** chance to bid or pass, going clockwise.
  
  **Rules:**
  - Each bid must exceed the current high bid
  - The **Auctioneer bids last**
  - After everyone has acted once, bidding ends
  
  **Result:**
  - **Highest bid** wins and pays the Auctioneer
  - If all pass, the Auctioneer keeps the painting for free

- <!-- rule-item: flow-sealed-auction -->
  **Sealed Auction**

  All players (including the Auctioneer) secretly hold money in their closed fist.
  
  **Rules:**
  1. Everyone chooses a bid amount (0 is allowed)
  2. Reveal simultaneously on count
  3. **Highest bid** wins
  
  **Tiebreaker:** The tied player **closest to the Auctioneer's left** wins.
  
  **Payment:** Winner pays the Auctioneer. If the Auctioneer wins, they keep the painting and pay nothing.

  Related topics:

- <!-- rule-item: flow-fixed-price -->
  **Fixed Price**

  The Auctioneer **names a price** for the painting.
  
  **Rules:**
  1. Starting left of the Auctioneer, each player may **buy at that price** or **pass**
  2. The **first player to buy** (clockwise) takes the painting and pays the Auctioneer
  3. If **everyone passes**, the Auctioneer **must buy** the painting from the bank at that price
  
  **Tip:** Set a price low enough to attract buyers, or high enough that you want to keep it yourself.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-double-auction -->
  **Double Auction**

  The Auctioneer plays a card and may pair it with a **second painting from the same artist** in their hand.
  
  **Contributing the second card:**
  1. Auctioneer may add a matching-artist card from hand
  2. If they cannot or choose not to, the next player clockwise may contribute one
  3. Continue clockwise until someone adds a second card, or all pass
  
  **Auction:** The pair is sold as **one lot** using the auction type on the **second card**.
  
  **Payment split:** The player who contributed the second card **splits the payment** with the Auctioneer.

  - **Round 4:** No new cards dealt
  
  ### End Game
  After **4 rounds**, the player with the **most total money** wins.

  After each round, sell all paintings you **purchased this round** to the bank at current market value. Paintings still in your hand are **worthless**.
  
  ### New Round Cards
  - **Rounds 2 & 3:** 3p: 6 cards; 4p: 4 cards; 5p: 3 cards
  - **Round 4:** No new cards dealt
  
  ### End Game
  After **4 rounds**, the player with the **most total money** wins.

## Quick reference
<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->

Jump directly to the rule topic you need.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-setup -->
  **Setup**

  1. Each player receives **100** in starting money (hidden behind their screen).
  2. Place the **market value board** in the center with **5 artist columns**.
  3. Shuffle all **70 painting cards** (5 artists) and deal starting hands:
     - **3 players:** 10 cards each
     - **4 players:** 9 cards each
     - **5 players:** 8 cards each
  4. Remaining cards form the face-down draw pile.
  5. The youngest player is the first **Auctioneer**.
  
  **Game length:** **4 rounds**. Most money after round 4 wins.

  - [Your Turn](#flow-turn)
  - [Auction Types](#flow-open-auction)
  - [Round End](#flow-round-end)
  - [Scoring](#flow-scoring)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-turn -->
  **Your Turn**

  As **Auctioneer**, play **one painting card** from your hand face up. Conduct the auction type shown on that card.
  
  **After the auction:**
  - Winner pays the Auctioneer and takes the painting
  - If no one bids (where allowed), the Auctioneer may keep it for free
  - Play passes clockwise — the buyer becomes the next Auctioneer
  
  **Auction types on cards:** Open, Once Around, Sealed, Fixed Price, or Double (see individual rules).

- <!-- rule-item: flow-open-auction -->
  **Open Auction**

  Starting with the player to the **left of the Auctioneer**, players bid freely in turn order.
  
  **Rules:**
  - Each bid must **exceed** the previous bid
  - Players may **pass** — once you pass, you cannot re-enter
  - Continue until only one bidder remains
  
  **Result:**
  - **Highest bidder** pays the Auctioneer and takes the painting
  - If **no one bids**, the Auctioneer keeps the painting **for free**

- <!-- rule-item: flow-once-around -->
  **Once Around Auction**

  Starting left of the Auctioneer, each player gets **exactly one** chance to bid or pass, going clockwise.
  
  **Rules:**
  - Each bid must exceed the current high bid
  - The **Auctioneer bids last**
  - After everyone has acted once, bidding ends
  
  **Result:**
  - **Highest bid** wins and pays the Auctioneer
  - If all pass, the Auctioneer keeps the painting for free

- <!-- rule-item: flow-sealed-auction -->
  **Sealed Auction**

  All players (including the Auctioneer) secretly hold money in their closed fist.
  
  **Rules:**
  1. Everyone chooses a bid amount (0 is allowed)
  2. Reveal simultaneously on count
  3. **Highest bid** wins
  
  **Tiebreaker:** The tied player **closest to the Auctioneer's left** wins.
  
  **Payment:** Winner pays the Auctioneer. If the Auctioneer wins, they keep the painting and pay nothing.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-fixed-price -->
  **Fixed Price**

  The Auctioneer **names a price** for the painting.
  
  **Rules:**
  1. Starting left of the Auctioneer, each player may **buy at that price** or **pass**
  2. The **first player to buy** (clockwise) takes the painting and pays the Auctioneer
  3. If **everyone passes**, the Auctioneer **must buy** the painting from the bank at that price
  
  **Tip:** Set a price low enough to attract buyers, or high enough that you want to keep it yourself.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-double-auction -->
  **Double Auction**

  The Auctioneer plays a card and may pair it with a **second painting from the same artist** in their hand.
  
  **Contributing the second card:**
  1. Auctioneer may add a matching-artist card from hand
  2. If they cannot or choose not to, the next player clockwise may contribute one
  3. Continue clockwise until someone adds a second card, or all pass
  
  **Auction:** The pair is sold as **one lot** using the auction type on the **second card**.
  
  **Payment split:** The player who contributed the second card **splits the payment** with the Auctioneer.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-round-end -->
  **Round End**

  A round ends **immediately** when the **5th painting of any single artist** is played.
  
  **Important:** That 5th card is **NOT auctioned** — it is **discarded**.
  
  ### Rank Artists
  Count paintings **sold at auction** this round for each artist. Rank the top 3:
  
  | Rank | Value per Painting |
  |------|--------------------|
  | **1st** | 30 |
  | **2nd** | 20 |
  | **3rd** | 10 |
  | **4th–5th** | 0 (worthless) |
  
  Place the corresponding value tile (30/20/10) on the market board. **Ties** are broken by artist order on the board.
  
  Then proceed to **Scoring** and deal new cards for the next round.

  Related topics: 
  - [Scoring](#flow-scoring)
  - [Your Turn](#flow-turn)
  - [Setup](#flow-setup)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-scoring -->
  **Scoring**

  ### Cumulative Artist Value
  An artist's value is the **sum of all value tiles** in their column across rounds played so far.
  
  *Example:* 1st in round 1 (30) + 2nd in round 2 (20) = **50 per painting** in round 2.
  
  ### Selling to the Bank
  After each round, sell all paintings you **purchased this round** to the bank at current market value. Paintings still in your hand are **worthless**.
  
  ### New Round Cards
  - **Rounds 2 & 3:** 3p: 6 cards; 4p: 4 cards; 5p: 3 cards
  - **Round 4:** No new cards dealt
  
  ### End Game
  After **4 rounds**, the player with the **most total money** wins.
