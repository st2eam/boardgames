# Palm Island Rules

## Overview

A pocket-sized **deck-transforming** game played in the palm of your hand — no table required. With a **17-card** deck over **8 rounds**, store resources, rotate and flip cards to upgrade your village, then score victory stars. Play **solo**, or use the included modes for **competitive** / **cooperative** 2-player games (more players with extra copies).

## Components

- 2 player decks (17 cards each: red / blue stripe)
- Feat cards, cooperative cards, competitive cards
- Reference card
- Rulebook

Each card has **4 orientations** (front/back × top/bottom half). Only the **face-up top section** is the **active area**.

## Solo Setup (Basic)

1. Take the red or blue **core deck** of 17. Set feat / co-op / competitive cards aside.
2. Orient every card so the white **sun (start marker)** is on the front, **top-left**.
3. Set the **round tracker** aside; shuffle the other 16 without changing orientation.
4. Put the round tracker as the **last** card of the deck.
5. Before starting you may note card order. During play you may look at only the **top 3** cards.

## How to Play

![Act on one of the top two cards using stored resources, or discard the top](/images/rules/palm-island/two-card.svg)

Each turn, do **one**:

1. **Act** on **one of the top 2** cards (see Actions), **or**
2. **Discard** the top card — move it to the back unchanged.

If you act on the **second** card, the first stays in place. You may never discard the second card. If you cannot act on either top card, you **must** discard the first.

### Actions (on the active area)

Pay the cost shown next to the action (if any), then:

| Action | What you do |
|--------|-------------|
| **Store** | Rotate **90° clockwise**, put at back so stored **resources** stick out of the side |
| **Rotate** | Rotate **180°** (same face), put at back — upgrades the card |
| **Flip** | Flip front↔back (no 180 rotate), put at back — upgrades the card |

### Paying costs

- Use **stored** resource cards: rotate them **90° counterclockwise** in place (do not reorder the deck).
- Slash (`/`) means **or**. Extra resources on a spent card are lost.
- Free actions need no payment.
- While deciding, you may peek at the **back** of either top-2 card, then restore orientation/order.

### Resource limit

At most **4** cards may be stored (sticking out) at once. To store a fifth, first reset one stored card (rotate counterclockwise in place). If a stored card becomes the **top** of the deck, immediately unstore it (rotate counterclockwise) and send it to the back.

Stored resources **carry over** between rounds.

## End of Round

![When the round tracker is on top, flip or rotate it and put it at the back](/images/rules/palm-island/round.svg)

When the **round tracker** is on top, the round ends. Flip/rotate it to the next number and put it at the back. You cannot activate it when it is only the second card.

Moving from round **4 → 5**: return to the side marked **1/5** and use the smaller numbers.

After **8** rounds, the game ends.

## Scoring

Sum all **victory stars** on the **active area** of every card in your deck. Solo: beat your best score (or compare to the chart in the rulebook).

## Multiplayer Modes (summary)

| Mode | Idea |
|------|------|
| **Competitive** | Race for outpost / milestone cards; highest star score wins. Ties → most upgrades, then most stored resources. |
| **Cooperative** | Pay together to upgrade disaster cards before round 8 ends, or all lose. Group score = lowest individual score. |
| **Feats** | Solo achievements that unlock extras for future games. |

See the official rulebook for full competitive, cooperative, and feat details.
