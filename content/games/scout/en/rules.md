# SCOUT Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

On your turn, either play a connected stretch of cards to beat the table, or take one card from the left or right end of the table and slot it into your hand.

Each card has a value at both ends. You may turn the **whole hand** once before play starts, but you never shuffle individual cards around.

The standard game supports 2–5 players and is played over as many rounds as there are players.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In SCOUT, you are a circus performer managing a hand whose order cannot change. Your goal is to beat the table with adjacent cards or scout wisely, then score best across the rounds.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- 45 dual-indexed Show cards (values 1–10)
- SCOUT & SHOW / score chips
- Start-player marker
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

![A whole hand may be rotated once to use its other values, but its card order may never be rearranged](/images/rules/scout/hand-orientation.svg)

1. Shuffle the Show cards while randomizing both **order and orientation**.
2. Deal the hand size for the current player count; set unused cards aside. Each player takes up their full hand **without changing its order**.
3. Before play begins, a player may rotate their **entire hand** to use the values at the other ends of all cards. Individual cards cannot be flipped at this time.
4. Put the start-player marker with the first player. The table begins empty.

## Your Turn: Choose One — Play or Take

**Do one thing this turn:** **Show** (play) a combination, or **Scout** (take) one end card. At the start of a round the table is empty, so the first player must Show.
<!-- rule-section: show-a-combination -->
### Show a combination

![A stronger adjacent combination can capture the active set; otherwise Scout one end card and insert it into your hand](/images/rules/scout/show-or-scout.svg)

Pick a stretch of **adjacent** cards from your hand. Their visible values must be either:

- a **group**: all the same value; or
- a **run**: consecutive values in ascending or descending order.

Put that stretch on the table. An empty table accepts anything legal; otherwise you must beat the active combination. Check these three things in order:

| Priority | Stronger combination |
|---|---|
| 1 | More cards always wins. |
| 2 | At equal length, a group beats a run. |
| 3 | At equal length and type, the higher-valued combination wins. |

If you beat it, take the old table cards and put them face down in front of you. Your newly played cards now wait on the table for the next player.
<!-- rule-section: scout-an-end-card -->
### Scout an end card

Instead of playing, take **one card from either end** of the active combination. Choose either orientation and slot it anywhere into your hand; the other cards do not move. The player who played that combination gets a 1-point SCOUT & SHOW chip.

Normally, taking a card ends your turn. Once per round, you may **Scout & Show**: take an end card, slot it in, then immediately Show a legal combination.
<!-- rule-section: end-of-a-round-and-scoring -->
## End of a Round and Scoring

![The round ends when a hand is empty or everyone else Scouts; captured cards and chips score plus one while cards left in hand score minus one](/images/rules/scout/round-scoring.svg)

The round ends immediately when either:

- a player Shows their last card; or
- every player other than the owner of the active combination Scouts instead of beating it.

Then score the round:

| Score source | Points |
|---|---:|
| Each face-down captured card | +1 |
| Each SCOUT & SHOW chip | +1 |
| Each card left in your hand | -1 |

The player whose active combination was repeatedly Scouted to end the round does not take the hand-card penalty. Pass the start-player marker and begin the next round. After a number of rounds equal to the number of players, the highest total score wins.
<!-- rule-section: important-reminders -->
## Important Reminders

- “Adjacent” always means adjacent **in your current hand order**.
- You may insert a Scouted card anywhere, and choose either of its two values, but may not move the other cards around it.
- A run may descend as well as ascend.
- Use the separate two-player setup and round rules from the rulebook when playing with two players.

  **Rotate the Whole Hand, Never Rearrange**

  Your hand order is the puzzle. A card may be inserted only when Scouted; otherwise cards stay in their existing left-to-right order. The opening whole-hand rotation is your one way to switch every card to its other value.

  **Show or Scout**

  Choose exactly one normal action: **Show** an adjacent group/run, or **Scout** an end card from the active combination. With an empty table, the first player must Show.

  Related topics: 
  - [Show a combination](#flow-show)
  - [Scout an end card](#flow-scout)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-show -->
  **Show Adjacent Cards**

  Choose adjacent cards that are all equal (a group), or consecutive ascending/descending values (a run). Against an active set, compare: **more cards**, then **group over run**, then **higher values**. A winning Show captures the previous active cards face down.

  Related topics: 
  - [Could it beat the active set?](#flow-comparison)
  - [Instead Scout](#flow-scout)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-comparison -->
  **Beat It or Improve Your Hand**

  A stronger adjacent combination replaces the active set and captures it. If you cannot or do not want to Show, Scout the leftmost or rightmost active card instead. You may orient and insert that one card anywhere in your hand.

  ![A stronger adjacent combination can capture the active set; otherwise Scout one end card and insert it into your hand](/images/rules/scout/show-or-scout.svg)

  Related topics: 
  - [Scout now](#flow-scout)
  - [Check round end](#flow-round-end)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-scout -->
  **Scout from Either End**

  Take exactly one end card, choose either orientation, and insert it anywhere in your fixed-order hand. The active-set owner receives a 1-point SCOUT & SHOW chip. Scouting normally ends your turn; the once-per-round Scout & Show opportunity lets you immediately Show after Scouting.

  Related topics: 
  - [Has everyone Scouted?](#flow-round-end)
  - [Next player's action](#flow-turn)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-round-end -->
  **End the Round and Score**

  End immediately when someone Shows their final card, or when every other player Scouts the same active set. Score +1 per captured face-down card and per chip, then -1 per hand card. The owner of the repeatedly Scouted active set skips that hand penalty.

  ![The round ends when a hand is empty or everyone else Scouts; captured cards and chips score plus one while cards left in hand score minus one](/images/rules/scout/round-scoring.svg)

  Related topics: 
  - [Another round remains](#flow-setup)
  - [All rounds complete](#flow-winner)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-winner -->
  **Choose the Best Scout**

  After a number of rounds equal to the player count, total all round scores. The highest score wins the game.
