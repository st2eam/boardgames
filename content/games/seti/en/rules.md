# SETI: Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

**SETI: Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence** (Tomáš Holek; Czech Games Edition, **2024**) is a 1–4 player eurogame of space exploration. You lead a scientific institution hunting for traces of life beyond Earth, using current and emerging technologies.

Over **five rounds**, launch probes to nearby planets and moons, scan distant stars for signals and data, analyze samples, and upgrade telescopes, probes, and computers. More than 200 unique cards depict real projects (ISS, Large Hadron Collider, Perseverance, Voyager, and others). Two of five alien species are hidden each game until traces of life reveal them.

The player with the starting-player marker takes the first turn of a round. Play clockwise, skipping anyone who has already passed. After round 5, highest score wins.

![One main action, free extras, then milestones](/images/rules/seti/turn-flow.svg)
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In SETI, you lead a research institute searching the solar system for signs of alien life. Your goal is to earn the most prestige through exploration, signals, technology, and discoveries over five rounds.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- Main board: planetary board + solar system board (3 discs, Sun, 4 sector boards) + tech board
- 4 player boards (assembled), 4 quick-reference sheets
- Per colour: 8 figures, 30 markers, publicity counter, score counter, 100/200-point token, income card
- 48 tech tiles (12 techs × 4), 4 gold scoring tiles, 12 two-point tiles
- Main deck of **138** cards, starting-player marker, 2 solar-system rotation tokens
- Credits, energy, data tokens (unlimited if you run out)
- **5 alien species** (2 used per game): boards, rule sheets, cards, and unique tokens
- Solo: 24 objective tiles, 19 rival action cards (solo only)

Figures and markers of the same colour may stand in for each other if you run out.
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

1. Assemble the main board. Randomize the three solar-system discs and four sector boards so eight sectors each have one nearby star (CGE QR: `cge.as/seti-solarsystem`).
2. Shuffle the five alien boards **face down**; place **2** in the slots on the planetary board. Return the rest unseen. Leave alien cards in the box until a species is discovered.
3. Shuffle the main deck; deal **3** face-up cards as the card row. Credits, energy, and leftover data stay nearby.
4. Fill each nearby star’s data slot (some stars get more tokens than others).
5. For each of the 12 techs, shuffle the 4 matching tiles **tech-side down** and stack them; put a 2-point tile on top of each stack.
6. Make **4** end-of-round stacks for rounds 1–4, each with **players + 1** cards.
7. Place one rotation token on the tech board; put the other on the round-1 stack (reminder: first passer rotates).
8. Gold scoring tiles: random side up beside the board.
9. **Neutral milestones** at 20 and 30 VP: unused-colour markers. **1–2 players:** 2 markers each. **3 players:** 1 each. **4 players:** ignore these milestones.
10. Last person who saw a UFO is starting player. Score starts at **1 / 2 / 3 / 4** in turn order. Publicity **4**. Take **4 credits, 3 energy**, draw **5** cards, then **tuck one card for income** (gain its corner resource immediately).
<!-- rule-section: card-anatomy -->
## Card anatomy

Each project card is used for **only one** benefit when you spend it:

| Part | When you use it |
|------|-----------------|
| **Main-action cost + white effect** | Pay the cost and resolve the effect as your main action |
| **Free-action icon** (corner) | Discard anytime on your turn for that resource / move / publicity |
| **Income type** | The resource it becomes if tucked under your starting card |
| **Sector colour** (upper right) | Matching sectors during Scan |
| **Mission box** | Extra rewards if you keep the card in front of you |

If a card’s main effect includes Launch, Scan, Research, etc., you perform that nested action **without** paying its usual main-action cost.

<!-- rule-section: how-to-play -->
## How to Play

**5 rounds.** On your turn take **one main action**, plus any **free actions** before, during, or after it. Then resolve milestones and any species discovered on your turn. Skip players who have passed. When everyone has passed, collect income and pass the start marker left.

There is **no hand limit** during a round. Publicity **caps at 10** — ignore further publicity if you are already at 10.
<!-- rule-section: main-actions -->
### Main actions

| Action | Cost | What you do |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Launch** | 2 credits | Place a probe on **Earth**. Default limit: **1 probe in space** (solar-system rings only; planetary-board figures do not count). |
| **Orbit** | 1 credit + 1 energy | Probe must be on a planet other than Earth. Move it to that planet’s orbit; take the orbit bonuses. **First** orbiter there scores **3 VP** (cover that space with your figure). No limit on later orbiters. |
| **Land** | 3 energy (**2** if **any** orbiter is already there) | Probe on a planet other than Earth. Move it onto the planet; take the land bonuses (always a life-trace icon and VP). **First** lander(s) also take the printed data (cover that space). **Mars has two** first-lander spaces. Moons: only if an effect/tech allows it; **one** lander per moon. |
| **Scan** | 1 credit + 2 energy | See **Scan** below. Always at least **2** signals; up to **4** with telescope techs. |
| **Analyze** | 1 energy | Top row of your computer must be full. Discard all data in the computer (not the pool) and mark one **analyze** life-trace. |
| **Play a card** | Printed cost | Resolve the white effect. Missions and gold-box cards stay in front of you. |
| **Research** | 6 publicity | Must already be on space 6+. **Rotate**, take a tech you do not have, gain its printed bonus, flip it onto your board. First tile from a stack: discard the 2-point tile and score **2 VP**. |
| **Pass** | — | Free actions first, then: discard down to **4**; if you are first to pass this round, rotate; in rounds **1–4**, take one card from this round’s shrinking stack. Round **5**: still rotate, then discard the reminder token (no stack). |

**“At a planet”** on cards means a figure on the planetary board (orbit, surface, or moon) — not a probe still on the solar-system ring.
<!-- rule-section: movement-free -->
### Movement (free)

Spend **1 energy → 1 move**, or discard a card with a move icon. Each move: one probe into an **adjacent** space (not diagonal). Leaving **asteroids** costs **+1** move. The Sun is not a space. Entering a space with a publicity icon (not Earth) gains **1 publicity**. You may move a probe the turn you launch it. Rotation can push a probe for free (and still grants publicity if that space shows it).
<!-- rule-section: free-actions-your-turn-only -->
### Free actions (your turn only)

- Place data from your pool into your computer (see below). Pool max **6**; excess data is lost. Cannot place off-turn.
- Exchange **2** of one resource (cards / credits / energy) for **1** card, credit, or energy. **Not mixed** (2 cards for 1 credit is fine; 1 card + 1 energy is not). A new card may come from the row or the deck. Alien cards in hand count as cards.
- Buy a card for **3 publicity** (row or deck).
- Complete a **conditional** mission if its condition is met; mark **triggerable** mission circles when you do the listed thing **after** playing the card.

When you draw from the card row, refill immediately. Empty deck: shuffle the discard pile.
<!-- rule-section: scan-and-sectors -->
## Scan and sectors

To Scan, do these in **any order** (then refill the row at the end):

1. Mark **1 signal in Earth’s sector**.
2. Discard **1 card from the card row** and mark 1 signal in a sector matching that card’s corner colour (one of the two sectors of that colour).
3. Activate telescope techs in any order (most have an extra cost).

If a card effect makes you discard several row cards for signals, resolve all of those discards **before** refilling.

![Left: sector data slot. Right: computer top row and tech column](/images/rules/seti/scan-computer.svg)

**Mark a signal** (mandatory — you may not skip): take the **leftmost** data in that slot into your pool and replace it with your marker. Extra markers beyond the slot still help you **win** but give no data. The **second** slot space: **2 VP** immediately.

When the last data is replaced, the sector **completes after your main action**:

- Most markers wins. **Later** marker wins ties.
- Winner places a marker by the star and takes the printed reward (often a life-trace). Leave that “already won” marker there.
- Everyone who contributed at least 1 marker gets **1 publicity** (including the winner).
- Second place (if any) leaves a marker on the **first** data space; refill the rest with data.
- The sector can be won again. Ignore the star-side “already won” marker when counting the next winner.

**Example:** Three players each have 2 markers. Orange placed last, so Orange wins even if that last marker overflowed and gave no data.
<!-- rule-section: computer -->
## Computer

Place pool data **left to right** on the top row. Covering printed spaces can give publicity, an income-tuck, or 2 VP.

**Computer techs** add a column: top space **2 VP** when you place data there; bottom space the printed reward. You may fill a bottom space only if the space **above** is full. Bottom spaces do **not** need to fill left-to-right. Analyze only requires the **top** row full — bottoms may stay empty. Analyze clears **all** computer data, including bottoms.

Placing a computer tech under existing data is **not** “placing data” — you do not score the 2 VP that token now covers.
<!-- rule-section: technologies -->
## Technologies

Probe techs go in probe slots, telescope techs in telescope slots. A computer tech may go in **any** computer slot, but you still cannot have the same tech twice.

Card effects that grant a tech still **rotate**, cost **no** publicity, and are limited to that tech type. If you already have all 4 of that type, ignore the tech (you still rotate).

| Type | Tech | Immediate / ongoing |
|------|------|---------------------|
| Probe | Dual probes | Limit **2** in space. Taking it lets you launch once for free (this bonus already uses the new limit). |
| Probe | Asteroids | Entering asteroids: +1 publicity. Leaving them no longer costs +1 move. |
| Probe | Cheap land | Land costs **1 less** energy (2 without an orbiter, 1 with). |
| Probe | Moons | You may land on a moon instead of the planet, same cost. |
| Telescope | Neighbour scan | During Scan, mark a sector **adjacent to Earth** instead of Earth’s sector. Taking it also gives **2 data**. |
| Telescope | Discard scan | During Scan, discard 1 **hand** card to mark an extra matching sector. |
| Telescope | Mercury scan | During Scan, pay **1 publicity** to mark an extra signal in Mercury’s sector. |
| Telescope | Scan launch | During Scan, pay **1 energy** to launch a probe, **or** gain 1 movement. |
| Computer | (4 techs) | Each adds a computer column (2 VP on top, printed reward below). |

Telescope techs activate **only during Scan**, in any order.
<!-- rule-section: research-and-rotation -->
## Research and rotation

Research always rotates the solar system first (main action or card). The **first pass** each round also rotates.

![Rotate top, then middle (with top), then bottom (all three), one sector counter-clockwise](/images/rules/seti/rotation.svg)

1. First rotation: **top** disc.  
2. Second: **middle** (top goes with it).  
3. Third: **bottom** (all three). Then back to the top.

Always **one sector counter-clockwise**. Probes usually rotate with discs. Cutouts may leave a probe still, or **bump** it into the next space (free; publicity if that space shows it). Advance the rotation counter each time.
<!-- rule-section: missions -->
## Missions

After you play a mission, keep it in front of you.
<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->
- <!-- rule-item: missions-item-1 -->
  **Conditional:** when the condition is true, complete it as a free action (even immediately). Turn it face down. You are not forced to complete it the instant it becomes true.
- <!-- rule-item: missions-item-2 -->
  **Triggerable:** mark a circle only for things you do **after** playing the card (some can trigger off-turn). Each circle once. If one effect would hit several circles, mark **only one**; trigger again for the next. All circles marked → completed, turn face down.

**Official example (Mariner 10):** if your probe is **already** at Mercury when you play the card, nothing happens. It triggers when you **move to** Mercury or Venus. You may skip the publicity if you are already at 10.

Gold-box cards stay in front of you for **end-game VP**.
<!-- rule-section: life-traces-and-aliens -->
## Life traces and aliens

Three trace types: typically from **scan**, **land**, and **analyze**. A wildcard can stand for any type. Mark an empty matching discovery space under an alien board, or an **overflow** space (**3 VP**). You may choose overflow even if a matching board space is still open.

When all three discovery spaces of a species are filled, **discover** it after your turn (**after** milestones): flip the board, read its sheet, set up its cards, reward the three markers. Do not read unused species.

Later traces mark the species board for printed rewards. Neutral (nonplayer) markers in 2–3p games only fill the six discovery spaces, never the species board itself.

Alien cards are slightly stronger and come only from the species. In hand they follow normal card rules (**Exertian** cards do not count toward the pass hand limit of 4).
<!-- rule-section: milestones -->
## Milestones

Resolved **between turns**, after the current player’s free actions. If several players crossed a threshold, resolve clockwise starting with the player who just finished. Neutral milestones resolve **last**.

**Gold (25 / 50 / 70 VP):** choose a gold tile you have not marked. First claimer takes the highest space; later claimers take the next. You mark **3 of 4** tiles. Passing 100 and lapping these spaces does nothing.

**Neutral (20 / 30, not 4p):** move a nonplayer marker onto the leftmost empty discovery space. This can discover a species. Unused if both species are already found, or if those markers are already spent.
<!-- rule-section: between-rounds -->
## Between rounds

1. Income: starting income card plus every tucked card (after rounds **1–4**; none before round 1 or after round 5).
2. Pass the start marker **left**.
3. Move the rotation reminder onto the next round’s stack.

You may take income as soon as you have passed. Tucking for income: gain the corner resource **immediately**, and again at each later income.
<!-- rule-section: end-of-game -->
## End of game

After the last player passes in round 5:

1. Gold-box **end-game cards**.
2. **Gold scoring tiles** (sets × the value you marked).
3. Any extra scoring from a discovered species.

Most points wins. Exact ties stand (the rulebook’s Carl Sagan joke).

**Gold tiles (one side each):**

| Tile | Side A | Side B |
|------|--------|--------|
| Tech | Sets of **3 different** tech types (equal to how many you have of your **rarest** type) | **Pairs** of techs (5 techs = 2 pairs) |
| Mission | Each **completed** mission (conditional + triggerable) | Pairs of completed missions and/or gold-box cards |
| Income | Sets of tucked credit + energy + card (ignore the **printed** 3 credits / 2 energy / 1 card on the starting card) | Points for the tucked type you have **most** of: credits **or** energy (again ignore the printed starting income) |
| Other | Sets of **3 different** life traces (overflow counts) | `min(sector wins, orbiters + landers)` — multiple wins in the same sector all count |
<!-- rule-section: solo-summary -->
## Solo (summary)

Compete against a rival institution (official rules p. 22). Pick a 1–5 star difficulty. First game: consider 1-star (no objectives).

The rival uses an action deck (4 basic cards; 3-star+ shuffle in one advanced card). Credits, energy, and cards they “gain” become **progress-track** steps instead (orbiters usually advance 4). Publicity they spend on techs as a player would. They claim only the **first** (highest) gold-tile space, using the action card’s arrow to break ties.

Objectives (not 1-star): stack I / II / III as printed on the rival board; reveal the top 3. Unmet objectives at round end advance the rival.

Outscore the rival after five rounds.

  **Analyze:** 1 energy if the **top** computer row is full. Clear **all** computer data (pool stays); mark one analyze trace. Pool max **6**.

  ![Left: sector data slot. Right: computer top row and tech column](/images/rules/seti/scan-computer.svg)

  Related topics: 
  - [Play a Card](#flow-cards)
  - [Aliens](#flow-aliens)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-cards -->
  **Play a Card**

  Pay the printed cost; resolve the white effect. Nested launch/scan/research **skip** the usual action cost.
  
  **Conditional missions:** keep in front; complete as a free action when the condition is true (even immediately).
  **Triggerable missions:** mark circles only for things you do **after** playing the card. All circles marked → complete.
  **Gold-box cards:** keep for end-game VP.
  
  Otherwise discard after the effect.

  Related topics: 
  - [Card Anatomy](#flow-card-anatomy)
  - [Research / Rotate](#flow-research)
  - [Free Actions](#flow-free)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-research -->
  **Research and Rotate**

  **Research:** 6 publicity (must be ≥6). Rotate the solar system, take a new tech, gain its bonus, flip it onto your board. First from a stack: **2 VP**.
  
  Rotation order: top disc → middle → bottom → repeat, one sector **counter-clockwise**. Probes usually move with discs; cutouts may leave them or bump them (free publicity if that space shows it).
  
  Also rotate on the **first pass** each round. Card techs rotate but cost no publicity.

  ![Rotate top, then middle (with top), then bottom (all three), one sector counter-clockwise](/images/rules/seti/rotation.svg)

  Related topics: 
  - [Pass / Income](#flow-pass)
  - [Your Turn](#flow-turn)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-free -->
  **Free Actions**

  Anytime on **your** turn:
  
  - Place pool data into the computer (left to right).
  - Exchange **2** cards, credits, **or** energy for **1** card, credit, or energy (not mixed).
  - Buy a card for **3 publicity** (row or deck).
  - Complete missions you qualify for.
  
  Publicity **caps at 10**. No hand limit until you pass (then 4).

  Related topics: 
  - [Pass / Income](#flow-pass)
  - [Your Turn](#flow-turn)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-pass -->
  **Pass and Income**

  Pass is a main action (free actions first):
  
  1. Discard down to **4**.
  2. First passer this round **rotates** (round 5: still rotate, discard the token).
  3. Rounds **1–4:** pick one end-of-round card from the shrinking stack.
  
  When all have passed: income (starting card + tucked cards), start marker left, reminder onto the next stack. You may take income as soon as you have passed.

  Related topics: 
  - [Scoring](#flow-scoring)
  - [Milestones](#flow-milestones)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-milestones -->
  **Milestones**

  Resolved **between turns** (after free actions).
  
  **Gold (25 / 50 / 70 VP):** mark one gold tile you have not marked; first claimer takes the highest space. You mark **3 of 4** tiles. Second lap past 100 does nothing.
  
  **Neutral (20 / 30, not 4p):** move a nonplayer marker onto the leftmost empty discovery space. May discover a species. Unused if both species are already found.
  
  If several players crossed thresholds, resolve clockwise from the player who just finished.

  ![One main action, free extras, then milestones](/images/rules/seti/turn-flow.svg)

  Related topics: 
  - [Aliens](#flow-aliens)
  - [Scoring](#flow-scoring)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-aliens -->
  **Alien Species**

  Traces: **scan / land / analyze** (plus wildcards). Mark a matching discovery space, or overflow for **3 VP**.
  
  When all three discovery spaces of a species fill, discover it **after** that turn (after milestones): flip the board, read its sheet, set up cards, reward those three markers. Don’t spoil unused species.
  
  Later traces mark the species board for printed rewards. 2–3p nonplayer markers never sit on the species board itself.

  Related topics: 
  - [Scoring](#flow-scoring)
  - [Scan / Analyze](#flow-scan)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-scoring -->
  **Final Scoring**

  After round 5’s last pass:
  
  1. Gold-box **end-game cards**.
  2. **Gold tiles:** sets × the value you marked.
  3. Extra VP from a discovered species.
  
  Most points wins; exact ties stand.
  
  Tile examples: 3 different techs · pairs of techs · completed missions · tucked income sets · most credit **or** energy income · 3 different traces · min(sector wins, orbiters+landers) · pairs of missions/gold-box cards.

  Related topics: 
  - [Setup](#flow-setup)
  - [Your Turn](#flow-turn)
  - [Techs](#flow-techs)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-card-anatomy -->
  **Card Anatomy**

  Spend a card for **only one** benefit:
  
  - **White effect** = main action (pay the printed cost). Nested launch/scan/research **skip** the usual action cost.
  - **Corner icon** = discard as a free action.
  - **Income type** = what you gain each round if tucked.
  - **Sector colour** = Scan matching.
  
  **Conditional mission:** complete as a free action when true (even immediately).
  **Triggerable:** only things you do **after** playing it. One trigger marks one circle.
  **Gold box:** keep for end-game VP.

  Related topics: 
  - [Play a Card](#flow-cards)
  - [Your Turn](#flow-turn)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-techs -->
  **Technologies**

  **Research (6 publicity):** rotate, take a new tech, gain its bonus. First from a stack: **2 VP**. Card techs rotate but cost no publicity.
  
  **Probe:** dual probes (limit 2, free launch when taken) · asteroids (publicity, no extra leave cost) · land −1 energy · moons.
  **Telescope (Scan only):** mark adjacent to Earth instead · discard a hand card for an extra match · 1 publicity for Mercury’s sector · 1 energy to launch or gain a move.
  **Computer:** extra column (2 VP on top, printed reward below). Analyze still only needs the **top** row full.

  Related topics: 
  - [Research / Rotate](#flow-research)
  - [Scan / Analyze](#flow-scan)
