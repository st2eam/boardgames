# SETI: Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence Rules

## Overview

**SETI: Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence** (Tomáš Holek; Czech Games Edition, **2024**) is a 1–4 player eurogame of space exploration. You lead a scientific institution hunting for traces of life beyond Earth, using current and emerging technologies.

Over **five rounds**, launch probes to nearby planets and moons, scan distant stars for signals and data, analyze samples, and upgrade telescopes, probes, and computers. More than 200 unique cards depict real projects (ISS, Large Hadron Collider, Perseverance, Voyager, and others). Two of five alien species are hidden each game until traces of life reveal them.

The player with the starting-player marker takes the first turn of a round. Play clockwise, skipping anyone who has already passed. After round 5, highest score wins.

## Components

- Main board: planetary board + solar system board (3 discs, Sun, 4 sector boards) + tech board
- 4 player boards (assembled), 4 quick-reference sheets
- Per color: 8 figures, 30 markers, publicity counter, score counter, 100/200-point token, income card
- 48 tech tiles (12 techs × 4), 4 gold scoring tiles, 12 two-point tiles
- Main deck of **138** cards, starting-player marker, 2 solar-system rotation tokens
- Credits, energy, data tokens (unlimited if you run out)
- **5 alien species** (2 used per game): boards, rule sheets, cards, and unique tokens
- Solo: 24 objective tiles, 19 rival action cards (solo only)

## Setup

1. Assemble the main board. Randomize the three solar-system discs and four sector boards so eight sectors each have one nearby star (CGE provides a QR randomizer).
2. Shuffle the five alien boards **face down**; place **2** in the slots on the planetary board. Return the rest unseen. Leave alien cards in the box until a species is discovered.
3. Shuffle the main deck; deal **3** face-up cards as the card row. Credits, energy, and leftover data stay nearby.
4. Fill each nearby star’s data slot (some stars get more than others).
5. For each of the 12 techs, shuffle the 4 matching tiles **tech-side down** and stack them; put a 2-point tile on top of each stack.
6. Make **4** end-of-round stacks for rounds 1–4, each with **players + 1** cards.
7. Place one rotation token on the tech board; put the other on the round-1 stack (reminder: first passer rotates).
8. Gold scoring tiles: random side up beside the board.
9. **Neutral milestones** at 20 and 30 VP: unused-color markers. **1–2 players:** 2 markers each. **3 players:** 1 each. **4 players:** ignore these milestones.
10. Last person who saw a UFO is starting player. Score starts at **1 / 2 / 3 / 4** in turn order. Publicity **4**. Take **4 credits, 3 energy**, draw **5** cards, then **tuck one card for income** (gain its corner resource immediately).

## How to Play

**5 rounds.** On your turn take **one main action**, plus any **free actions** before, during, or after it. Then resolve milestones and any species discovered on your turn. Skip players who have passed. When everyone has passed, collect income and pass the start marker left.

A card is used for **only one** of its benefits: main-action effect **or** discard for its free-action icon, not both.

### Main actions

| Action | Cost | What you do |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Launch** | 2 credits | Place a probe on **Earth**. Default limit: **1 probe in space** (solar-system rings only; planetary-board figures do not count). |
| **Orbit** | 1 credit + 1 energy | Probe must be on a planet other than Earth. Move it to that planet’s orbit; take the orbit bonuses. **First** orbiter there scores **3 VP**. No limit on orbiters. |
| **Land** | 3 energy (2 if **any** orbiter is already there) | Probe on a planet other than Earth. Move it onto the planet; take the land bonuses (always includes a life-trace icon and VP). **First** lander(s) also take the printed data (Mars has **two** first-lander spaces). Moons: only if an effect/tech allows it; **one** lander per moon. |
| **Scan** | 1 credit + 2 energy | Mark **Earth’s sector**; discard 1 card-row card and mark a sector matching its corner color; refill the row. Activate telescope techs (most have an extra cost). Always at least **2** signals; up to **4** with techs. |
| **Analyze** | 1 energy | Top row of your computer must be full. Discard all data in the computer (not the pool) and mark one **analyze** life-trace. |
| **Play a card** | Printed cost | Resolve the white effect. Nested launch/scan/etc. **skip** the usual action cost. Missions and gold-box cards stay in front of you. |
| **Research** | 6 publicity | **Rotate** the solar system, take a tech you do not have, gain its printed bonus, flip it onto your board. First tile from a stack: discard the 2-point tile and score **2 VP**. Card effects that grant a tech also rotate, but do not spend publicity. |
| **Pass** | — | Free actions first, then: discard down to **4** cards; if you are first to pass this round, rotate the solar system; in rounds **1–4**, take one card from this round’s stack (earlier pass = more choice). Round **5**: still rotate, then discard the reminder token (no stack). |

### Movement (free)

Spend **1 energy → 1 move**, or discard a card with a move icon. Each move: one probe into an adjacent space (not diagonal). Leaving **asteroids** costs **+1** move. The Sun is not a space. Landing on a space with a publicity icon (not Earth) gains **1 publicity**. You may move a probe the turn you launch it.

### Free actions (anytime on your turn)

- Place data from your pool into your computer (left to right; resolve printed spaces). Pool max **6**; excess data is lost. Cannot place off-turn.
- Exchange **2** of one resource (cards / credits / energy) for **1** card, credit, or energy. A new card may come from the row or the deck. Mixed pairs are illegal (except as some alien cards allow).
- Buy a card for **3 publicity** (row or deck).
- Complete a **conditional** mission if its condition is met; mark **triggerable** mission circles when you do the listed thing **after** playing the card.

Publicity **caps at 10**; ignore further publicity if you are already at 10.

### Sectors (scan)

To mark a signal: take the leftmost data in that slot into your pool and replace it with your marker (mandatory). Excess markers beyond the slot still help you **win** the sector but give no data. Second slot space: **2 VP** immediately.

When the last data is replaced, the sector **completes** after your main action: most markers wins (later marker wins ties). Winner takes the star-side reward and leaves a marker there. Everyone who contributed gets **1 publicity**. Second place (if any) leaves a marker on the first data space; refill the rest with data. The sector can be won again; ignore the “already won” marker when counting the next winner.

### Computer

Covering spaces can give publicity, income-tuck, or 2 VP. Lower computer-tech spaces need the space above filled. Analyze only requires the **top** row full.

### Rotating the solar system

Happens on **every research** and on the **first pass** each round.

1. First rotation: top disc. 2. Second: middle (top goes with it). 3. Third: bottom (all three). Then back to the top. Always **one sector counter-clockwise**. Probes usually rotate with discs; cutouts may leave a probe still or **bump** it into the next space (free; publicity if that space shows it).

### Life traces and aliens

Three trace types: typically from **scan**, **land**, and **analyze**. A wildcard trace can stand for any type. Mark an empty matching discovery space under an alien board, or an **overflow** space (**3 VP**) if that color is full. When all three discovery spaces of a species are filled, **discover** it after your turn (after milestones): flip the board, read its sheet, set up its cards, reward the three markers. Do not read unused species.

Further traces after discovery mark spaces on the species board for printed rewards. Neutral (nonplayer) markers in 2–3p games only fill the six discovery spaces, never the species board itself.

## Between rounds

1. Income: starting income card plus every tucked card (after rounds 1–4; there is no income before round 1 or after round 5).
2. Pass the start marker left.
3. Move the rotation reminder onto the next round’s stack.

You may take income as soon as you have passed.

## End of game

After the last player passes in round 5:

1. Gold-box **end-game cards**.
2. **Gold scoring tiles** (sets × the value you marked at 25 / 50 / 70 VP; you mark **three** of the four tiles, highest remaining space first).
3. Any extra scoring from a discovered species.

Most points wins. Exact ties stand.

**Gold tile examples (one side each):** sets of three different techs; pairs of techs; completed missions; tucked income sets (ignore the printed 3 credits / 2 energy / 1 card on the starting card); most-tucked credit **or** energy income; sets of three different life traces (overflow counts); sector wins paired with orbiters/landers (the smaller count); pairs of completed missions and/or gold-box cards.

## Solo (summary)

Compete against a rival institution (official rules p. 22). Pick a 1–5 star difficulty, give the rival a board and action deck, and (except 1-star) an objectives stack. Rival progress converts credits/energy/cards into track movement. Outscore the rival after five rounds.

## Special Rules

- Nested actions on cards do **not** pay the printed main-action cost.
- “At a planet” means a figure on the planetary board (orbit, surface, or moon) — not a probe still on the solar-system ring.
- If the deck empties, shuffle the discard pile.
- Figures and markers of the same color may stand in for each other if you run out.
