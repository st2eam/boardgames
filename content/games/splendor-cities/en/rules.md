# Splendor: Cities of Splendor Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

*Cities of Splendor* is an expansion for *Splendor* with **four independent modules**: Cities, Trading Posts, The Orient, and Strongholds.  
**Play only one module at a time** — do not combine them. Requires the base game.

We recommend starting with **Cities** — it changes the victory condition and raises competition.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Splendor: Cities, you are a merchant using one expansion module with the base game. Your goal is to meet a City tile’s requirement first when using Cities, or otherwise use the chosen module to outscore rivals.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

| Module | Components |
|--------|------------|
| Shared | 1 first-player tile |
| Cities | 7 double-sided city tiles |
| Trading Posts | 1 “Route to the Orient” board, 20 coats of arms (5 per color) |
| The Orient | 30 Orient development cards (10 per level) |
| Strongholds | 12 strongholds (3 per color) |
<!-- rule-section: first-player-tile -->
## First Player Tile

Place this tile in front of the first player so you remember that the player to their **right** takes the last turn when the end of the game is triggered.

<!-- rule-section: i-cities -->
## I. Cities

![Cities replace nobles: meet prestige and bonuses, claim a city to win](/images/rules/splendor-cities/cities.svg)

Bruges, Lyon, Lisbon, Seville, Venice, Florence, Pisa — each city wants a unique masterpiece and will spend lavishly. Can you meet their demands?
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
### Setup

1. Do **not** use the noble tiles.
2. Shuffle the city tiles and always place **3** of them face up on either side at random (**regardless of player count**).
3. Remove the unused city tiles from the game.

Otherwise set up as in the base game.
<!-- rule-section: additional-rules -->
### Additional Rules

At the end of your turn, **automatically** take a city tile if you meet its requirements:

- Prestige points at least equal to the value on the tile; **and**
- At least the quantity and type of bonuses shown (same idea as nobles).

**Examples:**

- At least 13 prestige, 4 red bonuses, and 3 black bonuses
- At least 15 prestige and 5 bonuses of the same type (e.g. 5 blue)
- At least 14 prestige, 4 green bonuses, and 4 bonuses of another single color (e.g. 4 black)

If you qualify for several cities, choose **one**. Keep the tile until the end of the game.
<!-- rule-section: end-of-the-game -->
### End of the Game

When a player takes a city tile, **finish the current round**.

- If only one player took a city → that player wins
- If several players took a city → among them, the one with the **most prestige** wins

<!-- rule-section: ii-trading-posts -->
## II. Trading Posts

![After a noble visit, place a coat of arms to keep a trade power](/images/rules/splendor-cities/trading-posts.svg)

With sea routes to India open, each guild founds new posts. Who adapts fastest to the new rules of trade?

This module slightly shortens the game and grants twisting powers — watch your opponents!
<!-- rule-section: section-10 -->
### Setup

1. Place the board within easy reach.
2. Each player takes **5 identical coats of arms**.

Otherwise set up as in the base game (nobles are used).
<!-- rule-section: section-11 -->
### Additional Rules

At the end of your turn (**after** any noble visit), if you meet a power’s requirement, place one of your coats of arms on its space.  
You keep that power until the end of the game.
<!-- rule-section: powers -->
### Powers

| Requirement | Power |
|-------------|--------|
| 3 red + 1 white bonuses | After you buy 1 development card, take 1 gem token (not on the turn you place this coat; take before replacing the card; may take a color you just spent) |
| 2 white bonuses | When you take 2 gems of the same color, also take 1 gem of another color (still need ≥ 4 available to take 2 of a color) |
| 3 blue + 1 black bonuses | Each gold token counts as **2** gems of one color when buying; still counts as 1 toward the 10-token limit |
| 5 green bonuses + 1 noble | **+5 prestige** (can end the game; only once even with multiple nobles) |
| 3 black bonuses | **+1 prestige** per coat of arms you have on the board (can end the game) |

End-game trigger matches the base game (usually finish the round after someone reaches 15 prestige).

<!-- rule-section: iii-the-orient -->
## III. The Orient

![Orient cards mix into each level and change how nobles are claimed](/images/rules/splendor-cities/orient.svg)
The East opens its wealth; patrons chase the exotic. Can you reshape your commercial empire?

Orient cards put nobles at the center of new strategies — claim them faster, reserve them, or keep cards valuable after they are gone.
<!-- rule-section: section-14 -->
### Setup

For each level (1 / 2 / 3):

1. Shuffle the 10 Orient cards of that level;
2. Reveal **2** and place them to the right of the base-game row;
3. Place the remaining 8 as a deck on the right.

At all times: **4 base + 2 Orient** face-up cards per level (empty spaces stay empty if a deck runs out).
<!-- rule-section: section-15 -->
### Additional Rules

When a card is bought or reserved, replace it from its **matching** deck.
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: orient-card-effects -->
### Orient Card Effects
<!-- rule-section: level-1 -->
#### Level 1

- **Double gold:** Worth 2 gold (do not draw tokens; do not count toward the 10-token limit) usable in a **single** purchase; each may replace any color; if you use only one, the second is wasted. Then discard the card (box) — no further effect.
- **Pairing:** Immediately and permanently pair with a development you already own that has a bonus; this card is worth 1 bonus of that color. You cannot buy it with no bonus cards. Pair before replacing the card.
<!-- rule-section: level-2 -->
#### Level 2

- **Pair + free card:** Pair as above, then take a face-up level-1 card (base or Orient) for free.
- **Double bonus:** Worth **2** bonuses of the shown color (for buying, nobles, and discarding).
- **Reserve a noble:** Take a face-up noble face-down in front of you — only you can be visited by that noble (if you meet requirements). No gold. No limit on reserved nobles.
<!-- rule-section: level-3 -->
#### Level 3

- **Free level 2:** Take a face-up level-2 card (base or Orient) for free.
- **Discard to buy:** To buy, discard **2** bonuses of the shown color you already own (return those cards to the box). Cannot discard reserved cards; prioritize discarding “J” cards paired to that color if you have them.

End-game trigger matches the base game.

<!-- rule-section: iv-strongholds -->
## IV. Strongholds

As cities found colonies, tension rises. Each guild builds strongholds to guard wealth and push expansion.

More interaction; games may run a bit longer. Balance slowing others with speeding yourself up.
<!-- rule-section: section-21 -->
### Setup

Each player takes **3 strongholds** of one color and places them in front of them.

Otherwise set up as in the base game.
<!-- rule-section: section-22 -->
### Additional Rules

Each time you buy a development card (hand or table), **before** replacing it, choose one:

1. **Place or move** one of **your** strongholds onto a face-up table card that has no stronghold of another color (you may stack on your own).  
2. **Remove** another player’s stronghold from a table card and return it to them.
<!-- rule-section: stronghold-powers -->
### Stronghold Powers

- **Occupation:** A card with a stronghold can be bought or reserved only by that owner; they take their strongholds back when they buy/reserve it.
- **Conquest:** While all **3** of your strongholds are on the same face-up card, you may buy that card **after** your action (pay → reclaim strongholds → place or remove a stronghold → replace).  
  Do this before a noble visit and before checking the token limit.

End-game trigger matches the base game.

  For each level: shuffle 10 → reveal **2** to the right of the base row → rest as a deck.
  
  Always **4 base + 2 Orient** face-up per level (gaps stay if a deck is empty). Replace from the matching deck.

  ![Orient cards mix into each level and change how nobles are claimed](/images/rules/splendor-cities/orient.svg)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-orient-cards -->
  **Orient – Card Effects**

  **Level 1:** double-gold joker for one purchase (then discard) · pair with an owned bonus card for +1 matching bonus
  
  **Level 2:** pair then free level-1 · double bonus of a color · reserve a noble (no gold; only you can host that noble)
  
  **Level 3:** free level-2 · buy by discarding 2 bonuses of the shown color (not from reserve)
  
  End game as in base Splendor.

  **Strongholds – Setup & Actions**

  **Components:** 12 strongholds (3 per color). Each player takes 3.
  
  After every purchase (before replace), you must:
  
  1. **Place/move** one of your strongholds onto a face-up card with no other color’s stronghold (stacking your own is OK); **or**
  2. **Remove** an opponent’s stronghold and return it.

  Related topics: 
  - [Occupation & Conquest](#flow-strongholds-powers)
  - [Back to Modules](#flow-overview)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-strongholds-powers -->
  **Strongholds – Powers**

  **Occupation:** Only the owner may buy or reserve a card with their stronghold(s); they reclaim those strongholds when they do.
  
  **Conquest:** If all **3** of your strongholds are on one face-up card, you may buy it **after** your normal action (pay → reclaim → place/remove → replace). Do this before nobles and before the 10-token check.
  
  End game as in base Splendor.
