# Splendor: Pokémon Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

Splendor: Pokémon is a standalone variant of Splendor themed around the Pokémon universe. Players are Pokémon trainers collecting Pokéballs to catch Pokémon, build their team, and attract Legendary Pokémon. The core mechanics remain the same as original Splendor, with key additions: an **evolution mechanic**, **Rare & Legendary Pokémon**, and a higher victory threshold of **18 points**. Does not require the original Splendor.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Splendor Pokémon, you are a trainer collecting Poké Balls and Pokémon. Your goal is to build your team and be the first trainer to reach 18 prestige points.
<!-- rule-section: what-s-different-from-original-splendor -->
## What's Different from Original Splendor

| | Original Splendor | Pokémon Edition |
|---|---|---|
| **Theme** | Gem merchants | Pokémon trainers |
| **Resources** | Gem tokens | Pokéball tokens (5 colors) |
| **Wild token** | Gold | Master Ball |
| **Cards** | Development cards | Pokémon cards (3 levels) |
| **Nobles** | Noble tiles | Rare & Legendary Pokémon |
| **Victory points** | 15 to win | 18 to win |
| **Evolution** | None | New mechanic (see below) |
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

Same as original Splendor: 3 levels of Pokémon cards (4 face-up each), Pokéball tokens in the center, and Rare/Legendary Pokémon revealed. Each player receives a **Trainer tile** (used as a removed-cards zone for evolution).
<!-- rule-section: turn-actions -->
## Turn Actions

![One action: tokens, catch a Pokémon, or reserve](/images/rules/splendor-pokemon/turn-actions.svg)

Same four actions as original Splendor:
<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->
1. <!-- rule-item: turn-actions-item-1 -->
   **Collect 3 different Pokéballs** (3 different colors)
2. <!-- rule-item: turn-actions-item-2 -->
   **Collect 2 same-color Pokéballs** (requires ≥4 of that color available)
3. <!-- rule-item: turn-actions-item-3 -->
   **Reserve a Pokémon** + take 1 Master Ball (wild)
4. <!-- rule-item: turn-actions-item-4 -->
   **Catch a Pokémon** by paying its Pokéball cost

Token limit remains **10 per player**.
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: new-mechanic-evolution -->
## New Mechanic: Evolution

![End of turn: evolve with bonuses only; old Pokémon goes under the Trainer tile](/images/rules/splendor-pokemon/evolution.svg)

At the **end of your turn** (after checking the token limit), you may evolve **one** of your caught Pokémon:
<!-- rule-section: evolution-requirements -->
### Evolution Requirements
1. The Pokémon's evolved form must be available — either face-up in the display **or** in your reserved cards.
2. You must have enough **Pokéball bonuses** (from caught Pokémon) matching the evolution cost shown on the card. **Tokens cannot be used for evolution — only permanent bonuses count.**
<!-- rule-section: how-evolution-works -->
### How Evolution Works
1. Take the evolved Pokémon card and place it in front of you (it provides its own points and bonuses).
2. Place the pre-evolution Pokémon card **under your Trainer tile** — it is removed from the game. It no longer provides points or bonuses.
3. If the evolved form came from the display, refill from the corresponding deck.
<!-- rule-section: evolution-rules -->
### Evolution Rules
- You may evolve at most **1 Pokémon per turn**
- Evolution is **optional** — you can skip it even if eligible
- Level 1 Pokémon evolve to Level 2; Level 2 evolve to Level 3
- Bonuses used for evolution are **not consumed** (they remain permanent)
<!-- rule-section: rare-legendary-pok-mon -->
## Rare & Legendary Pokémon

These replace Noble tiles from the original game.

| Type | Display | Bonuses | Master Ball Required | Points |
|------|---------|---------|:-------------------:|--------|
| **Rare** | 1 face-up at a time | 2 permanent bonuses | No | Lower |
| **Legendary** | 1 face-up at a time | 2 permanent bonuses | Yes (1 Master Ball) | Higher |
<!-- rule-section: key-rules -->
### Key Rules
- Rare and Legendary Pokémon **cannot be reserved**
- They are caught like regular Pokémon (pay the cost), but Legendary ones additionally require spending 1 Master Ball
- Each provides **2 permanent Pokéball bonuses** instead of the usual 1
- When caught, immediately reveal the next one from the corresponding deck
<!-- rule-section: game-end -->
## Game End

When any player reaches **18 or more points**, complete the current round.

**Winner:** Most points.

**Tiebreakers (in order):**
1. More Pokémon cards (including those under Trainer tile)
2. More evolutions performed
<!-- rule-section: strategy-tips -->
## Strategy Tips

- Evolution is very powerful — catching Level 1 Pokémon with 0 points can set up high-value evolutions later
- Master Balls are crucial for Legendary Pokémon — reserve cards strategically to collect them
- Rare & Legendary Pokémon provide 2 bonuses each, making them efficient for building your engine

- <!-- rule-item: flow-setup -->
  **Game Setup**

  **Components:** 90 Pokémon cards (3 levels), 5 Rare Pokémon, 5 Legendary Pokémon, Pokéball tokens (5 colors + Master Balls), Trainer tiles
  
  1. Shuffle each level separately; reveal 4 cards per level
  2. Shuffle Rare deck, reveal 1; shuffle Legendary deck, reveal 1
  3. Pokéball tokens: 2p = 4 each | 3p = 5 each | 4p = all 7 (Master Balls always 5)
  4. Each player takes a Trainer tile
  5. Target: **18 points** to win

- <!-- rule-item: flow-take3 -->
  **Collect 3 Different Pokéballs**

  Take **3 Pokéball tokens of different colors** from the supply.
  
  - If fewer than 3 colors available, take as many different as you can
  - **Cannot** take Master Balls (only gained by reserving)
  - Token limit: **10 tokens** max at end of turn

  Related topics: 
  - [Check Evolution](#flow-evolve-check)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-take2 -->
  **Collect 2 Same Pokéballs**

  Take 2 Pokéball tokens of the **same color**.
  
  ⚠️ That color must have **≥ 4 tokens** remaining in supply.
  
  - Token limit: **10 tokens** max at end of turn

  Related topics: 
  - [Check Evolution](#flow-evolve-check)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-reserve -->
  **Reserve a Pokémon**

  Take a card and keep it face-down in front of you:
  
  1. Choose from any of the 12 face-up cards **or** blind-draw from any level deck
  2. Gain **1 Master Ball** token (if available)
  3. Only you can catch this reserved Pokémon
  
  ⚠️ Maximum **3 reserved cards**
  
  ⚠️ **Rare and Legendary Pokémon CANNOT be reserved!**
  
  💡 Reserving is the only way to get Master Balls — needed for Legendaries

  Related topics: 
  - [Check Evolution](#flow-evolve-check)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-catch -->
  **Catch a Pokémon**

  Pay the cost to catch a Pokémon:
  
  **Eligible:** any of the 12 face-up cards **or** your reserved cards
  
  **Payment steps:**
  1. Apply **Pokéball bonuses** from owned Pokémon (permanent discounts)
  2. Pay remaining with **tokens** (returned to supply)
  3. **Master Balls** substitute for any color
  
  The caught Pokémon goes face-up in front of you — gain its points and bonus.
  
  💡 Even 0-point Level 1 Pokémon are valuable — they provide bonuses AND can evolve!

  Related topics: 
  - [Check Evolution](#flow-evolve-check)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-catch-special -->
  **Catch Rare / Legendary Pokémon**

  ### Rare Pokémon
  - Pay Pokéball cost like normal cards
  - Provides **2 permanent bonuses** (instead of 1)
  - **Cannot be reserved** — catch directly only
  
  ### Legendary Pokémon
  - Pay Pokéball cost **+ 1 Master Ball**
  - Provides **2 permanent bonuses**
  - **Cannot be reserved**
  - Usually higher point values
  
  After catching, immediately reveal the next one from its deck.

  Related topics: 
  - [Check Evolution](#flow-evolve-check)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-evolve-check -->
  **Evolution Phase**

  After your action (and checking token limit), you may evolve **1** of your caught Pokémon.
  
  **Evolution is optional** — you can skip even if conditions are met.
  
  Do you have a Pokémon that can evolve?

  ![End of turn: evolve with bonuses only; old Pokémon goes under the Trainer tile](/images/rules/splendor-pokemon/evolution.svg)

  Related topics:

- <!-- rule-item: flow-evolve -->
  **How to Evolve**

  ### Evolution Conditions
  1. The evolved form must be available — in the **face-up display** or your **reserved cards**
  2. You have enough **Pokéball bonuses** to pay the evolution cost
  
  ⚠️ **Key: Evolution can ONLY be paid with permanent bonuses, NOT tokens!**
  
  ### Evolution Process
  1. Take the evolved Pokémon card and place it in front of you (new points + bonus)
  2. Place the pre-evolution card under your **Trainer tile** (removed from play — no longer provides points or bonus)
  3. If the evolved form came from the display, replace it
  
  ### Rules
  - Level 1 → Level 2, Level 2 → Level 3
  - Maximum **1 evolution** per turn
  - Bonuses are NOT consumed (they remain permanent)
  
  💡 **Strategy:** Evolution is the most efficient scoring path — catch cheap Level 1s, then evolve into high-scoring forms

  Related topics: 
  - [Check Game End](#flow-endcheck)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-endcheck -->
  **Game End Check**

  Does any player have **18 or more points**?

  Related topics: 
  - [Yes → Final Round](#flow-final)
  - [No → Next Player](#flow-turn)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-final -->
  **Final Round & Scoring**

  Complete the current round (all players get equal turns).
  
  **Scoring:** Sum of all Pokémon card points in front of you (cards under Trainer tile don't count)
  
  **Winner:** Highest points.
  
  **Tiebreakers (in order):**
  1. Player with more Pokémon cards wins (including those under Trainer tile)
  2. Player who evolved more times wins
