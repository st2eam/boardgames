# Startups Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

You invest in six companies. At game end, the sole biggest holder in each company collects Capital from everybody else who owns that company.

The **Market** is the row of public cards everyone can take. Your **portfolio** is the cards already played in front of you; those cards decide who is the biggest holder.

The game supports 3–7 players and usually takes about 20 minutes.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Startups, you are an investor backing six risky young companies. Your goal is to become a company’s sole biggest holder when it matters, then finish with the most money.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- 45 Company cards across six companies
- 70 Capital chips (1-value side and 3-value side)
- 6 Anti-Monopoly chips
- 12 point chips
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

1. Shuffle the 45 Company cards and remove **5 cards** face down without looking at them.
2. Give every player **3 Company cards** as their hidden hand and **10 Capital chips**, with the 1-value side face up.
3. Place the remaining deck face down in the center. Leave room beside it for the **Market**: face-up Company cards will accumulate there.
4. Put the Anti-Monopoly chips nearby. Choose a starting player.

<!-- rule-section: every-turn-draw-1-play-1 -->
## Every Turn: Draw 1 → Play 1

![Drawing from the deck costs one Capital chip on every Market card; taking a Market card collects all Capital chips on it](/images/rules/startups/market-choice.svg)

Do both steps, in this order.
<!-- rule-section: 1-draw-or-take-1-company-card -->
### 1. Draw or take 1 Company card

Choose one source:

| Source | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Draw from deck** | Draw the top card into your hand. Then put one 1-value Capital chip on **every** card in the Market. If you cannot cover every Market card, you cannot draw this way. |
| **Take from Market** | Take one face-up Market card into your hand, plus every Capital chip sitting on that card. |
<!-- rule-section: 2-play-1-company-card-from-your-hand -->
### 2. Play 1 Company card from your hand

Choose exactly one card in your hand and place it:

- face up into your **portfolio** in front of you; or
- face up into the **Market** beside the deck.

If you took a card from the Market in Step 1, you may **not** put a card of that same company back into the Market this turn.
<!-- rule-section: who-is-the-biggest-holder-anti-monopoly-chips -->
## Who Is the Biggest Holder? Anti-Monopoly Chips

![The sole largest shareholder holds a company’s Anti-Monopoly chip, which protects that company’s Market shares from two costs](/images/rules/startups/anti-monopoly.svg)

For each company, whoever has the most cards in their portfolio takes its **Anti-Monopoly chip**. If first place is tied, the current holder keeps it until someone truly has more cards.

Holding a company’s chip changes two things:

- you cannot take that company’s cards from the Market; and
- when drawing from the deck, you do **not** place a Capital chip on that company’s Market cards.

Whenever someone plays a card into a portfolio, check the chip again.
<!-- rule-section: last-draw-reveal-hands-then-pay-out -->
## Last Draw: Reveal Hands, Then Pay Out

![After the final deck card is drawn, add hands to portfolios; each minority share pays three Capital to the sole majority shareholder](/images/rules/startups/final-payout.svg)

When someone draws the last deck card, finish that player’s “play 1” step, then end the game. Everyone adds their remaining hand to their portfolio.

Resolve each company separately:

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| One player has the most shares | Every other player who has shares of that company gives the majority shareholder **one 3-value Capital chip per share** they own. |
| Most shares are tied | Nobody receives a payout for that company. |
| A player cannot pay | Use the plus/minus point chips as needed. Do not use 3-value chips just received in this same payout to pay another debt. |

Add your Capital and point chips. The richest player wins. Break a tie by most 3-value chips, then by the player who played most recently.
<!-- rule-section: optional-four-round-game -->
## Optional Four-Round Game

For a longer game, award round point chips after each game: first place **+2**, second place **+1**, last place **−1**. Return the removed five cards, set up again, and let the last-place player start the next round. After four rounds, the highest point total wins; break ties by more +2 chips, then +1 chips, then the final-round winner.

  **Draw Only If You Can Fund the Market**

  Draw the top deck card, then put one 1-value Capital chip on every Market card. If you lack enough chips for all those cards, choose the Market instead.

  Related topics: 
  - [Place a hand card](#flow-place-card)
  - [Check Anti-Monopoly protection](#flow-anti-monopoly)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-take-market -->
  **Take One Market Share**

  Take one face-up Market card and all Capital chips on it. This turn, you cannot put a card of that same company into the Market; place it in your portfolio or choose another company for the Market.

  Related topics: 
  - [Place a hand card](#flow-place-card)
  - [Who owns the chip?](#flow-anti-monopoly)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-place-card -->
  **Invest or Feed the Market**

  Put exactly one hand card face up into your portfolio or the Market. A portfolio card can change the majority shareholder, so immediately update that company’s Anti-Monopoly chip.

  Related topics: 
  - [Update Anti-Monopoly chips](#flow-anti-monopoly)
  - [Next player](#flow-turn)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-anti-monopoly -->
  **Protect the Sole Leader**

  The sole largest portfolio holder takes that company’s Anti-Monopoly chip. A tie stays with the earlier holder. The holder cannot take its Market cards and skips adding Capital to them after a deck draw.

  ![The sole largest shareholder holds a company’s Anti-Monopoly chip, which protects that company’s Market shares from two costs](/images/rules/startups/anti-monopoly.svg)

  Related topics: 
  - [Continue the turn](#flow-turn)
  - [Is the deck empty?](#flow-game-end)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-game-end -->
  **Final Draw, Then Dividends**

  After the final deck card is drawn, finish that turn. Add all hands to portfolios. For each company, every minority holder pays one 3-value Capital chip per share to the sole majority holder; a tie pays nobody.

  ![After the final deck card is drawn, add hands to portfolios; each minority share pays three Capital to the sole majority shareholder](/images/rules/startups/final-payout.svg)

  Related topics: 
  - [Compare wealth](#flow-winner)
  - [Review chip protection](#flow-anti-monopoly)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-winner -->
  **The Richest Investor Wins**

  Total Capital and point chips. Highest value wins; break ties by most 3-value chips, then the player who played most recently. For the optional campaign, score +2/+1/−1 for first/second/last over four rounds.
