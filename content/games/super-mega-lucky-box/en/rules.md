# Super Mega Lucky Box Rules

<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

In **Super Mega Lucky Box**, everyone plays at once, crossing numbers off Lucky Box cards as number cards are revealed. Complete rows and columns to trigger bonuses, then score the most points over four rounds.

<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

Choose number grids that work well together, use Lightning tokens to reach useful numbers, and chain row and column bonuses. After four rounds, the player with the highest total score wins.

![A Lucky Box card has a 3 by 3 number grid and a bonus at the end of each row and column](/images/rules/super-mega-lucky-box/card-anatomy.svg)

<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- 60 Lucky Box cards, each with a 3×3 number grid and row and column bonuses
- 18 Number cards: two copies of each number from 1 to 9
- 30 Lightning tokens
- 24 Moon tokens
- 6 Super Mega Scorecards
- 6 dry-erase markers

<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

Prepare the shared supplies, then give each player a scorecard, marker, and starting cards.

<!-- rule-ui: sidebar default=shared-supplies -->
1. <!-- rule-item: shared-supplies -->
   **Set out the cards and tokens**

   Shuffle the Lucky Box cards and place them face down as a draw pile. Put the Lightning and Moon tokens beside the pile.

2. <!-- rule-item: number-deck -->
   **Prepare the first number deck**

   Shuffle all 18 Number cards. Deal 9 face down to make this round's deck. Set the other 9 aside without looking at them.

3. <!-- rule-item: player-equipment -->
   **Give each player equipment**

   Each player takes a Super Mega Scorecard, a dry-erase marker, and 4 Lightning tokens.

4. <!-- rule-item: starting-cards -->
   **Choose starting cards**

   Each player draws 5 Lucky Box cards, keeps 3 face up in a row, and discards the other 2 face up beside the Lucky Box draw pile. A useful starting set includes every number from 1 to 9 across its grids.

<!-- rule-section: how-to-play -->
## How to Play

Play four rounds of nine number cards each. Each round is simultaneous: one player reveals and announces a Number card, and everyone completes these steps before the next card is revealed.

<!-- rule-ui: sidebar default=reveal-number -->
1. <!-- rule-item: reveal-number -->
   **Reveal and announce a Number card**

   Turn over the top card and place it face up beside the cards already revealed this round.

2. <!-- rule-item: mark-one-square -->
   **Cross off one matching square**

   On one Lucky Box card, cross off exactly one open large square matching the revealed number. If you have no open matching square, you do nothing. You may spend Lightning tokens before crossing off the square to change the number for yourself.

3. <!-- rule-item: resolve-bonuses -->
   **Resolve every newly completed row or column**

   Circle each bonus icon whose row or column is now complete and take its effect immediately. Bonus effects can cross off more squares and trigger more bonuses. Resolve all bonuses before revealing the next Number card.

<!-- rule-section: bonuses -->
## Row and Column Bonuses

When a row or column becomes complete, circle its icon so you do not claim it again. A single square can complete both a row and a column; circle and resolve both icons. If you unlock several bonuses, resolve them in any order.

<!-- rule-ui: sidebar default=bonus-number -->
- <!-- rule-item: bonus-number -->
  **Number**

  Immediately cross off one open square showing that number on any Lucky Box card. This extra square can trigger more bonuses. You cannot save this effect or spend Lightning tokens to change its number.

- <!-- rule-item: bonus-question -->
  **Question mark**

  Immediately cross off any one open square on any Lucky Box card, then check for more bonuses.

- <!-- rule-item: bonus-lightning -->
  **Lightning**

  Take the number of Lightning tokens shown by the icon, usually 1 or 2. There is no holding limit. If the supply runs out, use another marker for each token owed.

- <!-- rule-item: bonus-star -->
  **Star**

  Circle the next open star on your scorecard in the current round. At round end, your first, second, and third stars that round are worth 1, 4, and 9 points respectively. Ignore any further stars that round.

- <!-- rule-item: bonus-moon -->
  **Moon**

  Take 1 Moon token. Moon tokens are scored at the end of the game.

![A completed row grants a number bonus, which can complete another line and trigger a second bonus](/images/rules/super-mega-lucky-box/bonus-chain.svg)

<!-- rule-section: lightning-tokens -->
## Using Lightning Tokens

Spend one or more Lightning tokens when marking the square for a revealed Number card. Each token moves that number up or down by 1; choose the direction for each step. Numbers loop from 9 to 1 and from 1 to 9. For example, a revealed 9 can become a 1 for 1 token, or a 2 for 2 tokens. This changes only your number for this reveal, not anyone else's. Lightning cannot change a Number bonus.

![Spend one Lightning token per step; numbers wrap from 9 to 1 and from 1 to 9](/images/rules/super-mega-lucky-box/lightning-loop.svg)

<!-- rule-section: round-end -->
## End of a Round

After the ninth Number card is resolved:

1. Score each Lucky Box card whose nine large squares are all crossed off. Each completed card is worth 15 points in round 1, 12 in round 2, 10 in round 3, and 8 in round 4. Record the total for that round on your scorecard.
2. Erase each completed card and place it face up in the discard pile. Leave incomplete cards in front of you.
3. Draw 3 Lucky Box cards, keep 1 face up, and discard the other 2. There is no limit to the number of cards you may have in front of you.
4. For the next round, shuffle all 18 Number cards and deal 9 face down. If the Lucky Box draw pile runs out, shuffle its discard pile to make a new one.

<!-- rule-section: game-end -->
## Game End and Scoring

The game ends after round 4. Add the points already recorded for completed cards and stars, then score your remaining incomplete cards and Moon tokens:

- **Incomplete cards:** score 1 point for every 2 crossed-off large squares across all incomplete cards; round down.
- **Moon tokens:** in a game with 3–6 players, the player or players with the most Moon tokens gain 6 points. The player or players with the fewest, including 0, lose 6 points. Tied players each gain or lose the full amount.
- **Two players:** each player tied for the most Moon tokens gains 6 points; no one loses points.

The highest total wins. If players tie, the tied player with more Moon tokens wins.

<!-- rule-section: solo-mode -->
## Solo Mode

Play the four rounds and score as usual. Instead of comparing Moon tokens with opponents, score your Moon tokens using this table:

| Moon tokens | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 or more |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Points | −6 | −2 | 0 | 1 | 3 | 6 | 10 |

<!-- rule-section: variants -->
## Variants

- **7–12 players:** combine two copies of the game. Combine their Lucky Box cards and Lightning and Moon tokens, but use only one set of 18 Number cards. To sort the Lucky Box cards back into their original boxes later, use the small 1–60 number printed on each card.
- **Solo score titles:** after adding your solo score, use the scorecard's rating scale: 44 or lower is Super Mega Unlucky; 45–49 is a good start; 50–54 is “on the trolley”; 55–59 is a great game; 60–64 is a super score; 65–69 is a mega game; 70 or more is Super Mega Lucky Champion.
