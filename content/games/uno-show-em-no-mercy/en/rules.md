# UNO Show 'Em No Mercy Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

UNO Show 'Em No Mercy is the most brutal version of UNO, published by Mattel. With 168 cards, massive draw penalties, stacking rules, hand-swapping mechanics, and the merciless elimination rule, there are two ways to win: empty your hand or knock everyone else out.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In UNO Show ’Em No Mercy, you are surviving the harshest version of UNO. Your goal is to empty your hand first, or become the last player who has not been eliminated.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the deck thoroughly.
2. Deal **7 cards** to each player.
3. Place remaining cards face down as the draw pile.
4. Flip the top card to start the discard pile.
<!-- rule-section: how-to-play -->
## How to Play

Match the top discard by **color**, **number**, or **symbol**. If you can't play, draw one card (play it immediately if it matches). When you're down to 1 card, yell **"UNO!"** — if caught, draw **4 penalty cards**.

<!-- rule-section: special-rules -->
## Special Rules

![Draw 2 and Draw 4 stack; next player draws the total](/images/rules/uno-show-em-no-mercy/stacking-mercy.svg)
<!-- rule-section: stacking-rule -->
### Stacking Rule
Draw cards **CAN be stacked**. If someone plays a Draw card on you (+2, +4, +6, +10), you may play a Draw card of **equal or higher value** to pass the penalty to the next player. The total accumulates until a player cannot stack, and they must draw **all** accumulated cards.
<!-- rule-section: 7-s-swap -->
### 7's Swap
When you play a **7** (any color), you **must** swap your entire hand with another player of your choice.
<!-- rule-section: 0-s-pass -->
### 0's Pass
When you play a **0** (any color), **all players** must pass their entire hand to the next player in the current direction of play.
<!-- rule-section: mercy-rule-elimination -->
### Mercy Rule (Elimination)
If a player ever has **25 or more cards** in their hand, they are **immediately eliminated** from the game. Their cards are set aside for reshuffling when the draw pile runs out.

## Action Cards

![Discard All, Skip Everyone, Wild Draw Six/Ten](/images/rules/uno-show-em-no-mercy/action-highlights.svg)
| Card | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Skip** | Next player loses their turn |
| **Reverse** | Reverses direction of play |
| **Draw Two (+2)** | Next player draws 2 and loses their turn. Can be stacked. |
| **Draw Four (+4)** | Next player draws 4 and loses their turn. Can be stacked. |
| **Discard All** | Discard ALL cards from your hand matching this card's color |
| **Skip Everyone** | All other players skip their turn. You play again immediately. |
| **Wild Color Roulette** | Choose a color. Next player draws cards from the pile until they reveal the chosen color. |
| **Wild Reverse Draw Four** | Reverses direction AND the new next player draws 4 |
| **Wild Draw Six** | Choose a color. Next player draws 6 and loses their turn. |
| **Wild Draw Ten** | Choose a color. Next player draws 10 and loses their turn. |

- <!-- rule-item: flow-stacking -->
  **Stacking Rule**

  Draw cards **CAN be stacked**. When hit with a Draw card (+2, +4, +6, +10), you may play a Draw card of **equal or higher value** to pass the penalty to the next player.
  
  **Example:** +2 → +4 → +6 → player can't stack → draws **12 total**.
  
  The total accumulates until someone cannot (or chooses not to) stack — that player draws **all** accumulated cards and loses their turn.
  
  Stackable cards: Draw Two, Draw Four, Wild Draw Six, Wild Draw Ten.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-seven-swap -->
  **Rule of 7's**

  When you play a **7** (any color), you **must** swap your **entire hand** with another player of your choice.
  
  - You choose which player to swap with
  - Both players exchange all cards — no partial swaps
  - Happens immediately when the 7 is played
  
  **Strategy tip:** Swap with the player who has the fewest cards when you're holding a large hand!

- <!-- rule-item: flow-zero-pass -->
  **Rule of 0's**

  When you play a **0** (any color), **all players** must pass their **entire hand** to the next player in the current direction of play.
  
  - Every player simultaneously gives their full hand to the next player
  - Direction follows the current play direction (clockwise or counter-clockwise)
  - Can dramatically shift who is winning or losing
  
  **Chaos alert:** A well-timed 0 can dump a huge hand onto your opponent — or onto you!

- <!-- rule-item: flow-mercy-rule -->
  **Mercy Rule**

  If a player ever has **25 or more cards** in their hand, they are **immediately eliminated** from the game.
  
  - Eliminated players' cards are set aside
  - When the draw pile runs out, eliminated players' cards are reshuffled back in
  - The game continues with remaining players
  
  This is the key to the **last player standing** victory condition. Stack chains and Draw Ten cards are the fastest path to elimination.
