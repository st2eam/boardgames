# Wingspan: Asia Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

Wingspan Asia is the third expansion for Wingspan, adding 90 new Asian bird cards, a standalone Duet mode for 1–2 players, and Flock mode for 6–7 players. It can be played as a card expansion mixed with the base game, or as a standalone game using the Duet mode. For Flock mode (6-7 players), the base game is required for additional player components.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Wingspan Asia, you are a bird enthusiast using Asia’s birds and modes. Your goal is to build the strongest preserve and meet the scoring objective of the mode you chose.

<!-- rule-section: duet-mode-1-2-players -->
### Duet Mode (1–2 Players)

Duet mode uses a dedicated **Duet Map** board instead of standard goal tiles. It can be played standalone or integrated with any card mix.

**Setup:**
- Give each player 15 duet tokens in their color.
- Place the Duet Map in the center of the table.
- Use the 6 duet-specific goal tiles.

**Duet Token Placement:**
Whenever you play a bird card, you place a duet token on the Duet Map in a space matching:
1. The bird's **habitat** type (Forest, Grassland, or Wetlands), AND
2. One of the bird's **traits** — either its nest type, food cost (number of food icons), or wingspan length.

**Scoring:**
- Each space on the duet map can hold at most **1 token per player**.
- **Connected groups** of your duet tokens score points at the end of the game. The larger the contiguous group, the more points.
- The **reset box** on the map can hold an unlimited number of tokens from either player.
- Duet map bonuses: When placing a token on a bonus space, gain the shown bonus (food die or card draw).
<!-- rule-section: flock-mode-6-7-players -->
### Flock Mode (6–7 Players)

Flock mode allows up to 7 players by splitting into two groups:

- Players are divided into **two groups**, each sharing a birdfeeder and bird card deck.
- **Two players take turns simultaneously** (one from each group) to speed up gameplay.
- A **turn-order dial** tracks which players in each group are active.
- Uses a dedicated **Flock round-end goal board**.
- Requires the base game for additional player components (mats, cubes, eggs).
<!-- rule-section: notable-rules -->
## Notable Rules
<!-- rule-ui: sidebar -->
- <!-- rule-item: notable-rules-item-1 -->
  **Great Cormorant**: Skip the first clause (moving a fish) for its first activation — just roll 2 dice. Once a fish is cached, the full ability activates on subsequent turns.
- <!-- rule-item: notable-rules-item-2 -->
  **Nectar caching**: Nectar can be cached as [wild] food. Cached nectar stays on the bird and is not placed in "spent nectar" — it's not discarded at round's end.
- <!-- rule-item: notable-rules-item-3 -->
  **Anatomist bonus card**: If using both the original and Asia versions, use the Asia version's updated point values.
- <!-- rule-item: notable-rules-item-4 -->
  **Mixing duet and standard goals**: Unofficially, you can mix Duet and standard end-round goals, but this de-emphasizes duet map tokens.

  **Duet Mode (1–2 Players)**

  ### Setup
  - Give each player **15 duet tokens** in their color.
  - Place the **Duet Map** in the center.
  - Use the **6 duet-specific goal tiles** (instead of standard goals).

  When you **play a bird card**, place 1 duet token on the duet map matching:
  1. The bird's **habitat** (Forest/Grassland/Wetlands), AND

  - Each space: max **1 token per player**.
  - **Reset box** (top-left): unlimited tokens from either player.

  ### Scoring
  At game end, your tokens form **connected groups** (orthogonally adjacent). Larger groups = more points. The duet map has its own scoring track on the goal board.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-duet-scoring -->
  **Duet Map Scoring**

  ### Connected Groups
  - Tokens of your color that are **orthogonally adjacent** (sharing an edge) form a group.
  - Diagonals do NOT connect.
  - The **reset box** does NOT connect to any other space.
  
  ### Bonus Spaces
  - **Food die icon**: When placing a token here, take 1 die from the birdfeeder (gain matching food). Remove the die; re-roll only if birdfeeder is empty.
  - **Card icon**: When placing a token here, draw 1 card from the tray or deck.
  - These bonuses are one-time — only when you place the token.
  
  ### End-of-Round Goals (Duet)
  The 6 duet goal tiles score differently from standard goals — they measure things like:
  - Most tokens in a specific habitat region
  - Most tokens adjacent to bonus spaces
  - Largest connected group so far
  
  For full Duet goal details, refer to the Duet mode rulebook included in the Asia expansion.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-flock-mode -->
  **Flock Mode (6–7 Players)**

  Flock mode allows up to 7 players by splitting the table into two groups.
  
  ### Setup
  - Divide players into **two groups** (e.g., Group A and Group B).
  - Each group shares: 1 birdfeeder, 1 bird card deck, 1 bird tray.
  - Each player still has their own mat, cubes, and food/egg supply.
  - Use the **Flock round-end goal board** instead of the standard goal board.
  
  ### Gameplay
  - **Two players take turns simultaneously** — one from each group.
  - Use the **turn-order dial** to track which player is active in each group.
  - When both active players finish, advance the dial to the next pair.
  - Players in the same group still take turns in order.
  
  ### Rules
  - You can only interact with your **own group's** birdfeeder and card tray.
  - Cross-group interaction is minimal — you're essentially playing two parallel games.
  - End-of-round goals compare all players across both groups.
  
  Requires the **base game** for additional player components.
