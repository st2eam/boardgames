# Wingspan: Oceania Expansion Rules
<!-- rule-section: overview -->
## Overview

The Oceania Expansion is the second expansion for Wingspan, adding 95 new bird cards featuring birds from Australia and New Zealand, a new nectar food type, redesigned player mats, and new yellow "end of game" bird powers. This expansion requires the base game to play.
<!-- rule-section: game-objective -->
## Game Objective

In Wingspan Oceania, you are a bird enthusiast working with new birds and nectar. Your goal is to use those additions to build the highest-scoring preserve.
<!-- rule-section: components -->
## Components

- 95 bird cards (Australia/New Zealand birds)
- 5 new player mats (alternate design)
- 5 new food dice (including nectar faces)
- 69 nectar tokens
- 15 yellow egg miniatures
- 5 bonus cards
- 4 new goal tiles
- New scorepad and reference tile
- Automa components
<!-- rule-section: setup -->
## Setup

Shuffle the expansion bird cards, bonus cards, and goal tiles into the corresponding base game decks. Replace the base game food dice with the new Oceania dice. You may choose to use either the original player mats or the new Oceania mats (nectar requires the Oceania mats and dice).
<!-- rule-ui: tabs -->
<!-- rule-section: new-mechanics -->
## New Mechanics

![Nectar is wild food; spent nectar scores by habitat majority](/images/rules/wingspan-oceania/nectar.svg)
<!-- rule-section: nectar -->
### Nectar

Nectar is the biggest new mechanic:

- **New food type**: Nectar can substitute for any other food type when playing a bird. When spent, place the nectar token on a **"spent nectar" space** on your player mat (in the habitat where the bird was played).
- **Scoring nectar**: At the end of the game, count spent nectar in each habitat separately. The player with the **most nectar in each habitat** earns 5 bonus points; second most earns 3; third earns 1. Unspent nectar is worth nothing.
- **Nectar dice**: The new dice include nectar faces alongside the standard food types. Nectar is wild — it can be used as any food.
<!-- rule-section: new-player-mats -->
### New Player Mats

The Oceania player mats replace the original mats and introduce:

- **Reset birdfeeder / Reset card tray**: As an action (instead of a regular turn action), you can reset the birdfeeder dice or the face-up bird card tray. This costs 1 action cube.
- **Faster early-game resources**: The mats let you gain more food and cards earlier, but laying eggs is reduced compared to the original mats.
- **No-food-cost columns**: The first column in each habitat on the new mats has no food cost for playing a bird, making early plays easier.
- You can **choose** to play with the original mats or Oceania mats, but nectar rules require the Oceania mats and dice.
<!-- rule-section: yellow-end-of-game-powers -->
### Yellow "End of Game" Powers

New yellow bird powers activate **after the game ends** (after final scoring), allowing abilities like:
- Tucking additional cards
- Caching extra food
- Laying extra eggs for bonus scoring

These powers resolve before the final score tally.
<!-- rule-section: all-player-interaction-powers -->
### All-Player Interaction Powers

Some new birds have powers that affect all players (e.g., "all players gain 1 food from the supply"), adding more interaction to the game.
<!-- rule-section: new-end-of-round-goals -->
## New End-of-Round Goals

The expansion adds new goal tiles including:
- Birds with body parts in their names
- Birds with specific beak directions
- Rounds with no end goal (a new possibility)
<!-- rule-section: key-differences-from-base-game -->
## Key Differences from Base Game

![Oceania mats add reset actions and faster early food; nectar needs the new kit](/images/rules/wingspan-oceania/new-mats.svg)

| Aspect | Base Game | Oceania |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Food dice | 5 standard dice | 5 dice with nectar faces |
| Player mats | Standard layout | Redesigned with reset actions |
| Early game | Slower buildup | Faster food and card gain |
| Egg strategy | Strong late-game | Reduced egg output |
| Food type | 5 types | 6 types (with nectar) |

- <!-- rule-item: flow-nectar -->
  **Nectar System**

  Nectar is the biggest new mechanic in Oceania.
  
  ### As a Food Type
  - Nectar is **wild** — it can substitute for any other food type when paying bird costs.
  - When you spend nectar to play a bird, place it in the **"spent nectar"** space on your player mat in the habitat where the bird was played.
  - Nectar dice faces appear on the new food dice — you can gain nectar just like any other food.
  
  ### Key Rules
  - **Unspent nectar = 0 points**. Only nectar spent on playing birds counts for scoring.
  - Nectar can be cached on bird cards (like other food), but cached nectar stays on the bird and is **not** placed in spent nectar.
  - Nectar tokens are limited (69 in supply). If nectar runs out, you cannot gain more.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-nectar-scoring -->
  **Nectar Scoring**

  At the end of the game, score nectar separately for **each habitat** (Forest, Grassland, Wetlands):
  
  | Rank | 2 players | 3–4 players | 5 players |
  |------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
  | 1st (most) | 6 pts | 5 pts | 5 pts |
  | 2nd | 3 pts | 3 pts | 4 pts |
  | 3rd | — | 1 pt | 3 pts |
  | 4th | — | — | 2 pts |
  | 5th | — | — | 1 pt |
  
  **Important**:
  - Ties: add points for tied positions, divide equally (rounded down).
  - If no one has spent nectar in a habitat, no points are awarded for that habitat.
  - Players who spent **0 nectar** in a habitat score 0 for that habitat.
  - Nectar in each habitat is scored independently — a player could score in all three.

- <!-- rule-item: flow-new-mats -->
  **New Player Mats**

  The Oceania player mats replace the original mats with redesigned layouts:
  
  ### Key Changes
  | Feature | Original Mat | Oceania Mat |
  |---------|-------------|-------------|
  | Column 1 food cost | Cost printed | **No cost** — free to play |
  | Early food gain | Slower | **Faster** — more dice icons early |
  | Early card gain | Slower | **Faster** — more card icons early |
  | Egg gain | Higher | **Reduced** — less egg spamming |
  | Reset actions | None | Reset birdfeeder or card tray (costs 1 cube) |
  
  ### Reset Actions
  - **Reset Birdfeeder**: Instead of a normal turn, spend 1 action cube to re-roll all 5 dice.
  - **Reset Card Tray**: Instead of a normal turn, spend 1 action cube to discard face-up cards and draw 3 new ones.
  - Both reset actions are on the player mat and cost a full action.
  
  ### Compatibility
  You can choose to play with original mats or Oceania mats. Nectar rules require the Oceania mats and dice.

  ![Oceania mats add reset actions and faster early food; nectar needs the new kit](/images/rules/wingspan-oceania/new-mats.svg)

- <!-- rule-item: flow-yellow-powers -->
  **Yellow "End of Game" Powers**

  Yellow bird powers are a new type that activates **after the game ends**:
  
  **Timing**:
  1. Game ends after Round 4.
  2. Activate all yellow "End of Game" powers.
  3. Then tally final scores.
  
  **Examples**:
  - "Tuck up to 3 cards from your hand behind this bird"
  - "Cache 1 food from your supply on this bird"
  - "Lay 1 egg on each bird in this habitat"
  - "Discard any number of [worms] from your supply; score 1 point each"
  
  **Key Points**:
  - Yellow powers can give you last-minute points!
  - They resolve BEFORE nectar scoring and final tally.
  - Multiple yellow birds can activate — resolve in any order.
  - These powers are in addition to (not instead of) normal end-of-round and end-of-game scoring.
